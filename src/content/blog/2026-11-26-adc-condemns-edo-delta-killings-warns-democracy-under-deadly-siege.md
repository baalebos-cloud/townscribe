**Abuja | November 26, 2026 —** The African Democratic Congress Presidential Campaign Council, ADC-PCC, has condemned in the strongest terms the deadly attacks on leaders and members of the Nigeria Democratic Congress, NDC, in Edo and Delta States, warning the violence poses a grave threat to the survival of multiparty democracy in Nigeria.

In a statement issued on Thursday, November 26, 2026, by its Director of Media and Publicity, Kola Ologbondiyan, the Council described the killings—in which more than four persons reportedly lost their lives—as barbaric, senseless, and unacceptable in a democratic society.

*“We strongly condemn these senseless killings and extend our deepest condolences to the leadership and members of the NDC, as well as the families of the victims,”* the statement said.

#### Not Isolated, But A Pattern

The ADC-PCC stressed the Edo and Delta attacks were not isolated, recalling previous violence and intimidation against its own members in Kaduna, Kebbi, Sokoto, and other parts of the country.

*“These attacks did not begin today. Our party, the ADC, has also experienced acts of violence and intimidation against its members in different parts of the country.* 

*What is happening in Edo and Delta is therefore not an isolated occurrence but part of a troubling pattern that threatens the survival of multiparty democracy,”* it said.

*“An attack on one opposition party is an attack on all opposition parties. Political disagreements must never become a justification for violence, bloodshed or the suppression of citizens' fundamental rights,”* the Council added.

The Council defended the popular “Ba Mayi” chorus—a Hausa expression of rejection—as legitimate dissent, not provocation. “*No Nigerian should be killed, attacked, or intimidated for saying ‘No’ to a government or expressing dissatisfaction with its policies. Peaceful dissent is a constitutional right, not a crime.”*

#### Arrest Sponsors, End Impunity

The ADC-PCC called on the Nigeria Police Force and security agencies to immediately investigate, arrest and prosecute gunmen and sponsors or facilitators. “The era of dismissing politically motivated violence as ordinary clashes must end.”

It reminded the Federal Government of its constitutional duty to protect all Nigerians irrespective of affiliation, urging the All Progressives Congress, APC, and President Bola Ahmed Tinubu to demonstrate tolerance and peaceful engagement. It also appealed to democratic institutions, civil society and the international community to speak out.

*“Violence cannot and will not rescue a government that has lost the confidence of the people. Nigerians deserve freedom to make choices without fear, and no amount of intimidation or bloodshed can substitute for the peopl*e’s *mandate,*” it said, insisting the 2027 general election must be decided by free will, not threats.
