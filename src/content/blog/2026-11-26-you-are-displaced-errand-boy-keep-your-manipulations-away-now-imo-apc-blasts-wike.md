**Owerri / Abuja | November 26, 2026—The** All Progressives Congress (APC), in Imo State, has launched a scathing attack on the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory, FCT, Nyesom Wike, describing him as a “politically displaced errand boy” after his offer to help President Bola Ahmed Tinubu secure victory in the state in the 2027 elections.

Wike had on Wednesday asked Tinubu and the leadership of the APC presidential campaign to assign him additional states to mobilize support for the president’s re-election bid.

Speaking at a town hall meeting of the Rainbow Coalition in Gokana Local Government Area of Rivers State, the minister boasted he had completed mobilization work assigned to him in Rivers and the FCT.

***“I was assigned to coordinate Rivers and the FCT. Frankly speaking, I am done with these states. All adequate political machinery has been set in motion to deliver Rivers State and the FCT for the president. So, I have no much work to do again,***” Wike had said, requesting Imo and Kwara where he claimed APC was facing challenges.

#### Imo APC: No Crisis Here

But in a blistering statement issued on Thursday, Onwuasoanya Jones, Imo APC publicity secretary, dismissed Wike’s offer, insisting the party has no internal crisis requiring his intervention.

***“Let us make it clear from the onset that there is no problem in Imo state APC, and our campaigns for the party and Mr President have been going on smoothly, and the promise of victory is assured,***” Jones said.

He branded Wike a *“notorious political refugee”* and *“PDP mercenary and errand boy,*” accusing him of trying to portray Tinubu as unpopular and reliant on outsiders to win.

“***We understand that his intention is to cast the president as unpopular and reliant on a PDP mercenary and errand boy to win his elections,”*** he said.

Jones slammed Wike’s claim of already securing Rivers and FCT over 50 days before the January 16, 2027 presidential poll as “*unwarranted disdain for democratic norms,”* saying it paints voters as irrelevant and associates APC with undemocratic practices.

“Nyesom Wike continues to ride on the popularity and acceptability of our party to cling to some form of political relevance,” he said, challenging Wike to campaign for his own struggling PDP, including in Rivers.

#### Bags Of Money, Godfatherism Allegation

In its strongest charge, Imo APC accused Wike of attempting to import *“godfatherism, suppression of the people’s will, and electoral manipulation,” al*leging he deployed emissaries with money to sow confusion.

*“We have made it clear to his numerous emissaries and agents who he had deployed to Imo State with bags of lurid lucre in an attempt to sow confusion within the ranks of our party, that Imo State is not for sale!”* Jones declared.

The clash exposes deep APC-PDP cross-party tensions as Tinubu’s re-election machinery takes shape.
