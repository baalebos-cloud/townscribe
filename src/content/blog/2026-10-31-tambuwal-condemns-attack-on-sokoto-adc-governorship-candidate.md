**Sokoto, Nigeria—October 31, 2026—**Senator Aminu Waziri Tambuwal has strongly condemned the reported attack on the African Democratic Congress (ADC) governorship candidate in Sokoto State, Hon. Manir Muhammad Dan’iya, and members of his convoy near Sokoto Airport on October 30, 2026.

In a statement released on Saturday, the former Sokoto governor and current senator representing Sokoto South described the incident as deeply disturbing.



**Details of the Alleged Attack**

According to the allegations, personnel of the Sokoto Marshal and Sokoto State Community Guard blocked Dan’iya’s convoy, fired gunshots, damaged his vehicle, and detained two Department of State Services (DSS) officers attached to his security detail.

Tambuwal called on the Nigeria Police Force, the DSS and other relevant security agencies to conduct a thorough, independent and impartial investigation and ensure that anyone found culpable is brought to justice.



**Call for Peace and Rule of Law**

*“Every Nigerian has the right to move freely, associate with a political party, and seek political office in accordance with the law. No citizen should face threats, violence, or harassment for exercising these rights,”* he stated.

He urged those with political differences to pursue peaceful and lawful means of expression, insisting that the people should speak through their votes rather than through arms.

Tambuwal stressed that security outfits have a duty to protect all citizens regardless of political affiliation and must never become instruments of political intimidation. 

He called on the Sokoto State Government to ensure that all security outfits under its authority operate lawfully and impartially.



**Campaign Flag-Off to Proceed**

The senator made it clear that no amount of intimidation or threats would deter the ADC from proceeding with its scheduled presidential campaign flag-off on November 4, 2026. 

He reaffirmed the party’s commitment to peaceful political engagement and the rule of law.

He urged ADC members and supporters to remain calm, exercise restraint, and stay resolute.



*“Sokoto belongs to all its people. Nigeria belongs to all of us. Our democracy must protect the right of every citizen to participate freely and without fear,*” Tambuwal concluded.



***This report is based on the official statement issued by Senator Aminu Waziri Tambuwal on October 31, 2026.***
