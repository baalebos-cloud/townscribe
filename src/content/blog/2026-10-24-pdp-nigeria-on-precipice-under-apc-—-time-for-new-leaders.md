**Abuja, Nigeria—October 24, 2026**—The Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) has declared that Nigeria has remained on the precipice for far too long under the All Progressives Congress (APC)-led administration, insisting that Nigerians are determined to elect new leaders capable of restoring the nation’s dignity and upholding the dignity of her people.

In a statement shared on its official X handle on Saturday, the party invited the public to read the latest edition of its publication, which expands on the party’s assessment of the current state of the nation and its vision for change.



**PDP’s Assessment of the Nation**

The party’s message reflects a consistent position that the country is facing deep challenges under the present government. 

It positions the upcoming 2027 general elections as a critical opportunity for Nigerians to choose leadership that prioritizes national restoration and the well-being of citizens.

This latest communication comes amid ongoing internal restructuring efforts within the PDP and its continued criticism of the APC administration’s policies and performance. 

The party has repeatedly argued that the opposition’s primary duty is to offer a credible alternative rather than align with the ruling party.



**Broader Political Context**

The PDP has in recent weeks rejected suggestions of supporting President Bola Tinubu’s re-election bid, describing such ideas as coming from *“pretenders”* rather than true party members. 

It has also called for greater transparency and accountability in major government projects while urging its members to remain steadfast.

By framing the current situation as one of national urgency, the party seeks to rally support around the need for change and to present itself as a vehicle for restoring dignity to both the country and its people.

The full edition of the PDP publication is available via the link shared on the party’s official platform.



***This report is based on the official statement released by the Peoples Democratic Party on October 24, 2026.***
