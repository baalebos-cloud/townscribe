**Ado-Ekiti, Nigeria—November 1, 2026**—African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has described the arrest of 11 suspects by the Ekiti State Police Command as a major admission that the violent incident involving him and his team was no ordinary altercation, while demanding the arrest of alleged masterminds and the public release of CCTV footage.

In a statement posted on Saturday, Sowore reacted to the police announcement that operatives of the Rapid Response Squad arrested the suspects at their hideouts in the Atikankan area of Ado-Ekiti around 10 p.m. on Friday, October 30, following a tip-off. 

The suspects were linked to the attack that occurred earlier that day at Tawakalitu Restaurant in the Dalimore area.



**Police Action and Recovered Items**

According to the police statement signed by Public Relations Officer SP Abutu Sunday, the 11 suspects are Joseph Tayo (32), Olatunji Abayomi (41), Isaac Amba (22), Kehinde Ajigbana (18), Tosin David (18), Solomon Iyanu (18), Ogunsola Ayomide (21), Ojo Ayodeji (22), Kayode Dada (32), Segun Olatunji (28) and Salami Emmanuel (53).

Items recovered included two walkie-talkies, substances suspected to be Indian hemp and Colorado, 13 mobile phones, eight international passports, seven ATM cards, two suspected criminal charms, and other materials. 

The command said investigations were ongoing to establish individual roles and identify any other persons involved. Commissioner of Police Falade Adegoroye Michael assured that those found culpable would be brought to justice. All victims, including Sowore, received medical attention and were discharged.



**Sowore’s Strong Reaction**

Sowore maintained that the incident was a coordinated, violent attack that could have cost lives and amounted to an assassination attempt by government-sponsored thugs. 

He questioned why the police initially described it as a mere “*altercation”* before announcing the arrests.

He specifically demanded the investigation and arrest of Sola Adenijo, popularly known as Solar, whom he identified as one of those involved in coordinating the attack, as well as the role of the Chief Security Officer to the governor, CSP Ayodele Akinlua. 

Sowore also called for probes into alleged links with the so-called City Boys group and officials of the Ekiti State Government.

*“Arresting 11 suspects is not enough if those who planned, financed, coordinated, and facilitated the attack remain untouched,”* he stated. 

He further demanded that the Commissioner of Police and the Department of State Services (DSS) immediately preserve and release the relevant CCTV footage so Nigerians can identify the attackers and those who directed or protected them.



**Calls for Independent Probe**

Sowore insisted on an independent, transparent investigation that follows the evidence wherever it leads, including to politically connected individuals. 

He rejected any cover-up that arrests only foot soldiers while masterminds walk free.

The Ekiti State Government has denied any involvement in the incident, describing allegations linking it to the attack as unfounded and reaffirming its commitment to the rule of law and the safety of all residents regardless of political affiliation.

The attack occurred while Sowore was in the state for campaign engagements and in connection with the Ikogosi Marathon.



***This report is based on Sowore’s statement and official police releases issued on October 31, 2026.***
