**Lagos, Nigeria – August 6, 2026** — The family of Nollywood actress **Temitope “Tope” Osoba** has broken its silence, firmly rejecting widespread claims that the 40-year-old star died from cancer.

In an official statement posted on the actress’s Instagram page on Thursday, the family described circulating reports about the circumstances of her death as *“false, speculative, and entirely unauthorized.”*

*“We are deeply saddened by the spread of false and unverified reports concerning the passing of our beloved,”* the statement read. “*At this difficult time, we wish to categorically state that the numerous claims circulating on social media and various blogs regarding the circumstances surrounding her death are false, speculative, and entirely unauthorized.”*

The family appealed to the public to stop sharing or promoting misinformation, saying it only deepened their pain. They promised that further details would be released on her official page at the appropriate time.

**A Sudden and Shocking Loss**

Tope Osoba died on Tuesday evening, August 4, 2026. According to reports, she had spoken with colleagues on the phone and posted the Yoruba phrase *“Ogo Tuntun"*—meaning “*New Glory”*—on her WhatsApp status at about 7:23 pm shortly before her passing.

News of her death spread rapidly the following day, with many initially attributing it to her earlier battle with breast cancer. 

The actress had publicly sought financial support for treatment nearly two years earlier, underwent surgery in late 2024, and announced in 2025 that she had overcome the disease and was returning to the screen.

She marked her 40th birthday in December 2025, describing the milestone as *“a victory, a blessing and a reminder that miracles happen.”* 

She had also returned to acting, promoting projects such as the film **Cost of a Lie.**

Close associates, including actor Alesh Sanni, also publicly stated that Osoba did not die of cancer and urged people to allow the family to grieve without speculation. 

Tributes poured in from across Nollywood, with colleagues expressing shock at the suddenness of her departure after what many believed was a hard-won recovery.

One colleague, Olamilekan Ayinla, shared that in their final conversation the actress had pleaded with him not to let her die—words that have since taken on a haunting significance.

While the family has not disclosed the actual cause of death, their statement underscores a clear request for privacy and accuracy at a time of profound loss. 

The entertainment community continues to mourn a performer who faced serious illness with public courage and later sought to inspire others through her recovery story.

Further official updates are expected on Tope Osoba’s verified social media channels as the family prepares to celebrate her life and legacy.
