**buja | November 26, 2026 | EFCC vs Emefiele Naira Redesign Trial—The** trial of former Central Bank of Nigeria, CBN, Governor, Godwin Emefiele, took a dramatic turn on Thursday, November 26, 2026, as a prosecution witness told the Federal Capital Territory, FCT, High Court, Maitama, Abuja, that the naira notes eventually produced under the controversial redesign policy were completely different from what the President approved.

The witness, Commissioner of Police Eloho Okphebolo, who testified as Prosecution Witness 11, PW11, made the explosive disclosure while being led in evidence by prosecution counsel, Rotimi Oyedepo, SAN, Director of Public Prosecutions of the Federation.

Emefiele is being prosecuted by the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission, EFCC, on a four-count charge bordering on alleged disobedience to lawful directives and unlawful acts that allegedly caused harm to members of the public in connection with the naira redesign policy.

#### No Board Approval, No Real Redesign—Only Colour Swap

Testifying before Justice Maryanne Anineh, Okphebolo, who said he has served in the Nigeria Police Force for 31 years, told the court he encountered Emefiele during an investigation of the CBN by a special investigative team appointed by the President and headed by Jim Obazee.

“I and some team of officers were seconded to a team appointed by the President to investigate the CBN. It was in the course of that assignment that I met the defendant,” he said.

He said the team found no evidence that the CBN Board ever met and approved the colour change of the redesigned notes.

“*We didn't see any approval that the board met and approved the colour swap,”* the witness declared.

According to him, Emefiele had written to the President seeking approval for the redesign, attaching samples of proposed designs which were approved. But what was eventually printed was different.

*“In the investigation, we saw that Emefiele requested the President to approve the redesign of currency note and he attached a sample of the design that he wanted, which the President approved, but what was eventually done was not a redesign as was approved by the President but colour swap,”* he said*.*

#### Mint Warned Emefiele, Witness Insists

When shown Emefiele’s letter to the President, the witness identified and authenticated it, insisting: “My evidence before this court is that what was approved by the President was not what is being done.”

He also cited Exhibit J1, a letter from the Chief Executive Officer of the Nigeria Security Printing and Minting Company, NSPMC, to the special investigator. 

After Emefiele forwarded the presidential approval to the NSPMC, the company warned against printing, citing inconsistencies in numbering, QR codes and watermarks after careful examination by its directors.

Pointing to currency sketches in court, Okphebolo said the proposed N1,000 note in Exhibit J2 contained a QR code, while the version Emefiele approved for production had none. 

The N500 note, Exhibit D2, and N200 note also had no QR codes, with no presidential approval for such production. He added the special team had not even been appointed when the notes were printed.

The case was adjourned for continuation of trial.
