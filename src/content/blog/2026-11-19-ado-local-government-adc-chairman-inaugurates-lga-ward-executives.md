**ADO-EKITI, EKITI STATE – November 19, 2026** — The African Democratic Congress (ADC) Caretaker Chairman for Ado Local Government, Prince Oba Adedimeji Ayodeji, on Wednesday inaugurated the Local Government and Ward Executives of the party in Ado Local Government Area.

The inauguration, held in Ado-Ekiti, forms part of the ongoing restructuring of the ADC in Ekiti State under the leadership of the State Caretaker Committee. 

It marks another important step in efforts to rebuild party structures from the grassroots and strengthen organisational presence ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Prince Oba Adedimeji Ayodeji, who was earlier appointed as the Ado Local Government Caretaker Chairman by the State Caretaker Committee with the approval of the national leadership of the party, used the occasion to charge the newly inaugurated executives to prioritise unity, discipline and effective mobilisation across the wards in Ado Local Government.

He reminded the officers that the task before them goes beyond titles, stressing the need for collective commitment to reconciliation, membership expansion and the overall growth of the party at the grassroots. 

The chairman urged the new executives to work closely with members in their respective wards, resolve lingering differences and ensure that the party remains accessible and responsive to the people.

According to him, Ado Local Government, being the political and commercial nerve centre of Ekiti State, holds strategic importance for the party. 

He therefore called on the inaugurated officers to demonstrate leadership by example and to focus on building a solid structure capable of supporting the party’s broader objectives in the state.

The inauguration comes against the background of the recent constitution of Local Government Caretaker Committees across the 16 local government areas of Ekiti State by the ADC State Caretaker Committee led by Dr. Bamidele Ekunola. 

The state leadership had emphasised reconciliation, structural development and grassroots mobilisation as key priorities in repositioning the party for future electoral contests.

Party stakeholders at the event noted that the formal inauguration of both the local government and ward executives would help deepen coordination between the local government leadership and the various wards, thereby enhancing mobilisation, membership registration and conflict resolution at the community level.

With the Ado Local Government structure now fully in place, attention is expected to shift to sustained engagement with members, expansion of the party’s base and the strengthening of its presence across the wards in the local government area.

The development is seen as part of the wider effort by the ADC in Ekiti State to move from internal reorganisation to functional grassroots operations in preparation for the political activities leading to 2027.
