**Lokoja/Abuja | November 25, 2026—In** a rare moment of cross-party commendation that has set Nigeria’s political space buzzing, former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar has publicly commended Kogi State Governor, Alhaji Ahmed Usman Ododo, for welcoming the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential campaign to the state.

Atiku, who is the presidential candidate of the ADC for the 2027 general election, said Ododo rose above partisanship by providing a peaceful environment, making the rally venue available, and ensuring adequate security for lives and property before, during, and after the opposition rally.

In a statement issued on Monday, November 24, 2026, by Phrank Shaibu, Director of Strategic Communication of the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, Atiku said the reception he received across Kogi East, Central, and West made the visit unforgettable and memorable.

***“Governor Ododo showed that political competition need not stand in the way of hospitality or public safety,”*** Atiku stated.

“***He accommodated our campaign, respected our right to meet the people of Kogi State and helped create an atmosphere in which everyone could participate peacefully. I thank him sincerely.”***

The former Vice-President, however, used the opportunity to take a direct swipe at other governors of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), whom he accused of deliberately frustrating and making opposition campaigns needlessly difficult in their states.

“***At a time when some governors of the ruling APC have made opposition campaigns needlessly difficult, Governor Ododo stands tall,”*** Atiku declared.

***“His conduct was accommodating, disciplined, and worthy of commendation. This is how a governor serves the whole state, including citizens who support a different party.”***

Atiku also expressed deep gratitude to Kogi residents, supporters, community leaders, and security personnel for their cooperation, orderliness, and warmth. 

He said they welcomed the ADC campaign with open hearts and conducted themselves with dignity.

“You welcomed us with open hearts and conducted yourselves with dignity before, during, and after the rally. Kogi State gave us a day we will remember with gratitude,” he added.

He reaffirmed his commitment to his Affordable Life agenda, promising that its benefits—on cost of living, jobs, food security, and inclusive governance—would reach every part of Kogi State if elected in 2027.

The development has sparked nationwide conversation on tolerance, level playing ground, and the conduct of state governors toward opposition parties as the 2027 presidential race gathers momentum.
