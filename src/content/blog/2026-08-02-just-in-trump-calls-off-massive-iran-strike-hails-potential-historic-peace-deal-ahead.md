**Washinghton,  August 3, 2026**- In a dramatic turn that could reshape the trajectory of the months-long conflict, U.S. President Donald Trump announced that he has called off a planned major military strike on Iran, describing the aborted operation as one that “would have been the biggest attack since WORLD WAR II” and “DISASTROUS FOR THEM.”

Speaking to reporters aboard Air Force One on Sunday, Trump revealed that new negotiations with Iran are scheduled to begin Monday afternoon. 

The decision to halt the strike came partly in response to urgent appeals from key U.S. allies in the Middle East, including Saudi Arabia, who pressed for diplomacy over further escalation.

The Department of War’s official Rapid Response account amplified the president’s remarks, sharing video footage of Trump delivering the comments in a formal setting aboard the presidential aircraft. 

The clip, labeled as new video from Air Force One and carried by Fox News, underscored the gravity of the moment under a ***“Historic Peace Deal”*** banner.



**A Conflict at a Crossroads**

The announcement comes amid a prolonged and intense confrontation between the United States and Iran that has already involved significant military exchanges, naval activity in the Strait of Hormuz, and repeated threats of broader strikes on Iranian infrastructure. 

Trump has previously warned of devastating consequences if Tehran failed to meet U.S. demands, including the full reopening of the critical waterway that carries a substantial share of global oil supplies.

By framing the cancelled attack in historic terms—comparing its potential scale to the largest operations of the Second World War—the president sought to signal both American military readiness and a preference for a negotiated outcome. 

*“We’re just going to see whether or not we can make a deal,”* Trump said, leaving the door open for continued pressure if talks falter.

Middle East allies reportedly played a pivotal role in the decision, urging Washington to give diplomacy another chance rather than risk a dramatic widening of the conflict. 

The emerging framework, according to Trump’s earlier statements, would prioritize the immediate and complete opening of the Strait of Hormuz and address concerns over Iran’s nuclear program.

 **Human Stakes and Global Implications**

For ordinary people on all sides, the stakes remain extraordinarily high. Seafarers, energy workers, and civilians across the region have lived under the shadow of disrupted shipping lanes, rising fuel prices, and the constant threat of further violence. 

A successful deal could ease those pressures; failure could quickly return the region to the brink.

Analysts note that previous ceasefires and memorandums of understanding in this conflict have proven fragile. Skepticism remains high in Tehran and among some U.S. observers who question whether lasting agreements can hold. 

Still, the pause in planned strikes offers a rare window for de-escalation after months of intense military and rhetorical confrontation.

As negotiators prepare to sit down on Monday, the world watches closely. The difference between a historic peace breakthrough and a return to large-scale warfare may now rest on the willingness of both sides to compromise. 

For now, the largest attack since World War II has been averted — at least temporarily — by a last-minute pivot toward talks.
