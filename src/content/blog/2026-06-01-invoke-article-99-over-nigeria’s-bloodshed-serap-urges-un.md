SERAP demands António Guterres trigger Article 99 immediately. Nigeria's escalating crisis threatens international peace, the group warns.

The organization cites relentless killings, mass abductions, and forced displacements nationwide. Benue, Borno, Plateau, Kaduna, Zamfara, and Oyo suffer gravely.

Years of violence have unleashed destruction, humanitarian suffering, and collective trauma. Communities now endure an unending cycle of terror and despair.

Article 99 empowers the Secretary-General to spotlight threats to global security. Preventive diplomacy and coordinated action remain urgently needed now.

Placing Nigeria's crisis on the Security Council agenda ensures international scrutiny. Attacks on civilians, kidnappings, and displacement demand global attention.

This is no mere domestic law-enforcement challenge, SERAP insists. Armed groups and weapons now cross borders, destabilizing the entire region.

Sustained international engagement will protect vulnerable communities effectively. The United Nations must reaffirm its commitment to global peace.

Neither the UN nor Nigeria's government has responded officially yet. The world watches as bloodshed continues without accountability or action.
