**Ibadan, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has lost two senior officials in his administration after the Commissioner for Budget and Economic Planning and the Special Adviser on the same portfolio tendered their resignations on Monday.

The officials are Barr. Ayobami Oluwaseun Ojo, Commissioner for Budget and Economic Planning, and Hon. Simeon Adegbola Oyeleke, Special Adviser on Budget and Economic Planning. Both resignations take immediate effect.



**Details of the Resignations**

Ojo submitted his resignation letter dated November 2, 2026, addressed to Governor Makinde through the Secretary to the State Government. Referencing his appointment letter of June 22, 2026, he formally stepped down without stating a specific reason.

*“I respectfully write to formally resign my appointment as Honourable Commissioner for Budget and Economic Planning, with effect from today, 2nd November, 2026,”* the letter read. Ojo expressed appreciation for the opportunity to serve, describing it as a great honor.

Oyeleke, a former two-term member of the Oyo State House of Assembly and local government chairman, also resigned the same day. 

In his letter, he cited an unconducive working environment that prevented him from effectively pursuing the aspirations of his people within the administration.

He thanked the governor for the confidence reposed in him and the chance to contribute to the state’s developmental programmes, while affirming his continued commitment to Oyo’s progress.



**Broader Context**

The dual exits form part of a recent wave of resignations from Makinde’s government ahead of the 2027 general elections. 

Makinde, who is also the presidential candidate of the Allied Peoples Movement (APM), has seen several high-profile departures in recent weeks, including his former Chief of Staff.

Shortly after the two budget officials resigned, a member of the Oyo State Independent Electoral Commission (OYSIEC), Sunday Olugbenga Falana, also stepped down.

As of Monday evening, the Oyo State Government had not issued an official response to the resignations.

Political observers note that the timing coincides with intensifying political activities and realignments in the state as stakeholders position themselves for the next electoral cycle.



***This report is based on the resignation letters and multiple local media confirmations published on November 2, 2026.***
