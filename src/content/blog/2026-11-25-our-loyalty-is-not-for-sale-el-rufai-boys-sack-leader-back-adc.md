**Kaduna—November 25, 2026—The** political storm around former Kaduna State Governor, Mallam Nasir El-Rufai, deepened on Tuesday as the legitimate leadership of El-Rufai Boys/Girls, Kaduna State, announced the removal of its leader, Ibrahim Modibbo Sunusi, and reaffirmed unshaken loyalty to El-Rufai and the African Democratic Congress, ADC.

In a fiery press statement dated 25th November, 2026, and shared on X by @Safreenarrhh at 8:59 p.m. with 4,912 views, the group dismissed misleading reports that the movement had abandoned El-Rufai and declared support for Governor Uba Sani and the APC.

“*Let it be clear: EL-RUFAI BOYS/GIRLS HAS NOT ABANDONED MALLAM NASIR EL-RUFAI. OUR LOYALTY IS NOT SALE!”FOR*  the statement declared.

The group said following extensive consultations and directives from senior counsellors, Sunusi—also spelled Sanusi—no longer has the mandate to represent or speak for the movement. He was warned to desist from using El-Rufai’s name to advance personal political interests.

*“No individual is bigger than the movement, and nobody should use the sacrifices of our members for personal political benefits,”* it said, amid claims Sunusi had resigned from ADC as El-Rufai Live director and was hobnobbing with the Uba Sani camp after being picked up by police at Aruwa Plaza.

On El-Rufai’s court ordeal, the group expressed deep disappointment after the Federal High Court in Abuja on Wednesday adjourned his phone-tapping trial to February 15 and 16, 2027.

El-Rufai faces a five-count amended charge by the DSS over his February 13, 2026, Arise TV interview, where he suggested someone intercepted National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu’s call to tip him of an arrest plot at Abuja airport.

Testifying before Justice Joyce Abdulmalik, El-Rufai denied owning interception equipment, saying his “we tapped” comment was rhetorical embellishment to make the government feel opposition pain. 

The court had on October 26 dismissed his no-case plea after the prosecution called two witnesses, including activist Deji Adeyanju.

*“We call for a fair, transparent, and timely judicial process that respects his fundamental rights,”* the Boys said.

They reaffirmed their last directive—support all ADC candidates, including Alhaji Atiku Abubakar, Rt. Hon. Isa Ashiru and all senatorial nominees—urging unity through lawful means.

*“You may buy individuals, but you cannot buy our history... Our loyalty is not based on appointments,” the statement concluded: “OUR LOYALTY IS NOT FOR SALE. OUR HISTORY IS NOT NEGOTIABLE.”*
