**KANO, Nigeria—November 27, 2026—The** long-running political marriage between Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf and his former mentor, Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, appears irretrievably broken, as the Kano State Government on Friday accused Kwankwaso of deliberately dragging Yusuf into his 11-year battle with the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) to win public sympathy.

Speaking on Channels Television's *PoliticsToday*, monitored on X, the Commissioner for Information and Internal Affairs, Sunusi Bature Dawakin Tofa, dismissed Kwankwaso's allegation that Yusuf personally petitioned the EFCC against him.

> "The same person (Rabiu Kwankwaso) who has been battling with EFCC cases for 11 years will say a governor who is three and a half years in office is responsible for taking him to the EFCC. This is a deliberate attempt to blackmail Governor Abba Yusuf and create public sympathy ahead of the elections because of the unpopularity of his candidature and the unpopularity of his principal, Peter Obi, in places like Kano," Tofa said.

The reaction follows a fiery statement issued earlier on Friday by Kwankwaso, now vice-presidential candidate of the National Democratic Congress (NDC), in which he accused Yusuf of plotting to tarnish an image he built in nearly 50 years of public life.

Kwankwaso alleged that Yusuf personally submitted a formal request to the EFCC months ago seeking his investigation, and after failing due to the Commission's integrity, resorted to using a group identified as the Society for Anti-Corruption and Good Governance Initiative (SACGGI) to stage a protest at the EFCC headquarters in Abuja on November 25, 2026.

The group had petitioned the EFCC to probe properties allegedly linked to Kwankwaso in Abuja, Kano and Nasarawa States, demanding asset declaration verification and a lifestyle audit. EFCC Chief of Staff, Michael Nzekwe, who received the petition, promised it would be treated on merit.

Kwankwaso also accused Yusuf and his cabinet of leaking information to the media and attempting to manipulate INEC and the judiciary to block his camp's Kano governorship candidate, Aminu Abdussalam, from contesting in 2027.

But Tofa insisted Yusuf has "absolutely no involvement," noting Kwankwaso's EFCC engagements predated the present administration, which took office in May 2023. He also distanced the government from the internal crisis and court cases rocking the NDC.

The rift marks a dramatic fall for a relationship that produced victory in 2023. Yusuf, who served under Kwankwaso as Personal Assistant, Principal Private Secretary and Commissioner, rode to power on the New Nigeria Peoples Party (NNPP) with Kwankwaso's backing before defecting to the All Progressives Congress (APC) in January 2026.

*"You cannot win public sympathy through blackmail and unfounded allegations,"* the Kano government declared, adding that voters will judge both camps at the ballot in 2027.
