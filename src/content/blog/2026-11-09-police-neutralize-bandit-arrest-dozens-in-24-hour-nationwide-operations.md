**Abuja, Nigeria—November 9, 2026**—The Nigeria Police Force has released a detailed summary of operational successes recorded across several states in the last 24 hours, highlighting the neutralization of a suspected bandit, multiple arrests, recovery of firearms and vehicles, and the rescue of a day-old baby.

The update was issued by the Force Public Relations Officer, CSP Ani Iniedu, who outlined key achievements from operations conducted between 7 and 8 November 2026.



\#**Key Operational Highlights**

In **Niger State**, police and vigilante operatives engaged armed bandits around Kambarin-Daji in Ibbi Local Government Area. A fierce gun duel resulted in the neutralization of one suspected bandit identified as Ketu Alhaji Boyi. 

Operatives recovered a fabricated AK-47 rifle loaded with six rounds of live ammunition and arrested 11 additional suspects. The case is under investigation by the State Criminal Investigation Department in Minna.

In **Delta State**, the Command Anti-Cultism Unit raided criminal hideouts in Agbarho Community following intelligence. Eleven suspects (nine males and two females) were arrested. Items recovered included eight locally fabricated rifles, one double-barreled gun, two pump-action rifles, and 18 live cartridges. Preliminary findings linked the suspects to a hunter commander known only as “Believe,” who remains at large.

**Imo State** recorded a major breakthrough against a car-snatching syndicate. Operatives of the Anti-Kidnapping Unit arrested Daniel Uche Okeugo (also known as Macerro), 40, in the FCT, Abuja. 

Three other suspects, including alleged receivers of stolen vehicles and arms keepers, were also arrested. Recovered exhibits included three fabricated Beretta pistols, one locally made pistol, ammunition, and three vehicles — a Toyota Solara, Toyota Camry and Toyota Highlander SUV. 

The syndicate is linked to several armed robbery and vehicle-snatching operations in Imo State.

**Other Significant Operations**

In **Enugu Stat**e, five suspects were arrested over an armed robbery at a residential building in Umuatugbuoma, Nkanu West LGA.

Operatives recovered a short gun, a machete, eight mobile phones, a PlayStation console and other valuables. 

One victim who sustained machete cuts is receiving treatment at the University of Nigeria Teaching Hospital, Ituku-Ozalla.

**Oyo State** police detained eight persons, including an apartment manager and a security guard, following a drowning incident that claimed two lives at Max Apartment, Agodi Gate, Ibadan. Investigations are ongoing to determine the circumstances.

In **Bayelsa State**, Safer Highway Patrol operatives rescued a day-old baby found in the possession of 46-year-old Patience Iloge along the East-West Road. 

The woman allegedly purchased the baby in Port Harcourt. Efforts are underway to reunite the infant with its biological family. 

In a separate case, police arrested Somkiemi Tommy, 41, over the suspected murder of a sand business operator whose body was allegedly submerged in a river.



**Kogi State** police successfully reunited a previously abducted victim with his family. The victim was confirmed to be in good health while tactical teams continue tracking the remaining abductors.



**Police Statement**

CSP Ani Iniedu emphasized that the operations demonstrate the Force’s commitment to tackling violent crimes, cultism, armed robbery, kidnapping, and human trafficking. All cases remain under investigation by respective State Criminal Investigation Departments.

The Nigeria Police Force continues to urge citizens to provide timely intelligence to support ongoing efforts aimed at making communities safe
