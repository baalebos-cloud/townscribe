**Jos, Plateau State—October 6, 2026—A** youth has been injured in an isolated attack by suspected cattle rustlers in Bakkanuk Village, Forof District of Bokkos Local Government Area, Plateau State. Security sources confirmed the incident occurred around 8:50 a.m. on October 5, 2026.

The victim, identified as Mr. Kamaillo Godfrey Benedict, a member of the local Volunteer Guard Network (VGN), sustained injuries during the attack. 

Troops from Sector 5 of Operation Enduring Peace (OPEP) responded promptly to the distress report. Upon arrival, they found Benedict wounded while the suspected attackers had already fled the scene.

Benedict was immediately evacuated to the Jos University Teaching Hospital (JUTH) for medical care. Security personnel have launched efforts to track the fleeing suspects and prevent further incidents in the area.



**Context of Persistent Security Concerns**

This incident occurs against a backdrop of recurring challenges in parts of Plateau State, where cattle rustling, farmer-herder tensions, and isolated attacks continue to strain communities. 

Operation Enduring Peace has been actively responding to such threats, recovering rustled livestock in various operations and mediating disputes to prevent escalation.

Recent weeks have seen multiple reports of violence in Bokkos and surrounding local government areas, including attacks on vigilantes and other security-related incidents. Authorities continue to urge residents to remain vigilant and report suspicious activities promptly.

Security sources emphasize that troops remain on high alert, conducting follow-up operations to apprehend those responsible and stabilize the affected communities.

The Plateau State security architecture, including military and police units, is maintaining surveillance in rural districts while working with local stakeholders to address the root drivers of these recurring threats.

Residents in Bokkos and neighbouring areas are advised to stay alert and cooperate with security agencies as investigations and manhunt operations continue.
