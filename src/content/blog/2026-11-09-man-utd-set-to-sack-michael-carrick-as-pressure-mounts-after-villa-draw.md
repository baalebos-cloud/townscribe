**Manchester, England—November 9, 2026**—Manchester United intend to relieve Michael Carrick of his duties as head coach, according to a breaking report from LiveScore citing respected journalist David Ornstein of The Athletic.

The update comes at a critical moment for the club, with Carrick’s position having been under intense scrutiny for weeks following a dismal start to the 2026/27 Premier League season.



**Background of Growing Pressure**

Carrick, the former United midfielder who took permanent charge in the summer after a successful interim spell, has overseen the club’s worst league start in four decades. 

United currently sit in the relegation zone after a run of results that has left supporters and the hierarchy deeply concerned.

The pressure intensified after heavy defeats, including a 4-1 Champions League loss and a 3-0 home reverse to Bournemouth. 

At one stage, Carrick appeared on the brink of dismissal, with crisis talks involving co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe, chief executive Omar Berrada, and director of football Jason Wilcox.

The club ultimately opted to give him more time, allowing him to remain in charge for subsequent fixtures. However, the latest report suggests that patience may finally be running out.



**Recent Drama Against Aston Villa**

United’s most recent match, a chaotic 4-4 draw with Aston Villa at Old Trafford on Sunday, did little to ease the tension. 

Carrick’s side twice came from two goals down, scoring late through Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu to salvage a point. While the fightback showed character, the result left United in the bottom three and guaranteed they will spend the international break in the relegation zone.

After the game, Carrick insisted he would not resign and expressed confidence he could still turn results around. *“That’s not going to happen,*” he said when asked if he would walk away. He added that he expected to continue working with the squad during the break.



**What Happens Next**

Ornstein’s reported indication that the club now intends to make a change has reignited speculation about potential replacements. 

Names previously linked with the role include Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann, though no formal approaches have been confirmed.

United’s next Premier League fixture is away to Liverpool after the international window, a high-stakes clash that could further shape the decision if a change has not already been made by then.

Carrick’s tenure has been marked by early promise followed by a sharp decline in form. Supporters remain divided, with some calling for patience given the broader structural challenges at the club, while others believe a fresh approach is urgently required.

As of Monday evening, neither Manchester United nor Carrick had issued an official statement responding to the latest report. 

The coming days are expected to prove decisive for the future of the man in the Old Trafford dugout.



***This report is based on the update of David Ornstein, alongside broader reporting on Carrick’s situation at Manchester United as of November 9, 2026.***
