\*\*Police Train Thousands of Officers for Safe 2027 Elections\*\*



\*\*Subheadline:\*\* 

**Abuja, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—The Nigeria Police Force has concluded a five-day Election Security Management Training Programme as part of a broader nationwide drive to train 10,000 officers ahead of future electoral duties, including the 2027 General Elections.

The programme, organized in collaboration with the Nigeria Police Trust Fund (NPTF), ended on Thursday, 29 October 2026. The first phase covered 18 states and the Federal Capital Territory.



**IGP’s Charge to Officers**

At the start of the training, Inspector-General of Police Olatunji Rilwan Disu charged personnel earmarked for election duties to remain neutral, professional, and accountable. 

He urged them to protect voters, electoral officials and the entire electoral process without fear or favour.

The curriculum focused on election security management, electoral laws, strategic communication, intelligence-led operations, threat assessment, and the statutory responsibilities of the Police. 

Emphasis was placed on legality, impartiality, and inter-agency collaboration.



**Participants’ Commitment**

Delivering the vote of thanks on behalf of the participants, the course representative thanked the IGP and the NPTF for the opportunity. 

He commended the facilitators for blending professional expertise with practical insights and pledged that participants would apply the knowledge in their Commands and formations.

The officers also undertook to share the lessons learnt with colleagues in order to promote uniform standards in election-security operations across the country.



**Force’s Reaffirmation**

The Nigeria Police Force reaffirmed its commitment to sustained training, intelligence-led planning, and inter-agency cooperation. 

The goal, according to the Force, is to provide a safe and secure environment for Nigerians to exercise their electoral rights freely and without intimidation.



The statement was signed by CSP Ani Iniedu, Force Public Relations Officer, Force Headquarters, Abuja.



***This report is based on the official statement released by the Nigeria Police Force on November 2, 2026.***
