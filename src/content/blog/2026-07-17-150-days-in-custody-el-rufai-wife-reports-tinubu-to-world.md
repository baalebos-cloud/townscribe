**ABUJA, Nigeria**—In an emotional and pointed appeal that has ignited fresh debate about justice and political rivalry in Nigeria, Asia El-Rufai, wife of former Kaduna State Governor Nasir El-Rufai, has taken her husband’s prolonged detention to the global stage. 

Marking what she described as his 150th day in custody, she reported the Bola Tinubu administration to the international community, accusing it of political persecution and a threat to the rule of law.

Speaking not as a politician but “as a wife, a mother, and a Nigerian woman,” Asia highlighted the heavy human cost on her family: missed moments, interrupted medical care, and constant fear. 

She described the ordeal as “punishment before trial” and warned that selective justice undermines Nigeria’s democratic institutions.

El-Rufai was arrested in mid-February 2026 and has faced multiple charges across courts in Abuja and Kaduna, including allegations of abuse of office, money laundering, and wiretapping linked to his gubernatorial tenure. 

His family and supporters, including the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claim bail conditions are deliberately stringent and difficult to meet, while access to family, lawyers, and adequate medical care has been restricted. 

El-Rufai reportedly suffers from high blood pressure and high blood sugar.

Asia recalled her family’s support for President Tinubu during the 2023 campaign, pleading directly: their contributions should not result in this treatment. 

She insisted they are not asking for immunity—only fair process, reasonable bail, and humane conditions. “If guilty, let the court decide; if innocent, let the court decide,” she emphasized.

The case stems from investigations by agencies like the EFCC, ICPC, and DSS. 

Supporters view it as political payback following El-Rufai’s fallout with the APC and Tinubu. Critics of the government see it as a troubling pattern of using state institutions against perceived opponents. 

The presidency and anti-graft agencies maintain that the matters involve serious allegations deserving thorough investigation, denying any political motivation.

Asia’s international appeal calls on foreign governments, human rights organizations, and multilateral bodies to monitor the case closely for transparency, due process, and humane treatment. 

She framed it as bigger than one man: a test of whether falling out of political favor strips Nigerians of legal protections.

Beyond the politics lies a family in distress — wives pleading for their husband’s health and dignity, children missing their father, and a once-powerful leader now navigating prolonged uncertainty. 

El-Rufai, long a controversial and influential figure known for bold governance in Kaduna, now finds himself at the center of a national conversation about power, accountability, and fairness.

As Nigeria grapples with economic challenges and pre-2027 political maneuvering, this saga tests public faith in the justice system. 

Will it reinforce the principle that no one is above the law, or fuel perceptions of selective prosecution? 

The coming weeks — with ongoing court proceedings and possible further appeals — may provide clearer answers.

For now, Asia El-Rufai’s voice adds a deeply personal dimension to a complex legal and political battle, reminding all that behind every high-profile case are real people bearing profound costs.
