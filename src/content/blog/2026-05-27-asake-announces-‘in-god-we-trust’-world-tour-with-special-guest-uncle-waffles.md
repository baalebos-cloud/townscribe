**Lagos, Nigeria (May 27, 2026)** — Nigerian Afrobeats superstar Asake has officially announced his “In God We Trust” World Tour, in support of his fourth studio album \*M$NEY\*, released on May 1, 2026.

The announcement was made on Tuesday, May 26, via Instagram, where the singer shared official tour graphics. South African amapiano star \*\*Uncle Waffles\*\* will serve as special guest on select dates, primarily across the North American leg.

**Tour Details**

The 13-date tour kicks off on July 4, 2026, with Asake headlining *Afro Nation Portugal* in Portimão. It will then span major arenas across North America and Europe, with the following confirmed dates:



\- August 20– Chicago, IL – Credit Union 1 Arena

\- August 22 – Toronto, ON – Scotiabank Arena

\- August 23 – Laval, QC – Place Bell

\- August 26 – Brooklyn, NY – Barclays Center

\- August 27 – Lowell, MA – Tsongas Center at UMass Lowell

\- August 28 – Columbia, MD – Merriweather Post Pavilion

\- August 30 – Atlanta, GA – State Farm Arena

\- September 2 – Miami, FL – James L. Knight Center

\- October 15 – London, UK – The O2 Arena

\- October 24 – Paris, France – Accor Arena



Additional stops are expected in cities including Houston and Los Angeles.

Uncle Waffles is confirmed to join Asake on most North American dates, creating a high-energy fusion of Afrobeats and amapiano.

**Ticket Information**

Artist presale begins \*\*Wednesday, May 27, 2026\*\*, at 10:00 AM local time. Additional presales follow on May 28, with general public on-sale scheduled for \*\*Friday, May 29, 2026\*\*. Fans are advised to purchase through official channels such as Ticketmaster and AXS to avoid scams.

**Background**

The tour follows the strong commercial performance of \*M$NEY\*, which topped Apple Music charts in multiple countries. Asake has built a reputation for selling out major international venues, including previous successful runs at The O2 Arena, Barclays Center, and State Farm Arena.

The collaboration with Uncle Waffles highlights the growing synergy between the Afrobeats and amapiano movements, generating significant buzz across social media platforms since the announcement.

Further dates and additional special guests may be added. Fans should monitor Asake’s official social media accounts for the latest updates.
