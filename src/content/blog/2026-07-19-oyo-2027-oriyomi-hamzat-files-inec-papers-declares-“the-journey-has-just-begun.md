In a significant step toward the 2027 Oyo State governorship election, Alhaji (Dr.) Abdulrahman Oriyomi Hamzat, founder of Agidigbo 88.7 FM and a vocal human rights advocate, has formally submitted his candidate nomination profile on the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) portal as the Accord Party’s flagbearer.

Announcing the milestone on Sunday via his verified X account, Hamzat described the filing as “*another important step*” in a broader collective movement to build the Oyo State of citizens’ dreams.

*“The journey has just begun,*” he emphasized, framing it not as an endpoint but as the start of intensified grassroots work.

Known affectionately as “Kokoro Alate” and “Egbenu si mic,” Hamzat has built a formidable reputation as a broadcaster, journalist, and activist. 

Born in Ibadan, he studied at institutions including the University of Ibadan and various polytechnics, earning credentials in geology, mass communication, and linguistics. 

He worked across major outlets before launching Agidigbo FM, a platform celebrated for amplifying marginalized voices and championing social justice.

His entry into partisan politics gained momentum with a high-profile declaration rally at Liberty Stadium in Ibadan earlier in 2026. 

Positioning himself as a “people-focused” leader, Hamzat has stressed accountability, inclusivity, shared prosperity, and visionary governance that serves every community in Oyo State. 

He has repeatedly highlighted reliance on public support rather than vast personal wealth, stating, “I know I don’t have money, but I have what money cannot buy.”

The INEC submission marks a key procedural milestone for Hamzat under the Accord Party, following his emergence as consensus candidate and ongoing consultations. 

In his announcement, he expressed profound gratitude to supporters, volunteers, stakeholders, and “every son and daughter of Oyo State” for their prayers and commitment.

“*This mission is about building a government that truly serves the people. A government where you matter as much as I,” he wrote* *.*He invited all well-meaning citizens to join the movement, underscoring that the effort *“belongs to every one of you who believes that Oyo State can achieve more through visionary leadership, accountability, compassion, and shared prosperity.”*

Hamzat’s path has not been without hurdles. He has faced past legal and security-related challenges, including arrests and cases linked to public events, as well as political smears. Yet he has consistently rebounded, maintaining focus on service and community impact. 

Recent statements also reflect solidarity with Oyo residents during security incidents, such as abductions in Oriire Local Government, where he has praised rescue efforts while calling for stronger statewide safety measures.

With the 2027 polls still over a year away, Hamzat’s filing signals an active early campaign season in Oyo State. 

Observers note his appeal among younger voters, media followers, and those seeking alternatives to established political structures. 

His emphasis on divine guidance, grassroots mobilization, and non-traditional politics adds a distinctive flavor to the emerging race.

As reactions pour in—ranging from enthusiastic support to partisan criticism—Hamzat's message remains forward-looking: “*Today, we celebrate this milestone. Tomorrow, we continue the work… The best is yet to come.”*

The coming months will test the strength of his movement against established parties and candidates. For now, the broadcaster-turned-aspirant has taken a concrete step, inviting Oyo State to dream bigger.
