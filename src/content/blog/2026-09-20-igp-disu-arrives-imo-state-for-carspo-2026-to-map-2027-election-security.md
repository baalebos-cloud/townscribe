**Owerri, Imo State – September 20, 2026**—Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olutunji Rilwan Disu, has arrived in Imo State to kick off the Sixth Annual Conference and Retreat for Senior Police Officers (CARSPO 2026), a strategic gathering aimed at strengthening the Nigeria Police Force’s readiness for the 2027 general elections.

The official Nigeria Police Force account confirmed the IGP’s arrival on Sunday, noting that the conference provides a platform for senior officers to examine contemporary security challenges, refine operational strategies, and deepen collaboration with sister agencies and other stakeholders. 

The event forms part of ongoing efforts to boost institutional preparedness ahead of the polls.



**Conference Focus and Theme**

Scheduled for September 21 to 23 (with some reports indicating up to September 24) at the Landmark Event Centre in Owerri, CARSPO 2026 carries the theme "Developing **a Nigeria Police Roadmap for Effective Management of Security During Elections.”**

Deliberations will centre on intelligence-led policing, police-community partnerships, the use of digital technologies and artificial intelligence, and other modern approaches to secure the electoral process. 

The goal is to produce practical strategies that protect voters, electoral personnel, candidates, materials and the general public before, during, and after the 2027 elections.

The conference primarily targets officers of the rank of Commissioner of Police and above, alongside policing experts and key stakeholders. It is organized in collaboration with Lenders Consult International.



**Security Measures and State Support**

Imo State Police Command has already intensified security across the state. Commissioner of Police Garba Bosso confirmed that personnel have been deployed to the venue, hotels, major routes, the airport corridor, and other strategic points. 

Traffic wardens have also been stationed to ease movement, while officers have been directed to operate professionally and respect citizens’ rights.



Governor Hope Uzodimma’s administration is hosting the event for the third time and has expressed readiness to welcome participants, including high-level dignitaries.



**Broader Context of Election Preparedness**

The Nigeria Police Force has previously engaged the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) on security planning, activating measures such as intelligence mapping, coordination of electoral material movement, inter-agency collaboration, and countering misinformation. 

CARSPO 2026 builds on these efforts as political activities for the 2027 elections intensify.

Force Public Relations Officer CSP Ani Iniedu earlier described the retreat as part of the Force’s commitment to strategic leadership, professionalism, and operational readiness for a peaceful electoral environment.



**What to Expect**

The opening ceremony is set for 10:00 a.m. on Monday, September 21. Outcomes from the three-day deliberations are expected to include actionable frameworks that will guide police operations nationwide during the election cycle.

As senior commanders converge in Owerri, the gathering underscores the central role of the Nigeria Police Force as the lead agency for internal security and electoral stability. 

The arrival of IGP Disu signals the high priority the Force places on proactive planning months ahead of the 2027 polls.
