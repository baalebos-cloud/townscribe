**Yenagoa, Bayelsa State—October 31, 2026**—Presidential candidate of the National Democratic Congress (NDC), Mr. Peter Obi, on Thursday, travelled to Bayelsa State to attend the homecoming ceremony of Senator Henry Seriake Dickson, the party’s National Leader, while continuing his nationwide engagement with Nigerians.

Obi, who journeyed from Enugu to Yenagoa, used the occasion to highlight what he described as the federal government’s neglect of the state.



**Criticism of Presidential Visits**

Addressing the gathering, Obi reminded residents that after more than three years in office, President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has not spent a single night in Bayelsa State, even though he has spent over 260 days abroad.

*“I assured our numerous supporters and the people of Bayelsa State that this will not happen under my leadership. I will visit Bayelsa to hear directly from our people and respond to their concerns,*” he said.



**Pledges on Ports and Development**

Obi pledged to prioritize the upgrade of existing ports across the Niger Delta, including those in Warri, Port Harcourt, and Calabar. 

He also promised to commence construction of the long-delayed Ibaka seaport in Akwa Ibom State and establish a new port in Bayelsa to better serve riverine communities and the wider Niger Delta region.

He reaffirmed the NDC’s commitment to national unity, security, the fight against corruption, substantial investments in healthcare and education, and lifting citizens out of poverty.



**Traditional Celebration**

The visit concluded with Obi joining Senator Dickson in a traditional canoe war game and boat regatta in the senator’s hometown of Toru-Orua, Sagbama Local Government Area.

Obi congratulated Dickson on his successful return home and expressed appreciation to the party’s leadership and the people of Bayelsa for the warm reception and well-organized event.

He ended with his familiar refrain: “A New Nigeria is POssible, and Nigeria will be OK!”



***This report is based on the official statement released by Peter Obi on October 31, 2026.***
