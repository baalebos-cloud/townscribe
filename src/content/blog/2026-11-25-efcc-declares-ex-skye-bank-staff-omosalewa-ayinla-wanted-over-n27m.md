**Abuja – November 25, 2026 —** The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission, EFCC, has again declared a former staff of the defunct Skye Bank Plc, Mrs Omosalewa Ayinla, wanted over an alleged N27 million treasury bills fraud.

The anti-graft agency announced the wanted notice at 6:52 p.m. on Tuesday via its official X handle, @officialEFCC, in a terse post that simply read, "***OMOSALEWA AYINLA IS WANTED BY THE EFCC.”*** The post, which had garnered over 10,100 views within hours, attached a detailed wanted poster.

According to the Commission, Ayinla, 38, is wanted in a case of conspiracy, forgery, and obtaining money by false pretence.

Investigators allege that the former banker forged treasury bills certificates worth N27 million and handed them over to an unsuspecting customer in exchange for the cash sum.

“*Efforts to execute a duly signed Warrant of Arrest obtained from Magistrate Court 1, Iyaganku, Oyo State have so far proved abortive,”* the EFCC stated. *“The suspect is at large while efforts to apprehend her have been futile.”*

The Commission described Ayinla as a native of Ijebu North Local Government Area of Ogun State, dark-complexioned, of average height, who speaks Yoruba and English fluently. 

Her last known address was given as No. 7, Prince/Princess Elesin Meta Estate, Apata, Ibadan, Oyo State.

What has startled many Nigerians is that this is not a fresh case. Records show Ayinla was first declared wanted in April 2018 for the same offence, meaning the suspect has evaded arrest for over eight years.

Her renewed listing coincides with another wanted notice for one Adesanya Oluwatosin Adewale, 42, wanted over an alleged N3.5 million fraud involving a forged letter of non-indebtedness purportedly issued by Standard Chartered Bank to obtain a bank draft from Guaranty Trust Bank. Together, the two Ibadan-linked cases total about N30.5 million.

The EFCC is now appealing passionately to the public for help.

*“Anybody having useful information as to her whereabouts should contact the Commission,”* the notice pleaded, listing its offices in Enugu, Kano, Lagos, Gombe, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Maiduguri, and Abuja, as well as hotlines ***0809 3322 644, 0818 3322 644 and [info@efccnigeria.org](mailto:info@efccnigeria.org).***

Under Chairman Ola Olukoyede, the EFCC says it has intensified its tracking of fugitive bank insiders and forex fraudsters, having recovered over N1.23 trillion in recent years.

For victims of insider banking fraud, the renewed manhunt offers a flicker of hope that justice, however delayed, may still be served.
