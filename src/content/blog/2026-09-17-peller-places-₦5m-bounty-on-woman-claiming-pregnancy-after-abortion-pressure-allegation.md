Nigerian content creator and livestreamer Habeeb Hamzat, widely known as \*\*Peller\*\*, has placed a ₦5 million bounty on an unidentified woman who publicly accused him of impregnating her. 

The announcement, made on Thursday, September 17, 2026, has intensified an already heated social media controversy.

The woman, described as a TikTok content creator whose identity has not been confirmed, earlier shared a video alleging she was pregnant with Peller’s child. 

In the clip, she displayed what she claimed was a medical report from a doctor and said she had contacted him privately after discovering the pregnancy. 

According to her account, Peller’s response was a request that she terminate it—a demand she said she refused.

Peller strongly denied the allegation on September 14. In a subsequent livestream, he declared the woman ***“wanted”*** and appealed to the public for help locating her. 

*“The girl e dey wanted, anybody wey see her, 5 million make una bring her,”* he stated, according to multiple reports of his comments. 

Some accounts of the livestream also quote him warning that even pleas from her family would not soften his response if she was found.



**How the Drama Unfolded**

The controversy began last week when the woman’s video circulated widely online. She insisted the pregnancy was real and attached the purported doctor’s report as evidence. 

Peller, who married fellow content creator Jarvis earlier in 2026, rejected the claim and has consistently maintained he is not the father.

By offering a substantial cash reward, Peller has turned the private allegation into a public manhunt of sorts. 

The move has drawn mixed reactions across X, Instagram, and other platforms, with some users supporting his denial and others questioning the wisdom of placing a bounty. 

The pregnancy claim itself has not been independently verified by any news organization.



**Context Around Peller’s Public Profile**

Peller has built a large following through high-engagement livestreams and has faced several public controversies in recent months, including earlier disputes that also involved cash rewards. His marriage to Jarvis and the couple’s high-profile lifestyle continue to keep him in the spotlight.

At the time of writing, neither the woman’s full identity nor any independent confirmation of the medical documents has emerged. 

Peller’s camp has not issued a formal written statement beyond the livestream comments and social media posts circulating online.

As the story continues to develop, both the bounty offer and the original pregnancy allegation remain subjects of intense online discussion. 

Nigerians following the saga are watching closely for any verified update on the woman’s whereabouts or further response from either party.
