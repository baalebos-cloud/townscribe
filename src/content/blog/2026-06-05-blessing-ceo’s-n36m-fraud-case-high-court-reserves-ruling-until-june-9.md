LAGOS – It was another tense Friday at the Federal High Court in Ikoyi as celebrity relationship therapist Blessing CEO learned that her freedom would have to wait. 

Justice D.I. Dipeolu has reserved ruling on her hotly contested bail application, pushing the decision to June 9, 2026.

The self-styled socialite and influencer – real name Okoro Blessing Nkiruka – is no stranger to splashy headlines. But this time, the drama isn’t about love triangles or viral advice. 

She’s facing a two-count charge from the EFCC’s Lagos Zonal Directorate 1, for allegedly obtaining money by false pretense and stealing to the tune of N36 million.

Prosecutors argue the therapist-turned-accused convinced a victim to part with the hefty sum under false promises. 

Her legal team fought hard for bail, but Justice Dipeolu simply wasn’t ready to rule, setting Monday as the new decision day.

For Blessing CEO, that means at least four more days of waiting behind bars – or in detention – as the clock ticks toward June 9. 

The EFCC, which broke the news via its official X handle, has described the case as another major catch in its war against high-profile fraud.

Court insiders say the atmosphere was electric, with fans and foes alike packing the gallery. One observer whispered, “*She looked calm – but her eyes told a different story.”*

The EFCC has vowed to present all evidence, including transaction records and witness testimony, proving that the N36 million was “*obtained under fraudulent pretenses.”* 

Meanwhile, Blessing CEO’s supporters argue she’s a victim of a misunderstanding blown out of proportion.

All eyes are on June 9. If granted bail, Blessing CEO could walk out within hours – but with strict conditions. 

If denied, her legal team may appeal, or she could face a full trial that might send her to prison for years.
