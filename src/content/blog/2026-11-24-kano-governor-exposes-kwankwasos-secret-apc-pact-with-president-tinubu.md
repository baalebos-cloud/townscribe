**Kano, Nigeria – November 24, 2026** –Kano politics exploded on Sunday after Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf alleged that his former political godfather, Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, personally sent him to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu and asked him to swear by the Holy Qur'an over a secret deal to return to the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Speaking in an explosive interview with BBC Hausa presenter Aminu Kutama, Yusuf pulled back the curtain on the intrigues that followed the 2023 governorship election, when his victory under the New Nigeria Peoples Party (NNPP) was fiercely challenged by APC candidate Nasiru Yusuf Gawuna.

According to the governor, who formally defected from the NNPP to the APC on January 26, 2026, Kwankwaso initiated the outreach.

*“He himself sent me to Tinubu. He begged me to go and tell him that we had made this promise, and that if necessary, I should swear with the Qur’an right there that if we won, he wanted us to come together,”* Yusuf declared.

He alleged Kwankwaso asked him to assure the President that they would both return to the APC if Tinubu allowed the legal proceedings to run without interference. 

He claimed Kwankwaso held several meetings with Tinubu, sent delegations, and carried along lawmakers and local government chairmen.

“Everything that was done was done with the knowledge of the leader. We reached an agreement, but Kwankwaso did not fulfil it,” Yusuf said, insisting his defection was not betrayal but fulfillment of that pact, which collapsed after Kwankwaso publicly opposed it days later.

Kwankwaso had previously denied any deal, maintaining the Supreme Court decided the case on its merits when it upheld Yusuf’s victory in January 2024 after overturning lower courts that favored Gawuna.

The Kwankwasiyya Movement swiftly fired back through spokesman Hon. Habibu Mohammed, dismissing Yusuf’s claim as an excuse to justify defection. 

It challenged him to provide dates, witnesses, and documents and to explain why a 2023 understanding was used to justify a 2026 defection.

In a deeply human twist, Yusuf also bared his marital pain involving his estranged wife Zainab, Kwankwaso’s daughter, who reportedly filed for divorce in Abuja. 

*“He took my wife... doing what is essentially called abduction,”* he lamented, saying he was treading carefully because the matter is in court. Kano elder Mahmoud Adam urged restraint to protect his dignity, faith and Hausa culture.

Meanwhile, Yusuf has rallied the Igbo community in Kano behind Tinubu’s re-election for 2027, citing North-South rotation.
