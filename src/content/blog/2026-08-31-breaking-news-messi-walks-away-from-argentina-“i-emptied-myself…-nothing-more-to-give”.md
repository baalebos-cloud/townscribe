**Buenos Aires / Miami – August 31, 2026** — Lionel Messi has officially retired from international football with Argentina, bringing to a close one of the most remarkable national-team careers in the history of the sport.

In an emotional Instagram message posted Monday, the 39-year-old Inter Miami star confirmed the decision he first drafted two days after Argentina’s defeat to Spain in the 2026 FIFA World Cup final. 

*“Después de este tiempo que pasó desde la final, pensándolo mucho, quiero comunicarles a todos que me retiro de la Selección,”* Messi wrote — *“After this time that has passed since the final, thinking about it a lot, I want to tell everyone that I am retiring from the national team.”*

The announcement was quickly amplified by journalist Fabrizio Romano, who reported the breaking news to a global audience.



**“I Emptied Myself… I Have Nothing More to Give”**

Messi described the choice as one that *“hurt and hurts in the soul,”* yet one he now accepts as necessary. He insisted he always left everything on the pitch for the Argentina shirt—not only in the triumphant recent years but throughout two decades of service.

*“Queda poco y se van cerrando etapas y esta es una que me duele mucho,” he continued. “Amo, amé y amaré siempre estar en la Selección. Me vacié, ya no tengo más para dar y aparte vienen chicos muy grandes atrás que merecen estar.”*

***(“Little remains and stages are closing, and this is one that hurts me a lot. I love, loved and will always love being in the national team. I emptied myself, I have nothing more to give, and besides there are very big kids coming behind who deserve to be there.”)***

He thanked fans for 20 years of affection, said he would miss hearing the roar from inside the stadium, and pledged to support the team as ***“one more of you”*** from the outside in both good times and bad. 

Messi also expressed gratitude to his family, coaches, teammates, and officials, and closed by thanking God for making him Argentine.

A note at the end of the post revealed the words were written on July 21, just two days after the final. The recent death of his father, Jorge Messi, had only strengthened his resolve.



**A Career of Pain, Glory and Ultimate Redemption**

Messi’s international journey spanned more than two decades and included earlier heartbreak that once led him to announce a short-lived retirement after the 2016 Copa América final. 

He returned, transformed Argentina’s fortunes, and delivered the long-awaited World Cup title in Qatar in 2022 along with multiple Copa América crowns.

At the 2026 tournament in North America, the captain again shone, scoring eight goals and providing four assists as Argentina reached a second consecutive World Cup final before falling to Spain. 

Many viewed that campaign as a potential farewell; Messi has now made it official.

He leaves as Argentina’s all-time leading scorer and most-capped player, having rewritten the nation’s football history and given generations of supporters moments they once only dreamed of.



**What Comes Next**

Messi remains under contract with Inter Miami through the 2028 MLS season and has not indicated any immediate plans to retire from club football. 

For Argentina, the focus now shifts to a new generation of talent that the captain himself said deserves its opportunity.

Across the football world, the reaction has been one of gratitude mixed with the inevitable sense of an era ending. 

The boy from Rosario who became the face of a nation’s passion has stepped away from the blue-and-white shirt that defined so much of his life.

***“¡Vamos Argentina!”*** he signed off—a final call of support from the greatest player the country has ever produced.

Football will continue, but the Selección will never quite look the same again.
