**Gombe, Nigeria—November 13, 2026**—Professor Isa Ali Ibrahim Pantami, the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) governorship candidate for Gombe State, on Friday paid a solemn condolence visit to the family of the late Alhaji Sabo Usman Maiyadi at their residence in State Low Cost, Gombe. 

The visit, which took place two weeks after the prominent businessman’s passing, was marked by prayers, words of comfort, and a show of solidarity with the bereaved family.

The late Alhaji Sabo Usman Maiyadi was widely regarded as a respected community leader, elder, and upright entrepreneur within the Gombe Main Market. 

He was known for mentoring emerging traders and making notable contributions to business development, religious activities, and community service in the state. 

His death was earlier described by Gombe State Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya as a significant loss to the business community. 

The governor had prayed for Allah’s forgiveness for the deceased and asked that he be granted Aljannatul Firdaus.

During the visit, Professor Pantami extended his personal condolences to the children of the late businessman, including Alhaji Abdurrahman Sabo and Musa Sabo Usman, as well as other members of the family, friends, and neighbors. 

He joined them in prayers, seeking divine forgiveness for the deceased and strength for those left behind. Supporters and associates of the former Minister of Communications and Digital Economy accompanied him on the visit, reflecting the personal and community-oriented nature of the engagement.

The condolence visit comes at a time when Pantami has intensified his grassroots engagements across Gombe State ahead of the 2027 general elections. 

The former minister, who recently received a large and enthusiastic reception upon his return to the state, has made community outreach, including visits to bereaved families and traditional institutions, a consistent part of his political activities. 

Such visits have often drawn crowds of residents who view them as gestures of empathy and connection beyond partisan politics.

In recent weeks, Pantami has also spoken publicly about the challenges facing his candidacy, including earlier controversies surrounding the Independent National Electoral Commission’s candidate list. 

He has repeatedly called on supporters to remain steadfast in prayer and unity while promising accountability and people-centered governance if elected. 

His focus on poverty reduction, job creation, and support for farmers, youths, and women continues to form the core of his campaign message.

Observers note that Pantami’s consistent presence at moments of community joy and sorrow has helped strengthen his visibility among ordinary residents of Gombe. 

Friday’s visit to the Maiyadi family fits into this pattern of quiet, personal engagement that his supporters say distinguishes his approach.

As political activities gather pace across the state, acts of solidarity such as this condolence visit serve as reminders that leadership is also measured by how public figures respond to the personal losses of ordinary citizens. 

For the family of Alhaji Sabo Usman Maiyadi, the presence of the PDP candidate and the prayers offered provided a measure of comfort during a difficult period.

May Allah forgive the shortcomings of the late Alhaji Sabo Usman Maiyadi, accept his good deeds, and grant him Aljannatul Firdaus. Ameen.
