Umuahia, Abia State — May 27, 2026 — Governor Alex Otti of Abia State has used this year’s Children’s Day celebration to reaffirm his administration’s strong commitment to education, describing the state’s children as “the shining stars who carry the promise of tomorrow.”

In a heartfelt message shared on X (formerly Twitter) and delivered during the official celebration at Umuahia Township Stadium, Governor Otti highlighted education as the cornerstone of his government’s agenda. He noted that since taking office, his administration has consistently allocated 20% of the state’s annual budget to the education sector — meeting the UNESCO-recommended benchmark.

 *“You are the heartbeat of our state and the reason we work tirelessly to build a brighter future,”* Otti wrote. “*Our administration remains firmly committed to ensuring that every child in Abia has access to free, quality, and compulsory education.”*



The Governor pointed to tangible achievements under his leadership, including the recruitment of thousands of teachers, the establishment of smart schools, and the expansion of classrooms across the state. He also urged parents and guardians to take full advantage of the free education policy.

In a candid reflection, Governor Otti acknowledged past failures in the state’s education system, offering an apology on behalf of previous administrations:

 “*We apologize for the years when education was neglected, but I assure you that never again will the future of our children be relegated.”*

He stressed that the current focus is on building “a generation that is confident, resilient, and ready to lead,” while calling on children to embrace hard work and discipline.

This message comes as Abia continues to receive commendations for its education reforms. UNESCO has previously praised the state’s sustained 20% budgetary allocation, which has enabled the hiring of over 9,000 teachers in recent years and significant infrastructure improvements.

The Governor’s emphasis on education aligns with his administration’s wider human capital development drive, which also includes substantial investments in health and infrastructure. Many residents and observers on social media have welcomed the message, viewing it as both a celebration and a serious policy statement.

Parents, teachers, and children joined the colourful parade and activities at the stadium, where Governor Otti was physically present to interact with the young celebrants — a tradition he has maintained since assuming office.

As Nigeria marks another Children’s Day, Otti’s message serves as a reminder of the critical role education must play in securing the future of the next generation. 

The full text of the Governor’s address and social media post continues to generate positive reactions across platforms, with many Abians expressing hope that this sustained commitment will produce lasting results for the state’s children.
