**Port Harcourt, Rivers State—November 7, 2026**—President Bola Ahmed Tinubu on Saturday assured Rivers State Governor Siminalayi Fubara that he would not regret accepting federal intervention in the state’s prolonged political crisis, describing the governor as a committed democrat who showed maturity and respect for elders.

Speaking at a packed All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign rally at the Adokiye Amiesimaka Stadium in Port Harcourt, Tinubu thanked Rivers voters for their decisive support in the 2023 election and appealed for another mandate in the January 16, 2027 presidential poll to consolidate his administration’s reforms.

*“I want to thank you particularly for your maturity, understanding, respect for the wisdom of elders when we intervened. You respected our position. You maintained peace.* 

*You are a committed democrat. Fubara, you will not regret what you have done as long as all of us are alive,”* the President said, later embracing the governor in a public show of solidarity.



**Praise for Wike and Infrastructure Gains**

Tinubu also paid glowing tribute to Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike, calling him a “development partner” of the administration. 

He highlighted Wike’s hard work, vision, and contributions to national infrastructure, urging the crowd to acknowledge the minister’s role despite his non-membership of the APC.

The rally, organized by the Rainbow Coalition led by Wike, drew a mammoth crowd that filled the 40,000-capacity stadium and spilled into surrounding areas. 

Wike declared that Rivers State had already decided for Tinubu, stressing that the entire turnout came from within the state.

Governor Fubara pledged that under Wike’s leadership, Rivers would deliver at least three million votes for the President. 

He announced the state’s Rainbow Coalition arrangement involving APC and Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) members working together.



**Economic Message and Youth Appeal**

Tinubu recalled that when he assumed office, 27 of Nigeria’s 36 states could not pay salaries. He said his government had reversed that situation, clearing outstanding pensions and gratuities running into billions and ensuring more regular payments.

Addressing young people directly, the President said the 2027 election was about their future. “*To the youths of Nigeria, I am looking at you. This election is about you, it’s not about me. Your future depends on this election. This progress has no reverse gear,”* he declared.

He urged supporters to collect their Permanent Voter Cards (PVCs), describing them as the weapon against poverty through the ballot box. 

*“It is left for you to use the weapon you have, and that weapon is the PVC,”* Tinubu said, while promising continued focus on education, skills and welfare.

Vice President Kashim Shettima and APC National Chairman Professor Nentawe Yilwatda also addressed the gathering, describing the Tinubu-Shettima ticket as one of Nigeria’s most formidable political combinations and positioning Rivers as a key battleground for the 2027 contest.

The event formed part of Tinubu’s South-South campaign swing after a successful outing in Akwa Ibom State. Officials said the strong showing in Port Harcourt underscored the political realignment in Rivers following the resolution of the earlier crisis between Fubara and Wike.



***This report is based on the official State House press release and verified accounts from the APC Presidential Campaign Rally held in Port Harcourt on November 7, 2026.***
