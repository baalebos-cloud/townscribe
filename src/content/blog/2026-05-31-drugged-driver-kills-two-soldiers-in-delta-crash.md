**Asaba, Delta State –** A drugged driver caused a devastating road accident that claimed the lives of two Nigerian soldiers in Delta State. 

The tragic incident has sent shockwaves through the military community and raised fresh alarms about road safety and substance abuse among motorists.

The victims, both attached to the 63 Brigade of the Nigerian Army, died instantly when a speeding vehicle slammed into their motorcycle. 

The collision occurred on Saturday along 74 Road in Oshimili South Local Government Area. According to security analyst Zagazola Makama, who first shared the story on X, the soldiers were riding together when the fatal impact happened.

Transitioning from the immediate scene to laboratory findings, authorities confirmed that the driver tested positive for cannabis and cocaine after the crash. 

This revelation has intensified public outrage, as many residents question how an impaired individual was allowed behind the wheel on a busy road.

Security sources described the driver’s condition as clearly intoxicated, pointing to reckless endangerment that directly led to the deaths.

Furthermore, this tragedy unfolds against a backdrop of persistent security challenges in the Niger Delta region. 

While Nigerian troops continue to battle insurgency and banditry in various parts of the country, incidents like this highlight vulnerabilities even within relatively stable areas. 

The loss of these two dedicated soldiers underscores the everyday risks faced by military personnel, even when off major combat duties.

Local communities in Oshimili South now mourn the fallen heroes. Families of the deceased soldiers have received condolences from brigade commanders, while residents demand stricter traffic enforcement and mandatory drug testing for drivers. 

Many express frustration over recurring road accidents involving military personnel and civilians alike.

Moreover, the incident adds to growing national concerns about substance abuse and its deadly consequences on Nigerian highways. 

Experts note that drug-impaired driving contributes significantly to fatal crashes, yet enforcement remains inconsistent across states.

As investigations proceed, authorities promise swift justice for the victims. The driver faces multiple charges, including manslaughter and driving under the influence. 

Delta State police and military officials have launched a joint probe to determine if other factors, such as poor road conditions or excessive speeding, played additional roles.

This heartbreaking event serves as a stark reminder of the human cost of negligence. 

It also amplifies calls for better collaboration between security agencies and transport regulators to prevent future losses.

Nigerians continue to watch closely as more details emerge from this latest road tragedy.
