**ZARIA, Nigeria—November 27, 2026—In** a powerful reminder that national security is built not only on firepower but also on family welfare, the Chief of Army Staff (COAS), Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu, on Friday commissioned major welfare and training infrastructure at the Depot Nigerian Army, Zaria, Kaduna State.

The projects—two blocks of one-bedroom flats comprising 24 bedrooms for Senior Non-Commissioned Officers and remodelled dilapidated classroom blocks at the Depot Nigerian Army Nursery and Primary School, Chindit Cantonment—were unveiled on Friday, November 27, 2026, amid cheers from personnel, families and recruits.

According to a statement signed by Colonel Appolonia Anele, Acting Director, Army Public Relations, the commissioning reaffirms the Army's recognition that personnel welfare, family well-being, and educational development are critical enablers of troop morale, operational readiness, and national security.

General Shaibu said the intervention flows directly from his Command Philosophy and Soldier First Concept, which places the welfare, dignity, and well-being of personnel and their families at the forefront of military administration.

Providing decent accommodation for soldiers and a conducive learning environment for their children, he stressed, is essential to strengthening family stability, boosting morale and enabling troops to remain focused on their constitutional responsibilities of defending Nigeria's sovereignty and territorial integrity.

He described the new accommodation as a deliberate investment in improving the living conditions of senior non-commissioned officers—the backbone of discipline and regimental life—while the remodeling of the dilapidated classrooms demonstrates commitment to quality educational infrastructure for military children.

*"Investing in the welfare and education of soldiers' families remains an important component of building a resilient, motivated, and operationally effective force,"* the COAS noted.

The projects were executed under the leadership of the Commandant, Depot Nigerian Army, Brigadier General Usman Ahmad, who assumed command in July 2026 after serving as Commander, 27 Task Force Brigade, Buni Gari, Yobe State. 

Funding was provided by the erstwhile COAS and current Chief of Defence Staff (CDS), General O. O. Oluyede, a gesture Shaibu hailed as institutional continuity and visionary leadership.

Observers note that sustainable infrastructural development has become central to effective military administration, especially as the Army sustains operations against terrorism, banditry, and insurgency across theatres.

Significantly, the commissioning formed part of activities lined up for the Passing Out Parade of the 91 Regular Recruit Intake, the set of young Nigerians about to join the ranks after rigorous training at Nigeria's premier recruit training institution founded in 1924.

The COAS reaffirmed that the Army would continue to prioritize personnel and family well-being as a strategic investment in professionalism, retention, and combat effectiveness, complementing operational investments in weapons, training and intelligence.

For residents of Chindit Cantonment, the message was personal: beyond bullets and battles, home matters too.
