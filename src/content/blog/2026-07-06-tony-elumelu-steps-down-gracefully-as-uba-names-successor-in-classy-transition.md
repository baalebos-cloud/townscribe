**LAGOS, Nigeria** — In a significant but orderly development sending positive ripples across Nigeria’s banking sector, United Bank for Africa (UBA) Plc has officially announced the retirement of its long-serving Group Chairman, Tony O. Elumelu, from the board of directors. 

The iconic business leader and philanthropist will step down on August 21, 2026, after completing the maximum 12-year tenure limit for non-executive directors set by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).

The bank simultaneously named Emmanuel N. Nnorom, a seasoned non-executive director with deep banking expertise, as the incoming Group Chairman. 

His appointment takes effect on the same date, ensuring a seamless leadership transition at one of Africa’s largest and most influential financial institutions.

**A Legacy of Pan-African Impact**

Tony Elumelu’s tenure as Chairman since 2014 has been widely credited with driving UBA’s impressive pan-African expansion, digital innovation, and resilience through economic challenges. 

Under his stewardship, the bank strengthened its footprint across the continent while championing entrepreneurship through initiatives like the Tony Elumelu Foundation. 

His retirement marks the end of an era defined by visionary leadership and corporate governance excellence.

Emmanuel Nnorom brings extensive experience, having served in senior roles at UBA and as Group CEO of Heirs Holdings. 

His elevation is seen as a strong vote of confidence in institutional continuity and homegrown expertise.

**Positive Signal for Corporate Governance**

This planned transition stands out as refreshing good news in Nigeria’s corporate landscape, demonstrating adherence to regulatory term limits and proactive succession planning. 

Analysts view it as a model of maturity that could bolster investor confidence in UBA and the broader banking industry.

As Elumelu prepares to hand over the baton, tributes continue to pour in from business leaders, stakeholders, and the public, celebrating his contributions to Nigeria’s economic story. 

The move is expected to sustain UBA’s growth momentum while opening new chapters for both the institution and its outgoing chairman.

In a time when leadership stability matters more than ever for economic recovery, UBA’s announcement offers a beacon of professionalism and forward-thinking governance.
