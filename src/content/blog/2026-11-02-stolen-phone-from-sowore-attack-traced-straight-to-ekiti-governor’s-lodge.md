**Ado-Ekiti, Nigeria—November 1, 2026**—African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has alleged that a mobile phone stolen from a member of his team during a violent attack in Ado-Ekiti on Friday, October 30, has been electronically traced to the Governor’s Lodge in Ekiti State.

In a post on Sunday, Sowore stated that tracking data placed one of the stolen devices at the Governor’s Lodge, while another was located opposite Fajuyi Park in the state capital. 

He called on the Nigeria Police Force and the Department of State Services (DSS) to immediately release CCTV footage seized from Tawakalitu Restaurant, the scene of the incident.

*“You cannot bury the evidence,”* he wrote.



**Background of the Attack**

The assault occurred shortly after Sowore completed a radio interview at MIDAS FM 90.5. He and members of his team were at Tawakalitu Restaurant in the Dalimore area when a group of men stormed the premises, scattered food, assaulted team members, and stole phones and other items.

Sowore said the attackers, numbering about 20 and armed with sticks and bottles, beat him and threatened to kill him if he returned to Ekiti. 

He sustained injuries to his face, left leg and palm and received treatment at the Police Hospital in Ado-Ekiti. Several members of his security team were also injured and reportedly disarmed.

The activist has accused political thugs linked to the Ekiti State Government House and DSS operatives of involvement, claiming the attack was coordinated. He rejected the police description of the incident as a mere *“altercation.”*



**Official Responses**

The Ekiti State Police Command confirmed responding to a distress call, rescuing those involved and treating the injured. It initially characterized the event as an altercation and denied reports of a kidnapping. 

On Saturday, police announced the arrest of 11 suspects in the Atikankan area, recovering items including 13 mobile phones, walkie-talkies, suspected drugs, and charms.

The Ekiti State Government has denied any involvement in the attack and called for a thorough police investigation.

Sowore has continued to demand full transparency, insisting that the tracking of the stolen phones and the handling of the CCTV equipment point to a cover-up. 

He has also released video evidence he says shows the faces of some of the attackers.

The development has heightened political tensions in the state ahead of the 2027 elections, with human rights groups and opposition figures calling for an independent probe.



***This report is based on statements by Omoyele Sowore, police updates and official reactions as of November 1, 2026.***
