**Maiduguri, Nigeria – August 6, 2026**—At least six Nigerian soldiers have reportedly been killed following a fresh attack by suspected Boko Haram terrorists in Bitta, a community in Gwoza Local Government Area of Borno State.

The incident, which security sources say occurred on Thursday evening, marks another deadly strike against troops operating under the ongoing counter-insurgency campaign in the North-East.

According to security sources familiar with the development, the soldiers came under attack during the assault. One source confirmed the casualty figure, stating simply: “*Six men down in Bitta, North-East.”*

As of the time of this report, the Nigerian military had yet to issue an official statement. 

No videos or photographs from the scene had emerged publicly, and full details surrounding how the attack unfolded, the strength of the terrorist group involved, or whether any of the attackers were neutralized remained unclear.



**Persistent Threat in Gwoza Axis**

Bitta and the wider Gwoza Local Government Area have long been flashpoints in the fight against Boko Haram and its offshoot, the Islamic State West Africa Province (ISWAP). 

The area, close to the Mandara Mountains, has witnessed repeated ambushes, base assaults, and attempts by insurgents to disrupt military operations and civilian resettlement efforts.

The latest reported attack comes amid a pattern of renewed offensive actions by the terrorists against both military formations and civilian communities, despite sustained operations by troops under Operation Hadin Kai.



**Casualties and Ongoing Uncertainty**

Security sources indicated that the full circumstances of the Thursday evening assault were still being pieced together. 

There was no immediate information on whether additional soldiers were injured or if any civilian casualties were recorded.

The development once again underscores the high human cost of the protracted insurgency that has plagued Nigeria’s North-East for more than a decade. 

Soldiers continue to pay the ultimate price while holding the line against groups that have repeatedly targeted military positions in Borno State.



**Call for Official Clarity**

As reports of the incident spread, attention has turned to the Defence Headquarters and Theatre Command of Operation Hadin Kai for an official account. Families of personnel serving in the theatre and the general public await confirmation and further details.

The Nigerian Armed Forces have consistently maintained that they remain committed to degrading the capacity of terrorist groups and restoring lasting peace to the affected communities. 

However, the frequency of such reported attacks continues to raise questions about the sustainability of current operational strategies and the need for enhanced intelligence, logistics, and troop welfare.

Further updates are expected as more information becomes available from the field.
