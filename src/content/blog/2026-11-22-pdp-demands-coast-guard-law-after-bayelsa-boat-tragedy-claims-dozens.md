**Abuja, Nigeria—November 22, 2026**—The Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) has issued a strong statement on the fatal boat accident in Southern Ijaw  Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, declaring that Nigerians need concrete action rather than routine words of sympathy.

The party expressed deep sorrow over the tragedy that occurred on Friday, 20 November 2026, when a passenger boat carrying mourners from Ayama to a burial ceremony in Ukubie capsized near the river junction between Oweikorogha and Otuan communities.



**Rising Death Toll and Rescue Efforts**



The National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA) initially confirmed at least 24 deaths, with search and rescue operations continuing. 

Eyewitnesses estimated that about 300 passengers were on board. Later updates from state authorities and media reports indicated the toll had risen further, with more than 50 survivors receiving treatment.

The PDP extended heartfelt condolences to the bereaved families and prayed for the recovery of survivors treated in Ayama and Yenagoa. It also commended divers, marine police officers, and community members who risked their lives in the rescue effort.



**Criticism of Federal Response**



The party noted that the accident followed similar disasters on Nigeria’s waterways. It accused the APC-led federal government of offering only routine condolence messages while failing to address the root causes—overloaded boats, poorly maintained vessels, lack of life jackets, and weak enforcement.

*“For a country with 853 km of coastline and a vast network of inland waterways, maritime transport deserves far more attention than this government is giving it,”* the statement said. It stressed that Nigeria needs sound laws and properly equipped enforcement agencies.



**Four Key Demands**

The PDP demanded that the Federal Government:



1. Show the same urgency applied to the state police bill by advancing a coast guard law, beginning with reconsideration of the Nigeria Coast Guard (Establishment) Bill, 2026 (SB. 575), which the Senate stepped down on 22 July 2026.

2. Adequately fund the Marine Police and the Nigerian Navy to deploy personnel, boats and equipment for waterway safety enforcement.

3. Direct the National Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA) to enforce passenger limits, boat inspections and compulsory use of life jackets.

4. Conduct a transparent investigation into the accident and make the findings public.



The statement, signed by Comrade Ini Ememobong, National Publicity Secretary of the PDP Interim National Working Committee, concluded that Nigerians deserve a more responsive government and reminded the public that the 16 January 2027 elections will offer an opportunity for change.
