**Abuja, Nigeria—November 22, 2026**—The administration of President Bola Tinubu has committed ₦450 billion to agricultural financing programmes designed to strengthen food security, expand local production and support smallholder farmers across Nigeria.

According to a statement by Dele Alake, spokesperson of the APC Presidential Campaign Council, the Federal Government has already reached more than 500,000 farmers through the Bank of Agriculture. 

President Tinubu has also approved a ₦1.5 trillion recapitalization of the bank to significantly expand its capacity to finance the sector.



**Key Interventions Under the Package**



The Renewed Hope Smallholder Support and Value Chain Fund, valued at ₦250 billion, has reached farmers in 16 states. 

The programme has benefited more than 70,000 women and facilitated the distribution of 2.5 million bags of fertilizer and 300,000 bags of certified seeds.

A separate ₦200 billion Guaranteed Minimum Price Programme is currently being implemented. Under this scheme, about 170,000 metric tonnes of maize, rice, sorghum, and cassava have been procured to shield farmers from distress sales and price crashes.

Other targeted interventions include onion financing, dry-season farming support, ginger recovery efforts, women and youth empowerment schemes, and agricultural mechanization.



**Mechanisation and Energy Support**



A total of 1,571 tractors have been deployed to 23 states to support cultivation across 1.2 million hectares. In Kaduna State alone, the ginger recovery programme has assisted 3,200 farmers in Kachia.

Complementing these efforts is a ₦385 billion productive-use-of-electricity programme that involves the supply of 44,000 electric cargo tricycles and 100,000 solar irrigation pumps. The initiative is expected to benefit up to 500,000 farming families.

Alake stated that the combined interventions are intended to raise agricultural productivity, improve farmers’ incomes, and position the sector as a central driver of economic growth and national food security.

The announcement comes at a time when food prices and supply stability remain major concerns for Nigerian households, making the scale of the financing and the direct reach to half a million farmers particularly significant.



***This report is based on the official statement released by the APC Presidential Campaign Council and reported by the Nigerian Television Authority on November 22, 2026***
