**Ijero, Ekiti State – November 2026**— Leaders of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) in Ijero Local Government Area have formally constituted a Local Government Caretaker Committee and ward-level structures following the party’s statewide dissolution of existing executive bodies. 

The move forms part of broader efforts to rebuild grassroots organisation and promote internal unity ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The meeting of Ijero ADC leaders focused on implementing directives from the party’s national and state leadership after the inauguration of the Ekiti State Caretaker Committee, chaired by Dr. Bamidele Ekunola. 

Participants agreed that the caretaker arrangement represents the recognised direction of the party and resolved that ADC in Ijero remains one united political family with no room for factional leadership.



**Key Remarks and Call for Reconciliation**



Caretaker Chairman Chief Idowu Ajibola reinforced that members must follow the established party arrangement and avoid creating unnecessary divisions. He described the caretaker system as the path endorsed by the national leadership and called for collective work.

Hon. Lawrence Oluwole, House of Representatives Director General, appealed to leaders and members to *“let bygones be bygones”* and embrace one another as members of the same political family. He stressed unity, reconciliation and collective responsibility.

Captain Olu Alufa advised against internal conflicts and unnecessary disagreements, noting that efforts would continue to reach leaders who could not attend the meeting. 

He warned that factions could undermine the party’s electoral prospects. State Caretaker Treasurer Mr. Ogana Joseph also highlighted the need to strengthen local structures by bringing all members together.



**New Caretaker Committee and Ward Leadership**



The Ijero Local Government Caretaker Committee was announced as follows:



\- Chairman: Chief Idowu Ajibola  

\- Vice Chairman: Arowolo Ranti  

\- Secretary: Olowoyo Sunday  

\- OrganizingOrganising Secretary: Adesanya Fasina  

\- Publicity Secretary: Kehinde Obagbemiro  

\- Financial Secretary: Aromolaran Segun  

\- Treasurer: Awoyomi Joseph  

\- Legal Adviser: Omoyajowo Emmanuel B.  

\- Women Leader: Adaraniwon Modupe  

\- Youth Leader: Hon. Adebowale Ojo  

\- Welfare/Social Secretary: Alex Adesola Sesan

\- Assistant Publicity Secretary: Fapounda Francis  



Ward Caretaker Chairmen were also named for the 11 wards, including Osayomi Kayode (Ijero A), Adeleke Oluwole Omolade (Ijero B), Lasisi Ganiyu Ojoaro (Ijero C), Owolabi Felicia Olusola (Ijero D), Abe Sesan (Odo-Owa), Akin Akinleoye (Iloro/Ijurin), Oso Olaoluwa (Ekameta), Ebun Olajuyigbe (Ipoti A), Tijani Kareem (Ipoti B), Ojo Oladokun (Ikoro) and Adaralegbe Jimoh (Iloro A).

In addition, ward coordinators were selected to support Otunba Lekan Akinyele’s House of Representatives candidacy across several wards.

**Resolutions and Way Forward**

The meeting resolved that all leaders and members must work under the recognised caretaker structure. The Local Government Caretaker Chairman is to coordinate the formation of ward committees. Absent leaders will be contacted and encouraged to join. 

Members were urged to embrace mutual respect, avoid divisive statements and strengthen party structures through collective participation.

The gathering, attended by more than 20 leaders and members from various wards, ended with a renewed commitment to unity and reconciliation. 

The central message was clear: ADC in Ijero Local Government is one family, and all members must work together.

The development aligns with the recent statewide inauguration of Local Government Caretaker Committees across Ekiti’s 16 LGAs, aimed at deepening grassroots presence and preparing the party for the 2027 elections.



***This report is based on the official statement prepared by Kehinde Obagbemiro, Publicity Secretary of the Ijero Local Government ADC Caretaker Committee.***
