**Abuja, Nigeria—September 15, 2026**—Fresh and explosive allegations have emerged linking Bauchi South Senator Shehu Buba to one of northern Nigeria’s most feared bandit leaders, as whistleblower Hassan claimed the lawmaker “controls” Dogogide (also known as Dogo Gide) and facilitated specialised training for his associates in the capital.

In recent interviews with social media content creator D English Alhaji, Hassan alleged that individuals connected to Dogogide, including one identified as Baba Ngida (described as a relative or close associate), were brought to Abuja for advanced instruction in drone operations and professional sniping. 

He further claimed an aide linked to the senator, referred to as Adam, facilitated the movement and accommodation of these individuals.

Hassan went further, asserting that Dogogide wields greater operational capacity and influence than the notorious Bello Turji, another prominent bandit figure operating across parts of the north. 

The whistleblower said his knowledge stemmed from personal interactions and claimed to possess supporting evidence, including recordings, though he stated he would not pursue the matter in Nigerian courts, citing concerns that the judiciary would favour the senator and his “godfathers.”



**Senator Buba’s Firm Denial and Response**

Senator Shehu Buba has repeatedly and strongly rejected the allegations, describing them as fabricated, malicious, and politically motivated. 

In public statements and interviews, the lawmaker explained that Hassan was introduced to him in 2024 as an orphan seeking educational support. Buba said he helped enrol the young man in both Islamic and conventional schools in Kaduna and provided additional assistance, including a bicycle.

According to the senator, Hassan later abandoned his studies, secretly recorded visitors at Buba’s residence while under his care, and eventually fled to the Republic of Niger, from where he has continued making online claims. Buba has filed legal actions, including a defamation suit, and petitioned security agencies. 

He recently submitted himself voluntarily to the Department of State Services (DSS), police, and other relevant bodies for investigation over the renewed accusations.

The senator, who chairs the Senate Committee on Livestock and Animal Husbandry and is the Peoples Redemption Party (PRP) governorship candidate for Bauchi State in 2027, has faced similar allegations in previous years, all of which he has dismissed as smear campaigns.

**Broader Context and Public Reaction**

These latest claims come amid ongoing insecurity challenges in northern Nigeria, where banditry, kidnappings, and attacks by groups linked to figures such as Dogogide and Bello Turji continue to claim lives and disrupt communities. 

Security agencies have so far maintained relative silence on the specific allegations in the immediate aftermath of the interviews, prompting questions from some observers about the pace of official response.

The revelations have generated significant discussion on social media and among political watchers, with calls for thorough, independent investigations to either substantiate or definitively clear the claims. 

Parallel Facts and other platforms continue to track developments as the senator cooperates with probes.

As of this report, no formal charges have been announced against Senator Buba, and the allegations remain unproven. 

Both the whistleblower’s assertions and the senator’s denials underscore the deep complexities surrounding accountability, political rivalry, and the fight against insecurity in Nigeria.
