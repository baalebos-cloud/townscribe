**Buenos Aires, Argentina—September 15, 2026**—In a deeply emotional development for football fans worldwide, Lionel Messi will make a special farewell appearance with the Argentina national team on October 6. 

The eight-time Ballon d’Or winner is set to say goodbye at the iconic Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires during a friendly against Benin.

The news was confirmed by respected journalist Fabrizio Romano, who reported the arrangement as official. It comes just weeks after Messi, 39, announced his retirement from international football in a heartfelt Instagram statement, following Argentina’s runner-up finish at the 2026 FIFA World Cup.



**A Fitting Send-Off at Home**

Messi’s last competitive appearance for the Albiceleste was the World Cup final defeat to Spain in July. He later explained that the decision to step away *“hurts deep in my soul,*” adding that he had given everything for the shirt and that the recent passing of his father, Jorge Messi, reinforced his resolve.

Argentina’s football association (AFA) has scheduled a series of home friendlies in the upcoming international window: Bolivia on September 30 in Córdoba, followed by Burkina Faso on October 3 and Benin on October 6, both at the Monumental. 

The October 6 match against Benin has been designated as Messi’s official farewell, allowing him one last opportunity to wear the sky-blue-and-white jersey in front of the passionate Argentine supporters who adored him through triumphs and heartbreaks.

The Monumental, home of River Plate and a frequent venue for national team matches, is expected to host a sold-out, emotionally charged atmosphere. Fans have already begun preparing tributes, continuing the wave of applause, banners, and chants that swept Argentine stadiums after Messi’s retirement announcement.

**Legacy of a National Icon**

Messi leaves the international stage as Argentina’s all-time leading scorer with 125 goals in 207 appearances. 

He captained the side to the 2022 World Cup title in Qatar, ended a 28-year drought with the 2021 Copa América, added another continental crown in 2024, and led the team to consecutive World Cup finals.

His journey with the national team transformed from early frustrations and near-misses into a story of redemption and glory. Supporters credit him with restoring pride and belief after years of near-misses in major tournaments.



**What Comes Next**

While Messi will continue playing club football for Inter Miami in Major League Soccer, this October 6 appearance is widely viewed as his definitive goodbye to the Albiceleste. Coach Lionel Scaloni’s future remains a separate talking point, with his contract set to expire later in the year.

For millions of Argentines and admirers around the globe, the night at the Monumental promises to be unforgettable—a chance to thank the player who delivered the nation’s greatest footballing moments in a generation.

Messi has often said the love of the Argentine people meant everything to him. On October 6, that love will be returned in full force as one of the greatest careers in football history receives its proper home farewell.
