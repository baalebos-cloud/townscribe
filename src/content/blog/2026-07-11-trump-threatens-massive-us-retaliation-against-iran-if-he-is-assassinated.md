**WASHINGTON** — In a characteristically bold and unfiltered statement, US President Donald Trump has issued a grave warning to Iran, declaring that the United States would "**completely decimate”** the Islamic Republic if it attempts to assassinate him. 

The remark, posted on his Truth Social platform, comes amid heightened geopolitical tensions and intelligence reports of potential threats against the American leader.

*“1000 Missiles are Locked and Loaded and aimed at the Islamic Republic of Iran, with thousands more to immediately follow, should the Iranian Government act on its threat... to assassinate, or attempt to assassinate, the sitting President of the United States of America, in this case, ME!*” Trump wrote. 

He added that orders have already been given, and the US military is prepared for sustained action to “completely decimate and destroy all areas of Iran” for up to a year, subject to extension.

The statement follows recent intelligence shared by Israel with the US, indicating that Iran had developed a fresh plot to assassinate President Trump. 

Reports suggest this warning emerged against the backdrop of strained ceasefire talks, Iranian state media rhetoric, and broader regional conflicts, including events surrounding the funeral of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei where calls against Trump were reportedly voiced.

Trump has long maintained that he remains a top target for Iran, citing retaliation for the 2020 drone strike that killed Iranian General Qasem Soleimani. 

His latest comments underscore a policy of maximum deterrence, emphasizing readiness to respond overwhelmingly to any direct threat against his life.

The strong rhetoric has drawn international attention, raising concerns about potential escalation in the already volatile Middle East. 

While some view it as a necessary show of strength to deter adversaries, others worry it could complicate diplomatic efforts or further inflame tensions. 

Diplomatic observers stress the importance of de-escalation channels even as both sides exchange pointed messages.

This development occurs as the Trump administration continues to navigate complex foreign policy challenges, including nuclear negotiations, regional alliances, and domestic political pressures. 

The president’s willingness to publicly detail military contingencies highlights the personal dimension he attributes to US-Iran relations.

As the situation evolves, global powers and regional stakeholders are closely monitoring for any signs of further escalation or diplomatic breakthroughs. 

For now, Trump’s message leaves little ambiguity: any attempt on his life would trigger a response of unprecedented scale from the United States. 

The world watches to see whether this war of words leads to heightened military posturing or opens avenues for renewed dialogue in one of the most volatile relationships in international affairs.
