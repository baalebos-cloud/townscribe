**Lagos, July 22, 2026 -** In a decisive strike against cultism and violent crime in Lagos State, the Lagos State Police Command has arrested three suspected cultists during a targeted intelligence-led operation in the Imota area. 

The swift action, which yielded a significant cache of firearms and dangerous weapons, underscores the Command’s proactive stance in safeguarding residents and dismantling criminal networks.

Acting on credible intelligence from vigilant members of the public about the activities of suspected cultists, operatives stormed a location in Imota and apprehended Jelili Adeyemo (37), alleged to be the group’s armourer, alongside Bisoye Adewale (42) and John Okeafo (60). 

The operation highlights the critical role of community-police collaboration in early threat detection.

Exhibits recovered during the raid paint a disturbing picture of the group’s capabilities: one Beretta pistol, one locally made pistol, seven Dane guns, ten live cartridges, seven expended cartridges, one stun gun, two handcuffs, four cutlasses, and additional items. 

Preliminary investigations link the suspects to the Aiye Confraternity, with the Beretta pistol reportedly belonging to a notorious cultist known as "*Mayibone,*" who remains at large.

The images released by the police depict the suspects alongside the array of weapons, serving as a stark visual deterrent. Authorities have since arraigned the trio in court, where they have been remanded. 

Efforts continue to apprehend fleeing members and recover more arms.

The Commissioner of Police, Lagos State Command, CP Tijani Fatai, psc, mnips, reaffirmed the Command’s unwavering commitment to eradicating cultism and other violent crimes. 

*“We will continue to tackle these threats head-on,”* he stated, while commending the operatives and appealing to residents for ongoing support through timely information.

Public safety remains a priority, with the Command urging citizens to report suspicious activities via dedicated hotlines: 07061019374, 08065154338, 08063299264, 08039344870, 08080193432 (Marine), and 09168630929. 

This operation fits into broader efforts, including recent raids that have netted scores of suspects and weapons across Lagos in response to cult-related threats.

Cultism has long plagued parts of Lagos, often linked to territorial disputes, initiations, and retaliatory violence, particularly around dates like 7/7. 

The Imota arrests demonstrate effective intelligence gathering and rapid response, contributing to a safer environment for residents and businesses in the area. By removing these weapons from circulation, police have likely prevented potential bloodshed.

This success story reflects the dedication of officers under CP Fatai’s leadership and the power of community vigilance. 

As investigations deepen, the apprehension of "*Mayibone"* and associates would deliver another major blow to cult activities in the state.

Lagosians can take heart: sustained collaboration between the public and security forces continues to yield results in the fight for a more secure metropolis.
