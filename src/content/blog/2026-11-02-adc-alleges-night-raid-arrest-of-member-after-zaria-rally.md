**Zaria, Kaduna State—November 2, 2026**—The African Democratic Congress (ADC) in Zaria Local Government Area has raised alarm over the alleged arrest of one of its members, Mallam Aliyu Abdullahi, hours after a party rally held in Tudun Wada.

Hon. Nuhu Abdullahi Sada, Campaign Coordinator of the ADC Campaign Council for Zaria LGA, disclosed in a statement on Monday that security operatives raided Abdullahi’s residence at about 1:00 a.m. and took him away.

*“His wife and brother have confirmed the arrest,*” Sada said. *“We demand clarification on his whereabouts and urge the authorities to respect his fundamental rights and ensure due process.”*



**Rally Context**

The arrest reportedly followed an ADC rally held on Sunday, November 1, in Tudun Wada, Zaria. The gathering was organized in support of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar, the party’s presidential candidate, and Rt. Hon. Isa Ashiru Kudan, the ADC governorship candidate for Kaduna State.

Sada emphasized that peaceful political participation remains a constitutional right and should not be treated as a crime.



**Call for Due Process**

The ADC official called on security agencies to provide information about Abdullahi’s location and the reasons for his detention. 

He stressed the need for authorities to uphold the rule of law and protect citizens’ fundamental rights.

As of the time of this report, there has been no official response from the police or other security agencies regarding the alleged arrest.

The incident comes amid heightened political activities in Kaduna State as parties intensify mobilization ahead of the 2027 general elections.



***This report is based on a statement issued by Hon. Nuhu Abdullahi Sada, ADC Campaign Coordinator for Zaria LGA, on November 2, 2026.***
