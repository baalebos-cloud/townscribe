**Abuja, Nigeria—October 17, 2026**—All Progressives Congress (APC) National Chairman, Prof. Nentawe G. Yilwatda, on Saturday attended the flag-off ceremony and commissioning of the office of the Arewa for Asiwaju Support Group, describing it as a significant step in the organization's efforts to back President Bola Ahmed Tinubu’s re-election bid.

In a statement shared on his official X account, Yilwatda said he was honored to witness the event, which marked an important milestone in the group’s organizational journey.

*“The event underscored the importance of purposeful leadership, grassroots coordination, and structured engagement in the democratic process.* 

*Establishing a functional office provides a focal point for the group’s activities, bringing members together around a shared vision and common objectives,*” he said.

The APC chairman commended the leadership and members of the Arewa for Asiwaju Support Group for strengthening their structure across the Arewa region and wished them a successful take-off and productive engagements in the months ahead.

**Broader Context of the Unveiling**

The ceremony formed part of a larger inauguration of the Arewa for Asiwaju (A4A) campaign head office in Garki, Abuja, which was formally unveiled by Vice President Kashim Shettima.

The group, led by a Board of Trustees chaired by former Kano State Governor Senator Ibrahim Shekarau, brings together former governors, senators, and other northern political stakeholders. 

It aims to mobilise support for the Tinubu/Shettima ticket across the 19 northern states and the Federal Capital Territory ahead of the 2027 general elections.

At the event, Yilwatda moved a motion urging the North to back the Tinubu/Shettima ticket, citing the Vice President’s inclusive leadership and ongoing development projects in the region. 

Other attendees included Deputy Senate President Barau Jibrin and Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf.

The Arewa for Asiwaju platform has positioned itself as a structured northern mobilization vehicle to complement the APC’s efforts, focusing on grassroots coordination, voter education, and countering narratives about the administration’s performance in the North.

As political activities gather momentum, the commissioning of the group’s office signals a more organized push to consolidate northern support for President Tinubu’s second-term ambition.



***This report is based on the official statement by APC National Chairman Nentawe G. Yilwatda and related coverage of the Arewa for Asiwaju office inauguration on October 17, 2026.***
