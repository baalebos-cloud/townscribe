**Abeokuta, Nigeria—October 31, 2026**—Former President Olusegun Obasanjo has strongly criticized President Bola Tinubu’s defence of government borrowing, declaring that debt is worse than leprosy because it destroys generations rather than a single individual.

Speaking on Friday at the Ajibosin Platform in Abeokuta, Ogun State, during a forum themed “*Leveraging Artificial Intelligence to Solve Grassroots Problems and Accelerate National Development,”* Obasanjo described Tinubu’s earlier statement that *“borrowing is not leprosy”* as “*absolute nonsense.”*



**Direct Response to Tinubu’s Remark**

In April 2026, President Tinubu had told a delegation of Plateau State leaders at the State House: “*If we have to borrow, we borrow. Borrowing is not leprosy; we just have to work hard to be able to pay for it.”*

Obasanjo rejected this view outright. “*And for anybody to say debt is no leprosy is absolute nonsense. Debt is worse than leprosy. Because leprosy destroys only one person. Debt destroys generations,”* he said.

His comments come days after the Federal Government sought National Assembly approval for a supplementary external borrowing plan of approximately $20.52 billion and €1.045 billion (roughly $21.7 billion combined) under the 2026–2027 plan. 

The funds are earmarked for infrastructure, power, agriculture, healthcare, education, security and other sectors.



**Broader Concerns Raised**

Obasanjo also questioned the quality of leadership and economic management over the decades. He recalled that when he started his farm 47 years ago, the naira exchanged at ₦1 for $1.85, asking what the country had achieved to reach about ₦1,500 to the dollar today.

He further criticized Nigeria’s reliance on the World Bank and International Monetary Fund, arguing that the institutions “were not designed for us” and were established before independence.

The former president, who secured significant Paris Club debt relief during his 1999–2007 tenure, warned that excessive borrowing without corresponding productive returns risks transferring an unsustainable burden to future generations.



***This report is based on remarks by former President Olusegun Obasanjo at the Ajibosin Platform in Abeokuta on October 30–31, 2026, and related coverage of the Federal Government’s external borrowing proposal.***
