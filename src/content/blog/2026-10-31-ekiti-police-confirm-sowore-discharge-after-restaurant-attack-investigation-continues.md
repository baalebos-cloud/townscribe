**Ado-Ekiti, Nigeria—October 30, 2026**—The Ekiti State Police Command has confirmed the discharge of African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore following a restaurant incident in the Dalimore area of Ado-Ekiti on Friday.

According to a statement by the Police Public Relations Officer, SP Sunday Abutu, operatives responded promptly to the incident, rescued those affected, and ensured they received medical attention at the Police Cottage Hospital. Sowore was discharged at about 4:30 p.m. and returned to Ikogosi Warm Springs Resort.



**Details of the Incident and Investigation**

The Command disclosed that Sowore declined repeated requests to make a formal statement. Commissioner of Police Falade Adegoroye Michael has ordered a comprehensive investigation to identify those involved and bring the perpetrators to justice.

Police also noted that Sowore did not formally notify them of his arrival in Ekiti State, which could have enabled adequate security arrangements in advance.

Earlier reports circulating on social media had alleged that Sowore was kidnapped by armed thugs. The Command firmly debunked those claims, clarifying that the activist was involved in an altercation with some individuals at the restaurant and sustained a minor injury.

The police assured residents and visitors of their continued commitment to public safety and urged the public to disregard unverified reports capable of causing unnecessary panic or tension.

The investigation remains ongoing as authorities work to establish the full circumstances of the incident.





***This report is based on the official statement released by the Ekiti State Police Command and reported by NTA News on October 30, 2026.***
