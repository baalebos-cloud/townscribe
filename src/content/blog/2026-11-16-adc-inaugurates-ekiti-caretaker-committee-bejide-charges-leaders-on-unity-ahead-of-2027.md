**ADO-EKITI, EKITI STATE – November 16, 2026** - The African Democratic Congress (ADC) in Ekiti State has inaugurated its State Caretaker Committee, with a renewed commitment to internal reconciliation, party unity and organisational restructuring ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The inauguration, held on Monday, November 16, at the campaign office of the party’s state leader and former governorship candidate, Ambassador Dare Patrick Bejide, in Ajilosun, Ado-Ekiti, brought together national leaders of the party, state elders, local government and ward chairmen, women and youth leaders, and other critical stakeholders.

The ceremony marked a significant step in the party’s efforts to strengthen its internal structures, reconcile aggrieved members and build a formidable political platform capable of competing effectively in the forthcoming elections.

Dr. Bamidele Ekunola was inaugurated as the State Caretaker Chairman, alongside other members of the committee.



**BEJIDE CHARGES NEW LEADERS ON UNITY AND ELECTORAL VICTORY**



In his welcome address, Ambassador Dare Bejide challenged the newly inaugurated caretaker committee to prioritise unity and reconciliation, stressing the need to bring all members together in pursuit of the party’s collective interests.

Bejide urged the committee to work diligently towards strengthening the party’s structures and ensuring the success of all ADC candidates in the 2027 general elections.

He emphasised that the task ahead required cooperation, commitment and a shared sense of responsibility among party leaders and members, irrespective of their previous political differences.

The former governorship candidate wished the committee success in its assignment and assured its members of his full support, expressing confidence in their ability to move the party forward.



**EKUNOLA DECLARES END TO FACTIONALISM, PROMISES INCLUSIVE LEADERSHIP**



In his acceptance speech, the newly inaugurated State Caretaker Chairman, Dr. Bamidele Ekunola, expressed profound gratitude to the national leadership of the party for the confidence reposed in him and other members of the committee.

He also appreciated ADC members and stakeholders across Ekiti State for their support and cooperation, describing his emergence as a call to service rather than an opportunity to occupy a political position.

Ekunola pledged to provide an open, transparent and inclusive leadership that would accommodate every member, irrespective of past disagreements, political differences or affiliations.

Declaring a new direction for the party, he maintained that the era of internal divisions, acrimony and factional tendencies must give way to reconciliation and collective progress.

*“This is a new dawn for ADC in Ekiti State. The era of divisive tendencies, acrimony, factionalism and pull-him-down politics is over. We must forgive the past and join hands to build a united, disciplined and formidable party,”* Ekunola declared.

Addressing concerns about possible divisions within the party, the chairman assured members that there was no faction within the ADC in Ekiti State, emphasising that the new leadership recognised all members as part of one political family.

He maintained that the collective responsibility of every stakeholder was to work towards the success of the party and its candidates rather than promote individual interests capable of undermining its progress.

*“There is no victor and no vanquished. In this new ADC, there is room for everyone. We are one family, one party, one ADC. Let us put Ekiti first and ADC first. Let us work with one voice, one spirit, and one purpose,”* he added.



**RECONCILIATION, GRASSROOTS MOBILISATION TOP COMMITTEE’S AGENDA**



Outlining the committee’s priorities, Ekunola identified reconciliation, structural development, membership expansion and electoral preparedness as major areas of focus.

He explained that the committee’s immediate responsibility would be to heal existing wounds, reconcile aggrieved members and strengthen the party’s organisational structures from the ward level to the state.

He further disclosed plans for extensive grassroots mobilisation and membership registration to expand the party’s reach and consolidate its political presence across Ekiti State.

The chairman assured the national leadership and party members that the committee would not disappoint, declaring that ADC in Ekiti was back, united and prepared to offer the people a credible political alternative anchored on good governance, justice, equity and people-oriented leadership.

He particularly called on women, youths, professionals and other well-meaning residents of the state to join the party, describing ADC as the party of the future and a credible voice for the people.



**EKITI ADC YOUTH LEADER PROMISES GRASSROOTS MOBILISATION**

Also speaking, the newly inaugurated State Youth Leader, Hon. Adewumi Oluwasola, expressed his readiness to mobilise young people across Ekiti State towards strengthening the party and securing electoral support for its candidates.

He assured party members that the youth structure would actively engage young people at the grassroots, encouraging their participation in the party’s activities and political programmes.

Oluwasola pledged to work closely with other party stakeholders to build an effective youth mobilisation network that would strengthen ADC’s presence across the 16 local government areas.

His commitment reinforces the new caretaker committee’s determination to make youth participation a critical component of its political repositioning strategy ahead of the 2027 elections.



**COMPOSITION OF THE NEW ADC EKITI STATE CARETAKER COMMITTEE**

The newly inaugurated State Caretaker Committee comprises the following members:



1. Dr. Bamidele Ekunola – State Chairman

2. Mrs. Stella Funmilayo Alonge – Deputy Chairman

15. Prince Clement Adeoba – Ekiti South Senatorial District Chairman.



**STATEWIDE RECONCILIATION TOUR TO BEGIN**



As part of its immediate plans, the new caretaker committee announced that it would embark on a statewide reconciliation and mobilisation tour across all 16 local government areas of Ekiti State in the coming days.

The initiative is expected to provide an opportunity for the new leadership to engage directly with members, address grievances, strengthen existing party structures and encourage greater participation in the party’s activities.

The tour is also expected to lay the foundation for a more coordinated grassroots network as political activities gradually intensify ahead of the 2027 general elections.



**A NEW CHAPTER FOR ADC IN EKITI**

The inauguration represents an important organisational development for the African Democratic Congress in Ekiti State as the party seeks to consolidate its political presence and strengthen its capacity ahead of the next electoral cycle.

However, beyond the assurances and declarations made at the inauguration, the real test for the new caretaker committee will be its ability to translate its promises into tangible results.

The successful reconciliation of aggrieved members, effective grassroots mobilisation, transparent leadership and the ability to accommodate diverse political interests will be critical to determining the party’s future direction.

With Bejide pledging his support, Ekunola promising an inclusive administration and the youth leadership committing to grassroots mobilisation, expectations are now on the new committee to transform its message of unity into a stronger and more functional political structure.

For ADC in Ekiti State, the immediate assignment is clear: reconcile the past, unite the present and strategically prepare for the political battles of 2027
