Nubationg, Plateau- Security forces have arrested a man suspected of criminal activity following a swift response to a tip-off in Jos East, Plateau State.

Officials say troops from Operation Enduring Peace moved into Nubationg Village on Saturday after residents reported a stranger acting suspiciously.

Troops from Sector 1, Sub‑Sector 12 conducted a targeted check and detained 35‑year‑old Mamuda Idris, who remains in custody while investigators probe his movements and any links to local crime networks.

Local commanders described the arrest as the result of close cooperation between troops and community members. 

*“Timely information from residents enabled us to identify and take action quickly,*” one source said, noting that officers will continue intelligence-led patrols across the area.

Authorities did not disclose details about alleged offenses or whether Idris has prior records, but they emphasized that further inquiries are under way to establish his activities and any possible connections to broader criminal operations. 

Security agencies also stressed that arrests are followed by rigorous investigation to ensure due process.

The operation underscores ongoing efforts by the military and police to reassure communities in Plateau State after months of security concerns. 

Officials called on residents to sustain reporting of suspicious behavior to help prevent crime and protect vulnerable areas. 

Community leaders welcomed the arrest but urged transparency and speed in the investigative process to build confidence and avoid rumors.

As the probe continues, residents of Nubationg Village and neighboring communities remain watchful, hoping the arrest signals more effective security coordination and a reduction in incidents that have unsettled the area in recent times.
