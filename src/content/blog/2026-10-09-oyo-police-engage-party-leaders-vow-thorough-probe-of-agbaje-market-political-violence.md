**Ibadan, Oyo State—October 9, 2026**—The Oyo State Police Command has convened a strategic engagement with leaders of political parties across the state, assuring them of a thorough and impartial investigation into the recent violence at Agbaje Market while calling for peaceful political participation ahead of the 2027 general elections.

In a press release issued on Friday, the Command said the meeting formed part of proactive efforts to sustain security gains and prevent political activities from descending into violence. 

The Commissioner of Police emphasized that political disagreements must not threaten public peace, undermine security, or reverse progress made in maintaining a safe environment across Oyo State.

Political party representatives and other stakeholders present at the engagement praised the Command’s timely intervention. 

They specifically demanded a full investigation into the violent incident at Agbaje Market during a political gathering, insisting that anyone found culpable must be prosecuted according to the law. 

The stakeholders also pledged full cooperation with the police to maintain peace throughout the electioneering period.



**Background to the Agbaje Market Incident**

The violence occurred on Wednesday when the campaign train of the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for Oyo South Senatorial District, Hon. Aderemi Oseni, came under attack while consulting with traders at Agbaje Market in Ibadan North-West Local Government Area. 

Reports indicated that armed political thugs opened fire, hitting vehicles in the convoy—including a security utility vehicle—and shattering windshields. No casualties were recorded as security operatives attached to the team quickly contained the situation.

Similar disturbances were also reported affecting other APC campaign activities in parts of Ibadan on the same day. 

The incident drew strong condemnation from political actors, with calls for security agencies to identify and prosecute those responsible.



**APC Women Leaders Visit Police Headquarters**

In a related development, the women caucus of the APC, led by the party’s Women Leader, Dr. Adekemi Opatunde, alongside an aspirant, visited the Police Headquarters. 

The delegation expressed appreciation for the Command’s proactive stance on political security and pledged continued collaboration to ensure peaceful political activities.

Receiving the group, the Commissioner of Police reiterated the Command’s readiness to work with all political parties without bias. 

He urged leaders to notify the police in advance of planned political activities so adequate security arrangements can be made, thereby preventing a recurrence of incidents like the one at Agbaje Market.

Addressing the specific incident, the CP assured both the delegation and the general public that the Command would leave no stone unturned in its investigation. 

He stressed that the probe would be guided by professionalism, fairness, and strict adherence to the rule of law, with suspects identified, arrested, and prosecuted where evidence warrants.



**Call for Peaceful Politics**

The Oyo State Police Command appealed to political party leaders, aspirants, supporters, and other stakeholders to conduct themselves peacefully, avoid inflammatory statements and provocative actions, and promptly inform the police of upcoming gatherings. 

The Command stressed that political ambition must never be pursued at the expense of human lives, public order, or the collective security of the state.

It reaffirmed its commitment to professionalism, impartiality, and the protection of lives and property, urging all political stakeholders to treat peaceful participation as a shared responsibility as Oyo State prepares for the forthcoming elections.

Residents needing assistance can contact the Oyo State Call Response Center on the toll-free line 615 or the Police Control Room on 08081768614 and 07055495413.

The Command remains resolute in its determination to safeguard the democratic process and maintain peace across the state.
