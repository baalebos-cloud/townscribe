**Asuncion**— A major racism controversy has erupted at the 2026 FIFA World Cup after Paraguayan Senator Celeste Amarilla launched a series of derogatory and racially charged attacks on France captain Kylian Mbappé, only to later claim her Instagram account was hacked.

The incident unfolded in the aftermath of France’s hard-fought 1-0 Round of 16 victory over Paraguay on July 4, 2026, in Philadelphia. 

Mbappé scored the decisive penalty in the 70th minute, sending Les Bleus into the quarter-finals. What should have been a celebration of football quickly turned sour when Amarilla took to social media with inflammatory remarks.

Amarilla reportedly described Mbappé as ***a “brute,” a “colonised Cameroonian,”*** and made derogatory references to his upbringing, including claims that as a child he *“sucked on coconuts”* and that “***the most educated creatures he ever listened to were chimpanzees.”*** 

She also criticized him for allegedly refusing to shake hands with Paraguay’s goalkeeper.

The posts triggered immediate outrage worldwide. The French Football Federation filed a complaint, prompting French prosecutors to open an investigation into aggravated public insult or incitement to hatred. Potential penalties could include up to one year in prison and a substantial fine.

Mbappé responded firmly, calling Amarilla a “despicable woman” unworthy of public office. 

The controversy intensified when, during a Paraguayan Senate session, Amarilla reportedly doubled down on her criticism, using further insults while defending her position. The Senate subsequently passed a motion against her.

On Thursday, Amarilla claimed her Instagram account had been compromised days after the initial outburst. She posted on X (formerly Twitter) denying responsibility for content posted from that point onward. 

However, she later appeared to regain control of the account and posted a defiant message stating *“France can keep bothering me.”*

The senator had previously issued a partial retraction while demanding an apology from Mbappé and threatening legal action against him for alleged “**gender-based violence.”** 

Critics have widely dismissed the hacking claim as damage control, especially given her continued public defence of the remarks.

This episode highlights persistent challenges around racism in global football. Mbappé, one of the sport’s brightest stars with Cameroonian and Algerian heritage, has long been a target for such abuse despite his achievements on the pitch. 

The incident has drawn condemnation from football governing bodies, players, and anti-racism campaigners, reigniting calls for stricter enforcement against discriminatory behaviour.
