**ENUGU, Nigeria – Saturday, November 28, 2026** – President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has been conferred with the prestigious Igbo traditional title of **“Nwanne Di Na Mba"—meaning** “***A Brother From Another Land”*** – by the Enugu State Council of Traditional Rulers.

The honour, announced on X by the President’s Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, was bestowed on the sidelines of Tinubu’s campaign visit to Enugu State on Saturday.

According to Onanuga, the title was conferred by the Chairman of the Council, Igwe Samuel Asadu, at a colourful civic reception held at the cargo and international wing of the Akanu Ibiam International Airport, Enugu.

The recognition, the traditional rulers said, symbolizes the deep bonds of solidarity, brotherhood, and mutual respect between President Tinubu and the people of Enugu and the Southeast in general.

Tinubu is in Enugu to inaugurate the South-East Committee of his Presidential Campaign Council ahead of the 2027 general elections. 

He was received at the airport by the five Southeast governors, members of the Enugu State Council of Traditional Rulers, federal and state lawmakers, ministers and top All Progressives Congress (APC) stalwarts.

During the visit, the President is also expected to commission the newly constructed 300-bed Enugu Specialist International Hospital and other landmark projects executed by Governor Peter Mbah’s administration.

Addressing a massive crowd of supporters at the rally, Tinubu pledged renewed investment in education, youth development and agriculture, including interest-free loans for farmers to end hunger and boost food security.

“*We cannot build a nation of prosperity without investing in our youth. We will invest in all of you,*” Tinubu declared.

*“To our farmers, they will receive money—interest-free loans. We will relieve them of the burden of famine. We will connect you with infrastructure. Electricity will improve. The Eastern gas pipeline will work. We will connect the East, connect the South, connect the North. It is our honest promise for development,*” he said.

The President noted that the Eastern Nigerian rail line is already under construction, adding that the Federal Government is collaborating closely with Governor Mbah to accelerate infrastructure delivery in the region. He urged residents to collect their Permanent Voter Cards (PVCs) and mobilize massively for the APC.

Vice-President Kashim Shettima, who spoke at the event, urged the Southeast to embrace strategic political alliances and negotiations over emotion.

*“Politics is not only a game of numbers but a game of negotiations and strategic alliances. It is not a game of emotions,”* Shettima said, criticizing ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar and Labour NDCP candidate Peter Obi for lacking national coalitions.

Governor Peter Mbah described the rally as a clear declaration of political collaboration by the Southeast for Tinubu’s second-term bid, crediting the President’s economic reforms for pulling Nigeria back from the precipice and boosting allocations to states.

Senate President Godswill Akpabio also urged the region to back Tinubu, saying political collaboration would strengthen the Southeast’s chances of national support when it is the region’s turn to produce a president.

The traditional rulers, while endorsing Tinubu, appealed for more federal appointments for Enugu indigenes and a chancellorship position for the traditional institution.
