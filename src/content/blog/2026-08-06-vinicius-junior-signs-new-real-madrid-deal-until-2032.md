**Madrid, August 6, 2026** — Real Madrid have confirmed that Vinicius Junior has signed a new contract that will keep the Brazilian winger at the club until 30 June 2032, bringing a decisive end to a prolonged contract saga that had attracted strong interest from Arsenal.

The 26-year-old had entered the final 12 months of his previous deal and was free to negotiate with other clubs from January 2027. 

That situation sparked intense speculation about his future and prompted Premier League champions Arsenal to explore a high-profile move for the attacker.



**Arsenal’s Ambition and Madrid’s Response**

Throughout the summer, Arsenal’s interest was widely reported. Sources indicated that the Gunners’ ownership had given approval to pursue Vinicius as a top target to strengthen their forward line, with manager Mikel Arteta and sporting director Andrea Berta said to be keen. 

The prospect of losing one of the world’s most dynamic players on a free transfer or for a reduced fee had forced Real Madrid into action.

Negotiations between Vinicius’s representatives and Real Madrid had dragged on for more than 18 months, with the player’s camp seeking a significant salary increase and a renewal bonus. 

Talks had stalled at points, and the Brazilian was understood to be open to considering alternatives, including a move to the Emirates, if terms could not be agreed.

A breakthrough finally came after Real Madrid tabled an improved offer earlier this week. Vinicius accepted the revised package, which Spanish media link to around €24 million net per season plus performance-related bonuses—a clear uplift on his previous earnings.



**Club Hails a Defining Figure**

In its official statement, Real Madrid highlighted Vinicius’s remarkable contribution since arriving from Flamengo in 2018 as an 18-year-old. He has made 375 appearances, scored 128 goals and collected 14 major trophies, including two Champions League titles. 

He scored in both of Madrid’s recent Champions League final victories — in Paris in 2022 and London in 2024.

The club also listed his individual accolades, among them The Best FIFA Men’s Player award in 2024 and Champions League Player of the Season in 2023-24. 

*“Vinicius Jr. has become one of the most important players in one of the most successful periods in our history,”* the statement read.



**What It Means Going Forward**

The new deal removes any immediate uncertainty and allows Real Madrid to plan long-term around one of its most influential attackers. 

For Arsenal, the opportunity has closed — at least for now — as the Gunners turn their attention to other targets in the remaining weeks of the transfer window.

Vinicius returned to pre-season training this week after Brazil’s World Cup campaign. 

With his future secured at the club he has repeatedly called the team of his dreams, the Brazilian is expected to remain a central figure as Madrid continue their pursuit of domestic and European silverware.

The renewal marks a significant moment for both player and club: a talented star choosing to stay and write the next chapter of his career in white, after months of uncertainty that had tested the patience of supporters on both sides of the debate.
