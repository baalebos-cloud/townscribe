**Abuja, Nigeria—September 14, 2026**—The Nigeria Police Force has placed all formations across the country on high alert following fresh intelligence indicating that terrorist groups are mobilizing for possible coordinated attacks on places of worship, schools, National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) orientation camps, and other soft targets.

A confidential wireless message dated September 13, 2026, issued by the Force Department of Operations in Abuja and marked “*Security General – Treat as Very Important,”* was circulated to Assistant Inspectors-General of Police overseeing Zones 1–17, Commissioners of Police in all 36 states and the FCT, Mobile Police units, and counter-terrorism formations.



**Intelligence Details and Threat Assessment**

According to the directive, intelligence available to the police shows increased mobilization of terrorist elements for possible attacks. The message specifically flagged the movement of armed fighters from Katsina State through Kaduna towards Plateau State.

Suspected Islamic State West Africa Province (ISWAP) operatives and their local collaborators are reported to be conducting surveillance and reconnaissance of potential targets. 

Security sources expressed particular concern that the terrorists could exploit the resumption of the 2026/2027 academic session, when large numbers of students and staff gather in schools, to inflict mass casualties.

The timing also raises fears that such attacks could be designed to spread fear and destabilize communities ahead of the 2027 general elections.



**Operational Orders Issued**

Police commands and specialized units have been directed to intensify surveillance, increase patrols, conduct stop-and-search operations, tighten access control at vulnerable locations, and enhance rapid-response capabilities. 

Formations were also instructed to collaborate closely with other security agencies, traditional and religious leaders, school administrators, and NYSC officials, and to report any suspicious movements or threats without delay.

The Force Public Relations Officer had not issued an official public statement at the time of filing this report, though multiple independent outlets have confirmed the existence and content of the internal operational order.



**Broader Context of Insecurity**

The alert comes against a backdrop of persistent insecurity in parts of the North-West, North-Central and North-East, where banditry, insurgency, and communal violence continue to claim lives and disrupt daily activities. 

Similar warnings have recently been issued by other agencies, including the Department of State Services in some states, urging religious institutions to heighten vigilance.

Security analysts note that soft targets such as schools, places of worship, and youth camps have repeatedly featured in terrorist strategies because of their high concentration of unarmed civilians and the psychological impact of successful attacks.



**Public and Institutional Response**

As schools resume and NYSC orientation activities continue, education authorities, religious organizations, and parents are being urged to take practical safety measures. 

These include improved access control, community vigilance, and prompt reporting of unusual activities to security agencies.

The Nigeria Police Force maintains that the alert is a proactive measure based on credible intelligence and that every effort is being made to disrupt any planned operations before they materialize.

Nigerians are advised to remain vigilant, cooperate fully with security personnel, and report suspicious persons or movements through official channels without spreading unverified information that could cause unnecessary panic.
