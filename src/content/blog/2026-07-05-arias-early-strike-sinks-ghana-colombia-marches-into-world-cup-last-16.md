**Kansas City, Missouri – July 4, 2026** — In a tense, hard-fought Round of 32 clash at Arrowhead Stadium, Colombia secured their place in the 2026 FIFA World Cup last 16 with a narrow 1-0 victory over Ghana. 

Jhon Arias scored the only goal of the match in the 14th minute, proving once again why he remains one of South America's most dangerous attacking midfielders.

The goal came after an early injury to Jhon Córdoba forced a substitution. Luis Suárez, coming off the bench, delivered a dangerous curled cross from the right flank. 

Arias, ghosting into the box unmarked, showed superb composure to guide a cushioned finish into the bottom corner, sending the Colombian fans in the stands into raptures.

Colombia dominated possession (around 66%) and created more threatening moments throughout the first half. Despite Ghana’s efforts to push forward after the break, the Black Stars struggled to generate clear-cut chances and failed to register a single shot on target. 

Colombia’s defense, well-organized and disciplined, held firm to protect the slender lead.

This result marks Colombia’s third consecutive appearance in the World Cup knockout stages and completes the Round of 16 lineup. 

Néstor Lorenzo’s side now prepares to face Switzerland in Vancouver, while Ghana’s campaign ends with disappointment after a competitive group stage showing.

The match was played under sweltering conditions, testing the fitness and concentration of both teams. 

Colombia’s ability to control the tempo and capitalize on a swift counter-attacking moment highlighted their tactical maturity under Lorenzo. 

For Ghana, it was a familiar story of promise without the final clinical edge — a recurring challenge in major tournaments.

Arias, who previously shone in European leagues including a stint with Wolves, delivered a moment of magic that encapsulated Colombia’s tournament journey so far: efficient, unbeaten in the group stage, and dangerous on the break.

As the World Cup knockout stage intensifies, this victory sends a clear message — the Cafeteros are serious contenders once again. 

For Ghana, the focus shifts to rebuilding and preparing for future campaigns, with plenty of young talent showing glimpses of promise despite the exit.

The 2026 World Cup continues to deliver drama, and Colombia’s gritty progression adds another compelling chapter to this global spectacle.
