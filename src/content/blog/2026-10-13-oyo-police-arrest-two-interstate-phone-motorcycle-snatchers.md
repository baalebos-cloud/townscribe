**Ibadan, Nigeria—October 13, 2026**—The Oyo State Police Command has arrested two suspected members of a criminal gang specializing in snatching mobile phones and motorcycles from members of the public.

The suspects, identified as Timilehin Idowu, 23, and Kudus Agoeo, 25, were picked up following a raid on a residence in the Onirerin–Mokola area of Ibadan. The operation was based on credible intelligence received by police operatives.



**Gang Operates Across Three States**

According to the Command’s Police Public Relations Officer, DSP Ayanlade Olayinka, the suspects confessed during the preliminary investigation to specializing in phone and motorcycle snatching. 

They revealed that they operate as a three-man gang, using a motorcycle to carry out their crimes within Ibadan metropolis and in Ile-Ife, Osun State.

The suspects further disclosed that the gang’s activities extend across Oyo, Osun and Lagos states. They also provided information about other individuals linked to the network, including those involved in disposing of stolen items.



**Investigation Ongoing**

Police investigators are currently following up on the leads provided to identify and arrest remaining members of the gang and recover stolen property. The two suspects remain in custody and are cooperating with investigators.

Commissioner of Police Abimbola Ayodeji Olugbenga reaffirmed the Command’s commitment to intelligence-led policing and the sustained fight against street robbery and related crimes. 

He thanked residents for their cooperation and urged the public to continue providing timely and actionable information.



**Emergency Contacts**

The Command assured the public that it would continue working with stakeholders to dismantle criminal networks and maintain security across the state.
