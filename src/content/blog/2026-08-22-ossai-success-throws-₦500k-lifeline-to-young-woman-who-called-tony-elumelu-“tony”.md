**Lagos / Asaba – August 22, 2026** — A young United Bank for Africa (UBA) graduate trainee who went viral after addressing Group Chairman Tony Elumelu simply as **“Tony**” has received an unexpected lifeline from a Delta State government official.

Ossai Ovie Success, Senior Special Assistant on Media to Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori, on Saturday publicly offered the woman ₦500,000 in cash support, an all-expenses-paid VIP trip to Asaba, and an ambassadorial role for the fourth edition of the Delta Social Media Summit.



**The Viral Moment That Sparked Debate**

The incident occurred during the graduation ceremony of UBA’s Graduate Management Accelerated Programme (GMAP). 

When the young trainee took the floor, she began with “Good morning, Tony.” Elumelu, who initially appeared to mishear her, gently but firmly corrected her.

“*No, you won’t call me Tony. You’ll call me Mr Elumelu or TOE. You won’t call me Tony or Chairman. I don’t subscribe to that kind of Oyinbo life, okay?*” he said. The trainee promptly adjusted and continued with her question.

The short exchange was captured on video and spread rapidly across social media, triggering mixed reactions. 

Some Nigerians praised Elumelu for upholding formal workplace protocol and cultural respect, while others argued that first-name address is increasingly common in modern corporate environments and that the young woman should not face sustained online criticism for a momentary slip.



**A Gesture of Support Amid Backlash**

In a Facebook post, Ossai Success acknowledged the harshness of online reactions. “I know the internet can be harsh. 

Depression is real and words cut deep. She made a mistake, but we don’t need to torment her daily; she needs our support,” he wrote.



The package he outlined includes:

\- ₦500,000 cash support

\- Round-trip flight tickets to Asaba

\- Five-star hotel accommodation

\- A guided tour of developmental projects in Delta State

\- VIP access to the Delta Social Media Summit on Thursday, August 27, 2026, at the Mariam Babangida Leisure Park

\- Appointment as a brand ambassador for the annual summit

Ossai also said he would give the young woman an opportunity to share her side of the story, adding*: “Let’s turn this pain into purpose. She won’t be disappointed.”*



**Broader Conversation on Etiquette and Empathy**

The episode has opened wider discussions about generational differences in professional conduct, the balance between tradition and modern corporate culture, and the real-world impact of social media pile-ons. 

While many have defended Elumelu’s preference for formal address, others have called for greater empathy toward the trainee, noting that a single public correction need not define her career.

As of Saturday evening, it was not immediately clear whether the young woman had formally accepted the offer. 

The Delta Social Media Summit is expected to draw participants from across the country, and her potential appearance could give her a platform to address the episode on her own terms.

The story continues to evolve, reflecting both the power of a short viral moment and the capacity of public figures to respond with support rather than further condemnation.
