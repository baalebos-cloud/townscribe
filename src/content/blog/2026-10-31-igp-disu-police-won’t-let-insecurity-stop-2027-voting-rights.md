**Abuja, Nigeria—October 30, 2026**—Inspector-General of Police Olatunji Rilwan Disu has assured Nigerians that the Force will not allow ongoing security challenges to distract officers from their duties during the election period or deny any citizen the right to vote in the 2027 general elections.



Speaking at the flag-off of the Election Security Training for the 2027 General Elections, IGP Disu acknowledged the real threats facing the country.

*“Our country faces real security challenges, including terrorism and insurgency in the North-East, banditry and kidnapping in parts of the North-West and North-Central, and communal violence in several areas,”* he said. 

*“The Nigeria Police Force will not allow the election season to distract us from the fight against these crimes, nor will we allow them to deny any Nigerian the right to vote.”*



**Commitment to Dual Mandate**

The IGP’s statement underscores the Force’s dual responsibility: sustaining the fight against violent crimes while ensuring a secure environment for credible elections. 

The training programme, which began earlier in the month with an initial phase involving 10,000 officers, focuses on election security management, crowd control, conflict prevention, rules of engagement, human rights, and intelligence-led operations.

Disu has repeatedly charged personnel to remain neutral, professional, and accountable throughout the electoral process. 

He has also warned against political violence, voter intimidation, ballot-box snatching, and vote-buying, promising that offenders will be arrested and prosecuted.



**Broader Security Context**

The remarks come amid continued efforts by the Nigeria Police Force to tackle multiple threats across different regions. 

Recent operational updates from the Force have highlighted successes in rescuing kidnap victims, neutralizing bandits, recovering firearms, and intercepting criminal networks in several states.

By linking the ongoing security operations directly to election preparedness, the IGP signalled that the Force intends to maintain pressure on criminals while deploying personnel for electoral duties.

The training is being implemented in collaboration with the Nigeria Police Trust Fund and is designed to produce officers who are disciplined, impartial, and responsive.
