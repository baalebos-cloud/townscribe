**Kyiv, Ukraine – June 2, 2026**

Russia unleashed a ferocious overnight assault on Ukraine’s capital, raining missiles and drones across Kyiv and plunging terrified residents into chaos. 

The brutal attack killed at least three people, injured dozens more, and left families desperately searching for loved ones trapped beneath collapsed buildings. 

Ukrainian authorities reported that waves of explosive strikes hammered multiple districts in the early hours of Tuesday. 

Tymur Tkachenko, head of Kyiv’s military administration, confirmed the grim toll: at least 29 people wounded amid widespread destruction. 

Rescue teams worked frantically through the night as residential structures suffered heavy damage, with some buildings partially collapsing and trapping civilians inside the rubble. 

This latest barrage fits into Russia’s pattern of intensified aerial campaigns. 

In recent weeks, Moscow has launched some of the largest drone and missile assaults of the war, including strikes involving hypersonic Oreshnik missiles and hundreds of Shahed drones. 

Just days earlier, similar attacks killed dozens across Ukraine, highlighting the relentless pressure on civilian areas despite Ukrainian air defenses intercepting many incoming threats. 



**Human Cost Deepens Amid Ongoing War**



Ordinary Kyiv residents bore the brunt once again. Emergency services rushed to scenes of devastation where fires raged and thick smoke filled the air.

 Parents clutched children while sheltering in metro stations, a haunting routine now in its fourth year of war. 

The psychological scars run deep as families grieve lost loved ones and worry about those still missing under debris. 



Military analysts note that these strikes aim to exhaust Ukraine’s air defense systems and break civilian morale. 

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has repeatedly pleaded for more Western support, especially air interceptors, as Russia stockpiles munitions for sustained pressure. 

International condemnation has been swift, with allies viewing the attacks as deliberate targeting of civilian infrastructure. Yet the violence continues unabated. 

As rescue operations persist, the full extent of casualties may rise. 

This assault serves as another painful reminder of the war’s human toll, where nights in Kyiv bring not rest, but fear of sudden death from above. 

The world watches as Ukraine endures, its people displaying remarkable resilience even as the conflict grinds on with no end in sight.
