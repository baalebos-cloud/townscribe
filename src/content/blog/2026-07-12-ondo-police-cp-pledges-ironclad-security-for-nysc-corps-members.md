**AKURE, Ondo State** — In a move that underscores the critical importance of safeguarding Nigeria’s young graduates serving the nation, the Commissioner of Police in Ondo State, CP Felix Ohagwu, has strongly reaffirmed the Command’s unwavering commitment to the safety and security of National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) members deployed across the state.

The assurance came during a courtesy visit on July 9, 2026, when Mrs. Bakare Kuburat, the Ondo State NYSC Coordinator, led her management team to the Police Command Headquarters in Akure. 

The warm engagement highlighted the deepening strategic partnership between the two institutions.

Mrs. Bakare expressed deep gratitude to CP Ohagwu and the entire Ondo State Police Command for their consistent support, particularly in protecting corps members before, during, and after orientation exercises, as well as throughout their service year. 

She called for even stronger inter-agency synergy, enhanced intelligence sharing, and institutional cooperation to tackle emerging security challenges.

In response, CP Felix Ohagwu warmly welcomed the delegation and commended the NYSC for its pivotal role in fostering national unity, youth development, and nation-building. 

He assured them that all concerns raised had been duly noted and pledged the Command’s full readiness to continue close collaboration for the protection of corps members and the maintenance of peace across Ondo State.

Photographs from the meeting captured the cordial atmosphere: Mrs. Bakare, elegantly dressed in traditional attire, presenting the NYSC magazine to the Commissioner, who appeared sharp in his camouflage uniform and beret. 

A group photograph with senior police officers further symbolized unity of purpose.

### Context and Broader Significance

This high-level engagement is part of ongoing efforts by security agencies and the NYSC to prioritize the welfare of corps members, who are often posted to unfamiliar environments and sometimes face security risks. 

Ondo State, like many others, has seen proactive policing initiatives under CP Ohagwu’s leadership since he assumed duty earlier in 2026.

The NYSC scheme, established after the Nigerian Civil War to promote national integration, continues to play a vital role in youth development. 

However, ensuring the safety of participants remains a shared responsibility between the Corps and security forces. 

Calls for stronger collaboration reflect broader national concerns about youth safety amid various security dynamics.

CP Ohagwu’s proactive stance — emphasizing intelligence-led operations and stakeholder partnerships — sends a reassuring message to parents, corps members, and the public that the welfare of these young Nigerians is a top priority in Ondo State.

As the visit concluded with mutual commitments and a group photograph, it served as a powerful reminder: when institutions work hand-in-hand, the dreams and contributions of Nigeria’s youth can flourish in a secure environment. 

The Ondo State Police Command and NYSC partnership stands as a model worth emulating across the federation.
