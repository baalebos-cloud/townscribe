**MADRID, Spain** — In a decisive and unyielding declaration, Atlético Madrid CEO Miguel Ángel Gil Marín has slammed the door on any summer departure for star striker Julián Álvarez, making it crystal clear that the Argentine international is not for sale — no matter the price.

Speaking firmly about the club’s position, Gil Marín stated: 

“*My position is clear, the club’s position is clear. We’ve made it known to the player, his representatives, and the president of Barcelona.”* 

He added that Atlético has “absolutely no doubt” that the club is the perfect place for Álvarez, describing him as the ideal centre-forward for their project.

The CEO went further, revealing they rejected a €100 million offer and would turn down even €150 million or €200 million bids. 

“*We do NOT want to transfer him,*” he emphasized, while delivering a pointed response to Barcelona’s interest: *“I recently heard the president say that the offer he made to Atlético Madrid wasn’t unlimited.* 

*My only response is that our answer is unlimited.”*

This firm stance comes amid a high-profile summer saga. Álvarez, who joined Atlético from Manchester City in 2024, has publicly expressed a desire to leave in order to *“fulfill his dream,”* widely believed to point toward Barcelona. 

His comments during the 2026 World Cup with Argentina added fuel to the fire, creating visible tension between player and club.

Atlético has not included Álvarez in recent promotional materials, such as the launch of their new 2026-27 kit, signaling their frustration. 

The club has also considered formal complaints to FIFA regarding Barcelona’s approach to a contracted player.

Álvarez remains under contract until 2030, with a reported release clause of €500 million. Despite interest from several top European clubs, including Barcelona as the primary suitor, Atlético’s hierarchy—led by Gil Marín and President Enrique Cerezo—views the 26-year-old as central to Diego Simeone’s long-term plans.

For Atlético fans, the message brings relief and pride: one of their most talented attackers is staying, at least for now. 

For Álvarez, it presents a challenging situation — balancing personal ambitions with contractual obligations and club loyalty. Barcelona, meanwhile, faces a tough negotiation path if they hope to secure their top target.

This saga highlights the intense human drama of modern football: a player chasing his dream, a club protecting its investment and project, and rival clubs navigating delicate transfer politics. 

Gil Marín’s words reflect not just financial strategy but a deeper commitment to building stability at the Metropolitano.

As the transfer window heats up, all eyes remain on whether positions will soften or if Álvarez will ultimately push harder for an exit. 

For now, Atlético’s message is loud and unmistakable: Julián Álvarez is going nowhere this summer.
