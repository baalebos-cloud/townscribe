ADO-EKITI, EKITI STATE — The African Democratic Congress (ADC) Ekiti State Caretaker Committee has formally inaugurated Local Government Caretaker Committees across the state’s 16 Local Government Areas, following approval by the national leadership of the party. The list of the newly appointed Local Government Caretaker Chairmen was released and signed by the State Caretaker Chairman, Dr. Bamidele Ekunola, on 17 November 2026.

The inauguration marks a critical step in the party’s efforts to rebuild its grassroots structures, promote internal reconciliation, and prepare for the 2027 general elections. 

The process has been accompanied by addresses from the State Caretaker Chairman, Dr. Bamidele Ekunola, and the State Secretary, Rt. Hon. Gboyega Aribisogan, both of whom stressed the need for unity, disciplined organization, and collective mobilization at the local level.

**Chairman Stresses Reconciliation and Inclusive Leadership**

Addressing Chairmen during the local government inaugurations, Dr. Bamidele Ekunola described the assignment as a call to service. 

He thanked the national leadership for the confidence reposed in the committee and expressed appreciation to ADC members across Ekiti for their support.

*“This is a new dawn for ADC in Ekiti State. The era of divisive tendencies, acrimony, factionalism, and pull-him-down politics is over. We must forgive the past and join hands to build a united, disciplined, and formidable party,*” Ekunola stated.

The chairman assured members that the new leadership recognizes every stakeholder as part of one political family, insisting there is no victor and no vanquished. 

He pledged an open, transparent, and inclusive administration that would accommodate all members irrespective of past differences. 

Ekunola identified reconciliation, structural development from the ward level upward, membership expansion, and electoral preparedness as the committee’s immediate priorities.

He further disclosed that the state leadership would embark on a statewide reconciliation and mobilization tour across all 16 Local Government Areas. 

The tour, he said, would enable direct engagement with members, address grievances, strengthen party structures, and encourage greater participation. 

Ekunola particularly appealed to women, youths, professionals, and other well-meaning residents of Ekiti to join the party, describing ADC as the party of the future and a credible voice for the people.

**Secretary Highlights Organization, Discipline, and Coordination**

Rt. Hon. Gboyega Aribisogan, the State Secretary, focused on the administrative and organizational demands of the moment. 

Speaking in the context of the local government inaugurations, Aribisogan stressed that the newly appointed Local Government Caretaker Committees must operate with discipline, transparency, and strict adherence to the party’s constitution.

He underscored the need for seamless coordination between the state leadership and the local government structures so that mobilization, membership registration, and conflict resolution can proceed without the disruptions that previously hampered the party. 

Aribisogan called on the newly inaugurated local government chairmen and their teams to prioritize grassroots engagement, maintain accurate records, and ensure that every ward feels the presence of a functional and responsive party machinery.

The Secretary also reiterated the leadership’s commitment to fair processes in the handling of internal disputes. He noted that previous executives had been dissolved by the national leadership, removing any lingering claims of parallel authority, and urged all members to disregard unauthorized statements or actions from dissolved structures while channelling concerns through the recognized caretaker organs.

**Way Forward**

The inauguration of the Local Government Caretaker Committees across the 16 LGAs is expected to provide the party with a stronger and more coordinated presence at the grassroots. 

Party leaders have emphasized that internal cohesion, membership expansion, and disciplined organization remain essential foundations for the political contests ahead.

With the state leadership providing direction and the newly appointed local government chairmen now in place, attention has shifted to translating declarations of unity into functional, people-oriented party structures capable of offering Ekiti residents a credible alternative. 

The success of this effort will depend on the ability of both the state and local caretaker committees to work together with purpose and consistency from the wards to the state capital.
