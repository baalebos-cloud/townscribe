**Minna / Agwara, Niger State – August 25, 2026** — Bandits have killed the District Head of Gallah, Alhaji Ahmed Yusuf, a respected traditional ruler under the Borgu Emirate in Niger State, in an attack on his palace that appears linked to his public stand against the gunmen.

Alhaji Ahmed Yusuf, also known as the Magaji Garin Gallah (or Magajin Garin Gallah) of Agwara Local Government Area, was reportedly targeted after he issued a direct warning to the bandits to stop infiltrating his community. 

Security analyst Bakatsine first disclosed the incident on X, noting that the monarch was killed during the raid on his palace over the weekend.



**Confirmed by Emirate and Local Authorities**

The Borgu Emirate confirmed the death in a statement issued on Sunday, August 23, 2026, by Ibrahim Toriq Abubakar. The statement mourned the loss of two prominent sons of the Emirate: Alhaji Ahmed Yusuf (Magajin Garin Gallah) and Alhaji Garba Alhassan (Tafidan Borgu).

*“The Borgu Emirate mourns the painful loss of two great sons and princes of the Emirate… Their passing is a great loss to their families, the traditional institution, and the entire people of Borgu,*” the statement read.

The office of the Chairman of Borgu Local Government Council also confirmed the killing, describing it as *“particularly painful and deeply disturbing.”*

The late district head was identified as a brother of the Minister of State for Agriculture and Food Security, Senator Aliyu Sabi Abdullahi.



**Context of Rising Insecurity in Borgu and Agwara**

The killing adds to a troubling pattern of violence in the Borgu and Agwara local government areas of Niger State. 

The region, part of the broader historical Borgu area spanning parts of Niger, Kwara, and Kebbi states as well as the Benin Republic border, has faced repeated attacks, abductions, and killings by armed groups in recent months and years. 

Communities have struggled with the presence of bandits and related groups operating in forested areas, including near the Kainji Lake National Park.

Traditional rulers who publicly resist or warn against the activities of these groups have increasingly become targets in parts of the North-Central and North-West regions.



**Calls for Stronger Security Response**

Details of the exact time of the attack, the number of gunmen involved, and whether there were additional casualties remain limited in initial reports. 

Security agencies have yet to issue a detailed public statement on the specific incident as of the latest available information.

Residents and local leaders in the affected areas continue to call for enhanced security presence, including more effective patrols and permanent deployments, to protect communities and traditional institutions that form the backbone of rural governance and social cohesion.

The death of Alhaji Ahmed Yusuf has left a void in Gallah and the wider Borgu Emirate. 

As the traditional institution and families mourn, the incident once again highlights the urgent need for coordinated efforts to restore peace and safety in Niger State’s vulnerable communities.
