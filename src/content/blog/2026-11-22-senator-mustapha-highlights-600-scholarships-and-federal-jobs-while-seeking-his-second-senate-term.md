**Ilorin, Nigeria—November 22, 2026**—Senator Saliu Mustapha, the Turaki of Ilorin Emirate and representative of Kwara Central in the National Assembly, on Sunday reaffirmed his commitment to purposeful representation during a meeting with members of the Balogun Gambari Wards Development Association (BAGWDA) at their secretariat in Akerebiata, Ilorin.

Addressing the gathering, the lawmaker, who also chairs the Senate Committee on Agriculture Production, Services and Rural Development, described his tenure as fruitful, with tangible achievements recorded across all four local government areas of the senatorial district—Ilorin West, Ilorin East, Ilorin South and Asa.

He pointed to the facilitation of federal employment opportunities for youths and the award of full university scholarships to 600 indigent but brilliant students as key highlights of his interventions. The scholarship scheme, which began with 200 beneficiaries and later expanded, has become one of the most visible symbols of his focus on human capital development. 

Mustapha stressed that these efforts, alongside other community-focused initiatives, have directly improved the lives of constituents.

*“My representation at the National Assembly over the years has been fruitful for Kwara Central,”* he told the association members. 

He assured them that a second term would allow him to build on the existing foundation and deliver even greater development and prosperity across the communities.

The senator also used the engagement to mobilize grassroots support. He urged BAGWDA members to carry the message into their respective communities, intensify mobilization for the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) and its candidates, and work towards securing victory in the forthcoming general elections.

The meeting forms part of Mustapha’s ongoing series of consultations with community groups, traditional institutions, traders, youths and professional bodies as the 2027 polls approach. 

Since defecting from the All Progressives Congress (APC) to the PDP in August 2026, the incumbent has intensified grassroots engagements, receiving endorsements from various segments of Kwara society while positioning himself as a candidate with a proven record of delivery.

Observers note that the Kwara Central senatorial race is expected to be closely watched, given the political realignments in the state. 

For Mustapha, the message remains consistent: results already delivered in education, employment and community development form the basis of his appeal for continuity.

As he continues to engage constituents, the senator maintains that purposeful representation must remain the standard by which public office holders are measured.

***This report is based on the senator’s official statement and publicly available records of his constituency interventions.***
