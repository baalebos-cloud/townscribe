**Makkah, Saudi Arabia—November 2, 2026**—President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has prayed that Nigeria’s rich ethnic, religious and cultural diversity becomes a source of prosperity, unity, peace, and sustainable development for all citizens.

The President offered the prayer on Sunday while hosting members of his delegation for dinner in the holy city of Makkah, where he is currently on the Umrah pilgrimage.



**Call for Unity and Shared Progress**

Tinubu expressed hope that the country’s diversity would continue to serve as a foundation for national advancement rather than division. 

He stressed the need for citizens to embrace mutual understanding, peaceful coexistence and a shared commitment to Nigeria’s progress.

*“May Almighty Allah bless Nigeria, convert our diversity into prosperity as a country,”* the President said.

He prayed for enduring peace, harmony, and prosperity, asking Allah to grant the nation’s leaders the wisdom, courage, and foresight to steer the country towards greater development and improved living conditions.

Tinubu also sought divine guidance and protection for Nigerians at home and abroad, calling for stronger bonds of brotherhood, a renewed spirit of patriotism, compassion, and service to the nation.



**Foundation for Inclusive Growth**

The President noted that Nigeria’s abundant human and natural resources, combined with the talents, creativity, and enterprise of its diverse population, provide a strong foundation for building a prosperous and inclusive nation.

The dinner offered an opportunity for fellowship and reflection among the delegation, which included state governors, senior government officials, and business leaders.

Kwara State Governor and Chairman of the Nigeria Governors’ Forum, AbdulRahman AbdulRazaq, commended Tinubu’s commitment to national development and pledged continued prayers for the President and the country.

BUA Group Chairman Abdulsamad Rabiu also reaffirmed support for the President, describing him as passionate about Nigeria’s development.

President Tinubu performed Umrah at the Grand Mosque in Makkah on Friday, 30 October, where he earlier prayed for the nation’s peace, stability, and prosperity.
