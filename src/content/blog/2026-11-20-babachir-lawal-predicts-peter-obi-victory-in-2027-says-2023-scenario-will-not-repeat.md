**Abuja, Nigeria—November 20, 2026**—Former Secretary to the Government of the Federation and Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) chieftain Babachir Lawal has restated his confidence that the party’s presidential candidate, Peter Obi, will win the 2027 general election, declaring that the circumstances of the 2023 presidential poll will not be repeated.

Speaking on Channels Television’s **Politics Today** on Friday, Lawal predicted a different political outcome, arguing that Obi would secure both the majority of votes and the required geographical spread this time.



**Challenge to 2023 Outcome**

Lawal questioned the 2023 result, claiming Obi obtained the highest number of votes but lacked sufficient spread, while then-candidate Bola Tinubu secured the spread and finished second. He maintained that a runoff should have followed.

*“Peter got the majority of votes; he didn’t get the spread. Bola Tinubu got the spread and came second. There should have been a runoff in that election,”* he said.



**State-by-State Projections**



The former SGF projected that Obi would win Kogi, Benue and Plateau states. He further claimed Obi would defeat former Vice President Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) in Atiku’s home state of Adamawa, where he expects Atiku to finish third.

*“I give it to you that Peter didn’t have more spread. This time, Peter will get the spread in Nigeria. Atiku will come third in Adamawa State this time around,”* Lawal added.

He argued that Atiku lacks the broad ethnic support needed to secure the presidency.



**Character and Competence**



Lawal highlighted what he described as Obi’s strengths, saying Nigerians would turn to the former Anambra governor because of his character and competence. 

He listed four qualities: incorruptibility, a detribalized outlook, understanding of market forces as a businessman, and reliability in keeping his word.

Lawal, who joined the NDC in September 2026 after leaving the ADC, has consistently backed the Obi-Kwankwaso ticket.

 He has previously described both candidates as enjoying widespread public goodwill and divine favour, while expressing confidence that the NDC has built the structures needed to convert popularity into electoral success.
