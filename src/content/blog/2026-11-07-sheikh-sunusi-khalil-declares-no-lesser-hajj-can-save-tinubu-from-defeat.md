**Kaduna, Nigeria—November 7, 2026**—Prominent Kaduna-based Islamic cleric Sheikh Sunusi Khalil has delivered one of his strongest rebukes yet against President Bola Tinubu, declaring that neither the recent Lesser Hajj (Umrah) in Saudi Arabia, food palliatives such as spaghetti distributions, nor any amount of government propaganda will rescue the president from what he described as an “imminent defeat” in the 2027 elections.

In a widely circulating video sermon shared on Saturday by political commentary accounts, the outspoken cleric stated, "*No amount of Lesser Hajj, spaghetti, or propaganda will save President Tinubu from imminent defeat. Nigerians have made up their minds to defeat him, even if he relocates to Saudi Arabia.”*

The remarks come just days after President Tinubu returned from a high-profile spiritual visit to Makkah, where he performed Umrah and was granted the rare privilege of praying inside the Holy Ka’abah. 

The president had used the trip to seek divine wisdom, resilience, and success for Nigeria, while also linking the pilgrimage to his political journey ahead of the January 2027 general elections.

Sheikh Khalil, who has previously warned Tinubu’s campaigners to stay away from the North because of deep public anger over economic hardship, framed the president’s Saudi trip as yet another attempt to distract citizens from the daily struggles of rising food prices, fuel costs, and insecurity. 

He argued that ordinary Nigerians have already made up their minds and will not be swayed by religious symbolism or short-term relief packages.

This is not the first time the cleric has publicly challenged the Tinubu administration. Earlier this year, he alleged that large sums of money had been earmarked to mobilize influential Islamic scholars and traditional rulers in support of the president’s re-election bid—a claim he said he personally rejected when approached. 

He has also repeatedly criticized the handling of insecurity in the North and the treatment of former Kaduna Governor Nasir El-Rufai.

Political observers note that Khalil’s latest intervention reflects a growing willingness among some northern religious voices to openly question the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) as the 2027 race intensifies. 

His language, which mixes sarcasm about “*spaghetti”* palliatives with outright rejection of religious optics, has quickly gained traction on social media platforms where opposition narratives are currently strong.

Supporters of the president are expected to dismiss the sermon as partisan political commentary from a known critic. 

However, the cleric’s consistent messaging on the cost-of-living crisis continues to resonate with segments of the northern population still grappling with the effects of subsidy removal, currency reforms, and persistent inflation.

As the countdown to the 2027 presidential election continues, statements like those from Sheikh Sunusi Khalil underline the depth of discontent that both the ruling party and opposition forces must navigate. 

Whether such voices will translate into organized political pressure remains to be seen, but for now, the cleric has made his position unmistakably clear: no spiritual journey, no food distribution, and no propaganda will change what he believes is already a settled public verdict against the president.



***This report is based on the circulating video sermon of Sheikh Sunusi Khalil shared on November 7, 2026, and previous public statements by the cleric.***
