A woman has been arrested in Kano State after allegedly stabbing her boyfriend and completely cutting off his manhood during a violent altercation in the Badawa area.

The Kano State Police Command confirmed the incident in a statement released on Monday, July 27, 2026. Officers said the female suspect was taken into custody following the attack, while the victim sustained serious injuries and is currently receiving medical treatment at a hospital.

According to the police, the assault occurred amid a heated dispute. Detectives have opened a full investigation to establish the exact circumstances that led to the brutal attack. 

“*The Kano State Police Command has arrested a female suspect for allegedly stabbing her boyfriend and completely cutting off his manhood during a violent altercation in Badawa,”* the statement read. 

Authorities added that the victim remains under medical care as inquiries continue.

The case has drawn attention as the latest in a series of similar domestic violence incidents reported across Nigeria in recent years. 

In May 2025, a 43-year-old woman identified as Gift was arrested in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, for allegedly biting off the tip of her partner’s penis during a dispute over sex. 

In October 2024, a 28-year-old woman, Yemisi Ogunlana, was detained in Ogun State after using a sharp object to cut her husband’s manhood during an argument. 

A June 2024 incident in Kaduna saw neighbours intervene before a newlywed woman could complete a similar attack on her husband.

Police have not released the names of the suspect or the victim in the Kano case, nor have they disclosed any possible motive beyond the violent confrontation itself. Investigations remain ongoing, and further details are expected as the probe advances.

The incident underscores ongoing concerns about domestic violence and the need for timely intervention in relationship disputes across the country. 

Kano State Police continue to urge residents to report conflicts early and seek peaceful resolution through proper channels.
