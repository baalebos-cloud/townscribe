**Abuja, Nigeria – July 24, 2026** — In a significant diplomatic gesture, President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has received a formal invitation from Saudi Arabia’s Crown Prince and Prime Minister, His Royal Highness Prince Mohammed bin Salman bin Abdulaziz, to attend the 10th edition of the prestigious Future Investment Initiative (FII) in Riyadh this October.

The announcement underscores deepening bilateral relations between Nigeria and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, particularly in economic cooperation, investment opportunities, and strategic partnerships.

Scheduled for October 26–29, 2026, at the King Abdulaziz International Conference Center in Riyadh, FII10 marks a major milestone as the summit celebrates its 10th anniversary under the theme “*The Power of Legacy.”*

The event has grown into one of the world’s leading global platforms, convening heads of state, top investors, innovators, policymakers, and business leaders to discuss the future of capital flows, technology, artificial intelligence, geopolitics, and sustainable growth. 

Organized by the FII Institute—a nonprofit foundation focused on “*Impact on Humanity"*—the summit emphasizes long-term, resilient strategies in an era of rapid change and uncertainty.

President Tinubu’s anticipated participation comes at a time when Nigeria is actively seeking foreign direct investment, diversification of its economy, and stronger ties with key Gulf partners. 

Saudi Arabia remains a strategic ally, with collaborations spanning energy, agriculture, infrastructure, and human capital development.

Attendance at FII events has historically provided African leaders with valuable networking opportunities to showcase investment prospects and attract capital for national development priorities.

This invitation reflects mutual interest in fostering legacy projects that drive inclusive growth, innovation, and cross-border cooperation — aligning with Nigeria’s ongoing economic reform agenda.

Over recent years, high-level engagements between Nigerian and Saudi leadership have intensified, covering areas such as petroleum, mining, religious tourism, and private sector investments. 

President Tinubu’s potential presence at FII10 could open new avenues for Nigerian businesses and entrepreneurs on the global stage.

As the October summit approaches, expectations are high for impactful announcements and partnerships that could benefit emerging markets like Nigeria.

This latest development highlights the role of multilateral forums in shaping Africa’s engagement with the Middle East’s dynamic investment landscape.
