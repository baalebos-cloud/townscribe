**Ibadan, Nigeria—October 31, 2026**—Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has approved about ₦4.6 billion in outstanding compensation for residents affected by Phase 1 of the 110-kilometre Rashidi Ladoja Circular Road project in Ibadan.

The Governor made the disclosure on Saturday after meeting some of the residents who were omitted during earlier compensation payments following the initial verification and valuation exercises.



**Addressing Omitted Beneficiaries**

Makinde informed the affected residents that the approved sum would cover all outstanding payments for those impacted by the Phase 1 development. 

He also gave a firm commitment that compensation for people affected by Phase 2 of the project would be paid before the end of 2026.

*“I informed them that we had already approved a payment of about N4.6B to cover all outstanding payments to those affected by the Phase 1 development. I also promised that compensation will be made to those affected by Phase 2 before the end of this year,”* the Governor stated in a video message shared on his official X account.



**Measures to Reduce Displacement**

The Governor restated an earlier assurance given to residents that densely populated areas along the project corridor would be fenced off at the 75-meter mark. 

This measure, he explained, is designed to reduce the number of people and properties affected by the construction of the important infrastructure project.

The Rashidi Ladoja Circular Road is a major road infrastructure initiative aimed at improving traffic flow and connectivity around Ibadan, the Oyo State capital.

Makinde’s intervention comes as his administration continues to engage directly with communities impacted by ongoing development projects, emphasizing transparency and timely compensation.
