**PARIS** — In a move that feels both inevitable and historic, Zinedine Zidane is poised to become the next head coach of the French national team, ending months of speculation and marking the beginning of a new chapter for Les Bleus. 

According to multiple credible reports and insider confirmations, the decision has been clear since November, with Zidane already assembling his backroom staff and formal documents expected to be signed this month.

Zidane will take charge after Didier Deschamps steps down following the 2026 World Cup, leading France through the Nations League, Euro 2028, and the 2030 World Cup. 

The 54-year-old football icon — a 1998 World Cup winner as a player and three-time Champions League champion as Real Madrid manager — has long been viewed as the natural successor to his former teammate Deschamps.

The French Football Federation (FFF) has maintained discretion out of respect for Deschamps’ 14-year tenure, but president Philippe Diallo has hinted that the successor was already known. 

Zidane, who has been out of management since leaving Real Madrid in 2021, reportedly turned down other high-profile opportunities — including club roles — to wait for this national team role.

He is said to admire the current playing style under Deschamps, particularly the freedom given to Kylian Mbappé in a creative No. 10 role, and plans to build around France’s golden generation of talents. 

Discussions have already begun on his coaching staff, with names like long-time assistant David Bettoni and even Fabien Barthez (for goalkeepers) linked to the setup.

For Zidane — often regarded as one of the greatest players of all time — the appointment carries immense weight. 

He inherits a team that has been a consistent powerhouse but will be expected to deliver major trophies. 

Supporters like Brazilian legend Ronaldo have called him the “perfect profile,” praising his tactical intelligence, character, and experience.

This transition comes at a pivotal moment for French football. Deschamps’ era delivered a World Cup title in 2018 and consistent excellence, but recent tournament exits have fueled calls for fresh ideas. 

Zidane’s return symbolizes continuity with France’s glorious past while injecting star power and tactical creativity.

As documents are finalized this month, excitement is building across the country. From the streets of Marseille — where Zidane’s journey began — to Paris and beyond, fans are dreaming of new triumphs under one of their own.

In football, few appointments carry this much symbolism. Zinedine Zidane is coming home to lead Les Bleus. The next golden era may just be beginning.
