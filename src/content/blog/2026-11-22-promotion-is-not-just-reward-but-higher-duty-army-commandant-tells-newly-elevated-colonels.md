**Makurdi, Nigeria—November 22, 2026**—Promotion in the Nigerian Army is not merely a reward for past service but a solemn call to greater responsibility, higher commitment, and exemplary leadership. 

That was the firm message delivered by the Commandant of the Nigerian Army School of Military Engineering (NASME), Brigadier General Sebhil Adekunle Adeyemo, during a decoration ceremony for newly elevated senior officers.

The ceremony, held on 20 November 2026 at the NASME Watermanship Site, North Bank, Makurdi, brought together officers from NASME and the Nigerian Army College of Environmental Science and Technology (NACEST). 

Seven officers were decorated with the rank of Colonel: Bekimbo Banimibo Brown, Ishaya Jollo Galaki, Hussaini Bazata Yusuf, Barnabas Wushina Gowok, Danjuma Saidu Yakubu, Dapo Adewa and Aminu Mohammed Jibo.

Addressing the gathering, Brigadier General Adeyemo expressed gratitude to the Chief of Army Staff, Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu, for finding the officers worthy of elevation. 

He congratulated the new Colonels and paid special tribute to their spouses for the patience, understanding, and sacrifices that had sustained their military careers.

*“Promotion should not be viewed merely as recognition of past service but as a renewed commitment to greater responsibilities and higher expectations,”* the Commandant stated. 

He urged the officers to justify the confidence placed in them through diligence, discipline, loyalty, integrity and professionalism. Quoting the familiar military adage, he reminded them that “*to whom much is given, much is expected.”*

The Rector of NACEST, Colonel Olalekan Dimeji Bamiteko, also congratulated the officers and encouraged them to remain intellectually alert and committed to continuous self-development. 

He advised that even if one loses the ability to speak, the capacity to think critically must never be lost.

Speaking on behalf of the newly promoted officers, Colonel Bekinbo Banimibo-Ofori Brown thanked the Chief of Army Staff, the Commandant, and the Rector for their leadership and mentorship. 

He also acknowledged the support of colleagues, families, and well-wishers who made the occasion memorable.

Senior officers, soldiers, family members, and invited guests, including the Commandant of the Nigerian Air Force School of Air Intelligence, witnessed the ceremony. 

The event underscored the Nigerian Army’s emphasis on professional growth, accountability and the weight of leadership that accompanies higher rank.



***This report is based on the official statement released by the Nigerian Army on 21 November 2026.***
