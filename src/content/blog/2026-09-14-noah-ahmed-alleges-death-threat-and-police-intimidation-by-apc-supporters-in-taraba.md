**Jalingo, Taraba State—September 14, 2026**—Taraba State governorship aspirant of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Hon. Noah Ahmed, has raised serious alarm over what he describes as a death threat and police intimidation by supporters of the All Progressives Congress (APC).

In a post shared on X on Monday, Ahmed appealed to Nigerians for prayers and financial support, stating he is currently enduring significant hardship. 

He recounted that the incident began when he visited a printing shop and encountered a man he identified as an APC supporter who followed him on the platform. 

According to Ahmed, the individual verbally attacked him and explicitly threatened to kill him.



*“Guys please Pray for me am going through a lot please I need your support, nothing is too small, I went to print this Guy is an APC Guy I never knew he followed me he on x he Attacked me saying he will kill me as a trainer person we did he called police for me to come Arrest,”* Ahmed wrote.

He further alleged that the man subsequently involved the police in an apparent attempt to have him arrested, framing the episode as part of broader political intimidation linked to his ambition to contest the Taraba governorship under the NDC in the 2027 general elections.

Ahmed, who presents himself as a youth-focused leader emphasizing integrity, transparency, education, agriculture, healthcare, and economic opportunities for Tarabans, has been actively mobilizing support ahead of the polls. 

His campaign materials highlight themes of bold leadership and a brighter future for the state.

The claims surface at a time of rising political tension across Nigeria as parties and aspirants intensify activities toward 2027. 

Similar reports of threats and intimidation involving political actors have emerged in other states, prompting calls for greater vigilance from security agencies and civil society.

As of the time of this report, there has been no independent verification of the alleged death threat, the identity of the individual involved, or any specific police action. 

The Taraba State Police Command has not issued a public statement on Ahmed’s allegations.

Ahmed’s appeal for public prayers and modest financial assistance underscores the personal pressures he says he is facing while pursuing his political goals. 

Political observers note that such incidents, if substantiated, could further heighten concerns about the climate for opposition candidates and the integrity of the electoral process in the lead-up to 2027.

Security agencies and relevant authorities are expected to treat any verified threats seriously to safeguard democratic participation and protect the rights of all aspirants.
