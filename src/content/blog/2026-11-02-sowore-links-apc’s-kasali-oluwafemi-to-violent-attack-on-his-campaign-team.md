**Lagos, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has accused an All Progressives Congress (APC) operative of planning and celebrating a violent attack on him and members of his team in Ado-Ekiti.

In a post on his X account on Monday, Sowore named Asiwaju Kasali Oluwafemi, identified as an APC operative and State Director of the pro-Tinubu City Boy Movement in Ekiti, as having issued threats against him since 2025.

*“They didn’t start planning this attack on Friday!”* Sowore wrote. *“This fellow, Asiwaju Kasali Oluwafemi, an @OfficialAPCNg operative, began issuing threats against me since 2025 long before the latest brutal attack on me and members of my team in Ado-Ekiti on Friday, October 30, 2026. As soon as we were attacked, he was openly celebrating the violence unleashed against us.”*

He shared screenshots, including what he described as the individual’s latest threat.



**Background of the October 30 Incident**

The attack occurred at Tawakalitu Restaurant in the Dalimore area of Ado-Ekiti shortly after Sowore completed a radio interview at MIDAS FM 90.5. 

Sowore and his associates sustained injuries. He has repeatedly alleged that the assailants were political thugs linked to the Ekiti State Government House and received support from the Department of State Services (DSS).

Police initially described the incident as an *“altercation”* and said Sowore sustained minor injuries. 

The Ekiti State Police Command later announced the arrest of 11 suspects and recovery of items including walkie-talkies, phones, and suspected charms. 

Sowore has rejected the police characterization, calling it a coordinated violent assault and demanding the release of CCTV footage, which he claims was erased after security agencies took custody of the equipment.

Eyewitnesses and AAC members have identified individuals allegedly involved, while Sowore has pointed to a phone stolen during the attack that was reportedly traced to the Governor’s Lodge.



**Calls for Investigation**

Sowore has demanded an independent probe, the removal of senior police officers he accuses of a cover-up, and accountability for those he believes sponsored the attack. The AAC has described the incident as an attempted assassination.

Kasali Oluwafemi, a Special Assistant on Media to Ekiti Governor Biodun Oyebanji and City Boy Movement director, has not issued a public response to the latest specific allegation at the time of this report. 

Previous social media activity linked to him has included strong criticism of opposition figures.

The incident has drawn condemnation from opposition figures and civil society groups, who have called for thorough and transparent investigations to protect political freedoms ahead of the 2027 elections.



***This report is based on statements by Omoyele Sowore and related coverage of the October 30, 2026 incident in Ado-Ekiti.***
