**Owerri, Nigeria—November 1, 2026**—Presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Peter Obi, has criticized President Bola Tinubu for failing to spend a single night in Imo State after more than three years in office, while noting that the President has spent over 260 days abroad.

Obi made the remarks during a town hall meeting in Owerri on October 31, 2026, as part of his ongoing series of engagements with Nigerians. 

He arrived from Yenagoa, Bayelsa State, and began his visit with a stop at a local market where he interacted with traders, buyers, and residents.



**Economic Hardship and National Challenges**

The former Anambra governor said the stories he heard at the market offered a sobering reminder of how difficult life has become for ordinary Nigerians under the current administration’s economic policies.

In his address, Obi highlighted that the government has accumulated enormous debts without delivering commensurate improvements in the lives of citizens. 

He noted that Nigeria is now ranked among the hungriest nations in the world, with over 140 million people living in multidimensional poverty. 

The country also remains one of the most terrorized nations globally, while approximately 18 million children are out of school.



**Message of Hope and Commitment**

Obi reassured the people of Imo State that all hope is not lost. He said he and his running mate, Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, are committed to securing and uniting the country, making massive investments in health and education, and lifting millions of Nigerians out of poverty.

*“Among the leading contenders, we believe we possess the experience, competence, capacity, commitment, and readiness required to undertake the difficult but necessary task of turning Nigeria around,”* he stated.

During the interactive session, questions were raised about the protection of votes. Obi encouraged citizens to come out in large numbers, exercise their democratic rights and remain vigilant until the final results are declared.

He thanked supporters, party candidates, members, organizers, and the people of Imo State for their warmth and overwhelming support, emphasizing that building a new Nigeria is a collective responsibility.



*“A New Nigeria is POssible, and Nigeria will be OK!”* he concluded.



***This report is based on the official statement released by Peter Obi on November 1, 2026.***
