**Jos, Plateau State—November 9, 2026**—The Nigerian Army has announced a comprehensive push to modernize its administrative systems through the full digitization of service records, automation of salaries and allowances, and the deployment of an integrated human resource management platform across all formations.

The initiative was disclosed by the Chief of Army Staff, Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu, at the opening of the Second Bi-Annual Nigerian Army Chief Clerks’ Convention 2026, held at the Headquarters of 3 Division in Jos, Plateau State. Represented by the General Officer Commanding 3 Division and Commander of Operation Enduring Peace, Major General Maxwell Dangana, the Army Chief said the reforms are designed to enhance transparency, efficiency, and accountability while giving commanders real-time access to accurate personnel data for better planning and decision-making.

**Focus on Digital Transformation**

Lieutenant General Shaibu stressed that accurate record-keeping, timely information flow, and personnel accountability are just as critical to operational success as firepower and military maneuvers. 

In today’s complex security environment marked by terrorism, banditry and emerging cyber threats, he noted, traditional manual systems are no longer sufficient.

The new digital framework will eliminate delays in salary payments, reduce bureaucratic bottlenecks, and minimize opportunities for errors or manipulation in personnel records. 

An integrated human resource platform is expected to streamline promotions, postings, welfare entitlements, and career management across the Army’s numerous formations nationwide.



**Five Strategic Pillars**

The Army Chief outlined five strategic pillars guiding the transformation agenda of his leadership: strengthening professionalism and discipline; enhancing administrative efficiency; improving training and operational readiness; deepening technological integration; and advancing personnel welfare.

He announced plans to build the capacity of the clerical cadre through specialized training programmes covering leadership, information management, digital administration, and operational documentation. 

These courses will be delivered at the Nigerian Army School of Finance and Administration and other relevant institutions.



**Welfare and Operational Impact**

On personnel welfare, Lieutenant General Shaibu reaffirmed the Army’s commitment to improved accommodation, healthcare, and fair administration of entitlements. 

He urged chief clerks to see themselves not merely as office administrators but as operational enablers whose work directly supports mission success in a joint and multi-agency security environment.

The five-day convention brought together chief clerks from across the Nigerian Army under the theme ***“Enhancing Clerical Effectiveness in Support of the Decision-Making Process in a Joint Security Environment.”*** 

Participants are expected to share best practices and align administrative processes with the Army’s broader modernization goals.

Defence analysts view the move as a significant step towards building a more agile, data-driven and accountable force. 

By placing accurate digital records at the centre of administration, the Nigerian Army aims to free up resources and attention for core operational duties while improving the welfare and morale of its personnel.

The reforms come at a time when the military continues to confront multifaceted security challenges across the country, making institutional efficiency and reliable data management more important than ever.



***This report is based on official statements from the Nigerian Army and coverage by Channels Television published on November 9, 2026.***
