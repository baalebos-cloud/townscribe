**Lagos, Nigeria | July 22, 2026** - In an era where celebrity relationships often fuel endless online speculation, Nigerian TikTok sensations Habeeb “Peller” Hamzat and Amadou Elizabeth “Jarvis” have turned the narrative toward commitment and transparency. 

The pair, known collectively by fans as #Jarpel, recently shared elegant pre-wedding photos while Jarvis emphatically declared on The Honest Bunch Podcast that infidelity has no place in their union — and never has.

The striking images capture the couple in timeless black-and-white and color moments: Jarvis radiant in a flowing polka-dot gown with bold black velvet accents and a pearl headpiece, Peller dapper in a sleek black outfit with suspenders, exchanging tender looks beside a classic white car amid lush greenery. 

The visuals perfectly blend modern romance with traditional elegance, offering fans a glimpse into their highly anticipated August 1 white wedding.

**From Content Creators to Committed Partners**

Peller and Jarvis’s journey has been anything but conventional. What began as collaborative TikTok skits and livestreams evolved into a deeply public romance marked by proposals, breakups, reconciliations, and cultural milestones. 

Their relationship gained massive traction in 2024–2025, surviving rumors, public spats, and even a staged fake pregnancy for content.

Key turning points include Peller’s romantic beach proposal to Jarvis in Ghana on June 3, 2026, followed by the payment of her bride price in Edo State during a vibrant traditional ceremony on June 21. 

Dressed in Edo cultural attire with coral beads, the couple formalized their union in a heartfelt family gathering that silenced many doubters.

Jarvis, addressing cheating allegations head-on in the recent podcast, stated unequivocally that Peller has *“never been unfaithful.”* 

She emphasized the spiritual foundation of their bond: *“If you cheat, you die. It’s the rule of the relationship... God is involved*.” 

Peller echoed her sentiments, questioning why he would stray from such a committed partnership. The couple even considered a dramatic “*blood oath*” ritual (inspired by viral TikTok trends) but ultimately opted against it.

**Asoebi Drama and Wedding Buzz**

True to their content-creator roots, the couple has kept fans engaged with wedding logistics. They revealed premium asoebi pricing — N350,000 for four yards, N700,000 for eight, and a eye-watering N1 million for 12 yards — during a lively livestream, sparking both excitement and debate about the opulent celebration.

Their story resonates widely in Nigeria’s youth and entertainment circles. Supporters praise their growth from viral sensations to a couple embracing tradition, prayer, and accountability.

 Critics, however, continue to question the pace given their young ages (Peller around 21–22, Jarvis slightly older) and the performative nature of their online presence.

Peller and Jarvis represent a new generation of digital natives blending influencer culture with real-life milestones. 

Their emphasis on fidelity, family involvement, and faith amid public scrutiny offers a refreshing counterpoint to typical celebrity drama. 

As they prepare for the white wedding, expectations are sky-high for a spectacle that will blend glamour, culture, and unfiltered emotion.
