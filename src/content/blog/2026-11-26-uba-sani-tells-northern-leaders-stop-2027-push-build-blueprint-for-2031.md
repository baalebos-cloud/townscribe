**Kaduna, Nigeria—November 26, 202**6—Kaduna State Governor Uba Sani on Thursday urged northern political leaders and stakeholders to abandon any agitation for the presidency in 2027 and instead begin developing a comprehensive development blueprint to prepare the region for a possible return to power in 2031.

Speaking at a one-day endorsement program organized by the Arewa Think Tank and civil society groups in support of President Bola Tinubu’s second-term ambition and other All Progressives Congress candidates, Sani declared his full support for the incumbent president.

He insisted the North must use the next four years to confront its developmental challenges and produce a practical governance agenda rather than demand power without readiness.

*“We in the North, are we really ready? Are we prepared? Are we serious? That is a question that we need to look at ourselves in the mirror,”* the governor said. 

He added: *“I told them, show me the blueprint. We need to start working for a blueprint today so that in 2031, when we want the power to pass back to the North, we are set to implement. But today, we are not ready yet because there is no blueprint as we are speaking.”*

Sani argued that the 2027 presidential election should not be decided by ethnic or regional identity. He criticised politicians who campaign solely on the basis of being northerners, describing such appeals as insufficient. 

*“Someone said, ‘Vote for me because I am a northerner.’ Does it make sense? No!*” he declared. The governor also declared the era of empty promises over, challenging former governors, vice presidents and other office holders seeking elective positions to account for their past achievements rather than rely on rhetoric.

He called on northern leaders to prioritize education, healthcare, infrastructure, agriculture, and economic empowerment through regional cooperation and evidence-based planning. 

Sani linked his support for Tinubu to concrete federal interventions in Kaduna and the wider North, including the approval of ₦178 billion for the long-demanded Mando-Birnin Gwari road, the Zaria-Soba-Pambegua and Jama’a-Kwai roads, education loan schemes, technical and vocational training programmes, and other social interventions. 

He also highlighted his administration’s deployment of 100 compressed natural gas buses that have recorded 5.2 million passenger trips and saved residents more than ₦5 billion.

The governor reiterated that without Tinubu’s support, many of the current successes in security, infrastructure and social services in Kaduna would not have been possible. 

He urged northern stakeholders to stop merely highlighting problems and instead offer practical solutions, insisting the region can achieve meaningful progress if leaders take responsibility.

The event, held at the Sir Ahmadu Bello Memorial Foundation conference hall in Kaduna and convened by Arewa Think Tank’s Muhammad Alhaji Yakubu, formed part of broader efforts by APC supporters in the North to mobilize for the 2027 elections while framing the region’s long-term political strategy around preparation rather than immediate contest.



***This report is based on statements by Governor Uba Sani at the Arewa Think Tank endorsement programme on November 26, 2026, as reported by Channels Television and other outlets.***
