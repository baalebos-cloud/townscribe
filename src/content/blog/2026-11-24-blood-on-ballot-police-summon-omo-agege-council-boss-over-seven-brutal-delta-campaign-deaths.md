**Asaba, Nigeria – November 24, 2026** –The Delta State Police Command has invited former Deputy Senate President, Senator Ovie Omo-Agege, Ughelli North Local Government Chairman Hon. Jaro Movudu Egbo, and five others for questioning over the killing of seven persons during a bloody campaign outing in Uduere community.

The invitation, announced on Tuesday by Police Public Relations Officer SP Bright Edafe, follows the gunfire that erupted on Monday during a campaign visit by Omo-Agege, the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) candidate for Delta Central Senatorial District in the 2027 general elections.

According to the police, the seven invitees are “key political actors and persons of interest” needed to establish their level of involvement. 

Omo-Agege is to report to the State Criminal Investigation Department (SCID), Police Headquarters Asaba, on Thursday, November 26, at 11am, while Egbo will appear the same day at 2pm.

The five others—Gowon Egoide, former Chairman of Uduere Quarters; Onoriode Oneya, incumbent Youth Chairman of Uduere; Edirin Wokode Ewhubare, associate from Otovwodo; Erukaye Egoide, his nephew; and Tony Gabon Mevowovo—are scheduled for Friday, November 27 at noon.

Commissioner of Police CP Samuel E. Erale warned the invitees to come alone, stressing the Command will not accept supporters. He assured the investigation will be thorough and impartial, adding anyone found culpable will face justice irrespective of status.

Governor Sheriff Oborevwori, accompanied by CP Erale and security chiefs, visited Uduere on Tuesday for on-the-spot assessment. The state has since imposed a dusk-to-dawn curfew on Ughelli North.

Conflicting narratives deepen the mystery. Police preliminary report puts deaths at six, with several injured. Omo-Agege’s camp initially reported four deaths, including aides Moses Anaughe and Akima Efemena Umukoro, describing it as an assassination attempt on the senator, who escaped unhurt. 

Egbo counter-accused Omo-Agege’s entourage of opening fire, claiming three of his supporters died.

The probe comes on Inspector-General of Police orders amid rising political violence. It also coincides with a Federal High Court Warri judgment on Tuesday nullifying Omo-Agege’s NDC ticket over membership register issues—a ruling he has vowed to appeal.
