**Ado Ekiti, Ekiti State June 15, 2026** - Ambassador Oluwadare Bejide has reiterated his determination to fulfil every promise contained in his campaign manifesto, assuring residents that his administration, if elected, will be driven by accountability, transparency, and a genuine commitment to improving the welfare of the people.

The governorship aspirant gave the assurance during a strategic engagement with members of the Better Life Landlords Association in Ado Ekiti, where he unveiled key aspects of his development agenda and interacted directly with residents on issues affecting their communities.

The meeting, which attracted members of the association and other stakeholders, provided a platform for Bejide to outline his vision for economic growth, infrastructure development, security, youth empowerment, and social welfare. 

He stressed that leadership must go beyond making promises during election campaigns and should be measured by the ability to translate commitments into meaningful results that positively impact the lives of citizens.

Addressing the gathering, Bejide said the foundation of good governance rests on service, integrity, and responsible management of public resources. 

According to him, government must remain focused on projects and policies that directly address the needs of the people rather than pursuing initiatives that offer little value to ordinary citizens.

He noted that communities across the state deserve a government that listens to their concerns and responds with practical solutions. 

Bejide pledged that under his leadership, the voices of residents would play a central role in decision-making processes, ensuring that development is inclusive and beneficial to all segments of society.

The governorship hopeful further emphasized that campaign promises should never be treated as mere political slogans designed to win votes. 

Rather, he said, they represent a social contract between leaders and the people, one that must be honoured through concrete actions and measurable achievements.

The interactive session also gave members of the Better Life Landlords Association the opportunity to raise pressing concerns ranging from infrastructure deficits and security challenges to economic hardships affecting families and businesses. 

In response, Bejide assured participants that their concerns would not only be heard but would also form part of the priorities of his administration.

Many members of the association expressed optimism about the vision presented by the governorship candidate, describing his message as one that resonates with the aspirations of ordinary citizens seeking credible and people-centred leadership.

The visit ended on a positive note, with participants commending the engagement and expressing hope for a future anchored on accountability, sustainable development, and responsive governance. 

Political observers say the interaction further reflects the growing momentum around Bejide’s campaign as he continues to reach out to various groups and communities in his quest to earn public support ahead of the election.
