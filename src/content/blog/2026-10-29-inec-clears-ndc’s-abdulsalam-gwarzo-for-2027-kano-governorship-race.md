**Abuja, Nigeria—October 29, 2026**—The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has cleared the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) governorship candidate in Kano State, Abdulsalam Gwarzo, to contest the 2027 election, subject to the outcome of pending court proceedings.

An INEC source confirmed the decision after the commission’s meeting on Thursday, stating that the electoral body has no objection to Gwarzo’s candidacy but that the courts could still determine his final eligibility.



**Background to the Clearance**

Gwarzo’s name, along with that of his running mate Rabiu Mustapha, was earlier omitted from INEC’s final list of governorship candidates for Kano. 

The omission prompted the NDC candidate to file an originating summons dated October 20, 2026, asking the court to declare the exclusion unlawful and compel INEC to recognize their ticket.

INEC had attributed some omissions to alleged non-compliance with Sections 77(5) to (7) of the Electoral Act 2026, which require political parties to submit membership registers. 

A September 24, 2026 Supreme Court judgment had affirmed the constitutionality of those provisions.



**Conditional Approval**

According to the source, Gwarzo’s clearance remains conditional. *“Because of the court case, the court may rule otherwise. So his approval is subject to whatever the court finally decides,” t*he official said, while emphasizing that INEC itself has no issues with him contesting.

The commission is expected to issue a formal statement detailing its decisions on various petitions submitted by political parties over candidates omitted from the lists ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Gwarzo, a former deputy governor of Kano State and ally of Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, had emerged as the NDC flagbearer following recent political realignments in the state.
