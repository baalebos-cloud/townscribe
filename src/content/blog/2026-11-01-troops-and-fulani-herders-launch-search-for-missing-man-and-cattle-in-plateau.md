**Jos, Plateau State—November 1, 2026**—Anxiety has gripped communities in Mangu Local Government Area of Plateau State following the disappearance of a Fulani herder and 33 cattle, prompting a joint search operation by troops of Operation Enduring Peace and local herdsmen.

Security sources revealed that the incident was reported at about 8:45 a.m. on Saturday, October 31, 2026, at Gyambwas Rigi. 

The missing herder had left with the cattle for grazing towards the boundary with Barkin Ladi Local Government Area but failed to return.

Troops of Sector 8, Sub-Sector 81, who are deployed at Gyambwas, immediately swung into action. Working side by side with local Fulani herders, the soldiers conducted extensive search operations in the direction the herder was believed to have taken. 

Despite their efforts, no contact had been established with the missing man or the livestock as of the latest report.

The cattle owner, identified as Alhaji Shehu, has been actively involved in the ongoing search. Community leaders in the general area have also been contacted and urged to provide any information that could assist in locating the herder and recovering the animals.

Military sources emphasized that troops remain fully engaged in the operation. They are determined to establish the whereabouts of the missing herder and ensure the safe recovery of the 33 cattle. 

The collaborative approach between security forces and the herding community underscores efforts to maintain peace and address security concerns promptly in the volatile region.



Plateau State has experienced recurring farmer-herder tensions and security challenges in recent months. Operations such as this highlight the continuous presence of Operation Enduring Peace personnel working to protect lives, property, and livelihoods across affected communities.



Residents and stakeholders have been called upon to remain vigilant and share any useful leads with security agencies. Authorities continue to monitor the situation closely while the search intensifies.



The joint effort reflects a growing recognition that lasting solutions to security issues in Plateau require cooperation between troops, traditional leaders, and the various communities that share the land.

As the search continues, hope remains that the missing herder will be found unharmed and the cattle safely recovered. Security operatives have assured the public that no effort will be spared until the matter is resolved.



***This report is based on information provided by security sources through Zagazola Makama on November 1, 2026.***
