**LAGOS, Nigeria** — In a recent interview on ARISE News, public policy analyst and development expert Abba Kaka offered glowing remarks on the performance of Vice President Kashim Shettima, emphasizing his unwavering loyalty, intellectual depth, and commitment to assigned responsibilities under President Bola Tinubu.

Kaka, who appeared on the channel, stressed that the role of a Vice President inherently involves executing the President’s directives. 

*“When you are the Vice President, your responsibility is to carry out the assignments the President gives you. Whatever the President entrusts to you becomes your job,”* he stated.

He went further to describe Shettima as *“a man of immense knowledge, a reservoir of ideas,”* highlighting the Vice President’s energy, passion, and dedication in fulfilling his duties. 

According to Kaka, Shettima has actively represented the President across various parts of the country, demonstrating reliability and effectiveness in high-level national assignments.

Vice President Kashim Shettima, a former Governor of Borno State and Senator, has been a key figure in the Tinubu administration since 2023. 

His retention as running mate for potential future contests and active involvement in diplomatic, economic, and security-related engagements have been subjects of public and political commentary.

Analysts like Kaka often point to Shettima’s background in academia, banking, and politics as assets that bring intellectual rigor and practical experience to the executive branch. 

His travels and representations on behalf of the President—ranging from climate summits to domestic stakeholder engagements—underscore a hands-on approach to governance support.

The endorsement comes at a time when discussions about the 2027 presidential elections and the future of the APC ticket are gaining momentum. 

Praise for Shettima’s loyalty and competence contrasts with ongoing national debates over economic reforms, security challenges, and leadership performance.

While supporters view Shettima as a stabilizing and capable deputy who has justified his selection, critics in Nigeria’s polarized political landscape frequently question the administration’s overall direction. 

Kaka’s comments, however, focus squarely on the Vice President’s personal attributes and execution of duties rather than broader policy outcomes.

Public reactions to such assessments often reflect Nigeria’s deeply divided political opinions. 

Some see them as timely affirmations of teamwork within the executive, while others call for more tangible results in addressing inflation, insecurity, and youth unemployment — issues that continue to dominate public discourse. 

Interviews like this on platforms such as ARISE News play an important role in shaping narratives around leadership and governance. 

Abba Kaka’s perspective as a public policy analyst adds an informed layer to conversations about what effective vice-presidential support looks like in Nigeria’s complex federal system.

As the country navigates economic reforms and prepares for future electoral cycles, the emphasis on loyalty, knowledge, and dedication from figures like Shettima remains central to how the administration projects itself. 

Whether this praise translates into stronger public confidence or fuels further scrutiny will likely unfold in the months ahead.

For now, Kaka’s words paint a picture of a Vice President fully immersed in his role—energetic, idea-rich, and steadfastly aligned with the President’s vision. 

In the high-stakes arena of Nigerian politics, such endorsements carry weight in ongoing discussions about leadership continuity and national direction.
