**Abuja, Nigeria—October 31, 2026**—President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has welcomed Airtel Nigeria’s Open Network initiative, describing it as a positive step towards greater transparency, consumer protection and improved telecommunications services across the country.

In a statement released via the official Presidency handle on Saturday, the President said the initiative aligns with his administration’s economic reforms aimed at building a stronger, more productive, and prosperous Nigeria where businesses can invest, grow and create opportunities for citizens.



**Commendation for Network Expansion**

Tinubu specifically commended Airtel’s continued investment in telecommunications infrastructure. The company has added more than 1,050 new network sites during its 2025/26 financial year, including 657 fifth-generation (5G) sites. 

This expansion has grown Airtel’s infrastructure from approximately 15,000 sites two years ago to more than 17,000 nationwide.

*“This is the kind of investment we are working to attract,*” the President stated. “*Better connectivity means more opportunities for businesses, greater access to education, and more Nigerians participating in our growing digital economy.”*



**Government Measures and Expectations**

On the government’s part, Tinubu noted that the administration is strengthening regulatory oversight, protecting consumers, and expanding digital access. This includes the provision of free daily data for eligible students to access educational resources.

He, however, charged telecommunications operators to match these investment opportunities with better network coverage, fewer service disruptions, and improved services, particularly in underserved communities.



*“Our prosperity drive must reach every part of Nigeria,*” the president emphasized.

The Open Network initiative, launched in Lagos on Friday, October 30, 2026, allows subscribers to access information about network developments in their areas and report service difficulties directly to the operator. 

The launch was attended by the Executive Vice Chairman of the Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC), Dr. Aminu Maida.

Tinubu recalled his earlier meeting with Airtel Group Chairman Sunil Bharti Mittal and reaffirmed the Federal Government’s commitment to creating a favourable environment for private-sector investment in the digital economy.
