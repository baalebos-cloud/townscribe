**Abuja, Nigeria—October 31, 2026**—President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has commended the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) after the United States Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) praised the agency’s critical role in a major international operation targeting sextortion of minors.



In a letter to EFCC Chairman Ola Olukoyede, FBI Director Kash Patel acknowledged the Commission’s contribution to Operation Artemis, a joint investigation that dismantled criminal networks involved in the sexual exploitation and extortion of children.

The operation resulted in the arrest and extradition of two Nigerian suspects, Adebola Festus Adekunle and Mudasiru Afeez Olawale, in August 2026. Both men now face federal charges in the United States.

“The successful outcome of these cases would not have been possible without your assistance and dedication to international law enforcement collaboration,” Patel wrote.



**Presidential Commendation**

President Tinubu described the FBI’s recognition as further evidence of Nigeria’s progress in fighting financial crimes and deepening cooperation with global partners.

*“This is the kind of institutional performance we are determined to build across our country. I commend the EFCC Chairman and his team for their professionalism and dedication,”* the President said.

“*Nigeria must never become a haven for criminals who exploit innocent people, whether at home or abroad. Our law enforcement and anti-corruption institutions have my full support to pursue offenders and bring them to justice in accordance with the law.”*



**Broader Context of Reforms**

The commendation comes against the backdrop of growing international recognition of Nigeria’s anti-corruption efforts. 

These include the country’s removal from the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) grey list in October 2025 and from the European Union’s high-risk financial jurisdictions list in January 2026.

Tinubu said these milestones reflect his administration’s commitment to strengthening financial integrity, restoring global confidence, and creating a safer environment for legitimate business and investment.

*“Our fight against corruption is not measured by publicity but by results. Stronger institutions, effective law enforcement, and international cooperation are essential to building a Nigeria where honest enterprise can thrive and criminals are held accountable,*” he stated.

The President urged the EFCC and other security agencies to sustain their momentum, assuring them of continued presidential support in protecting citizens and elevating Nigeria’s standing among international partners.



***This report is based on the official State House press release issued by Special Adviser to the President on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, on October 31, 2026.***
