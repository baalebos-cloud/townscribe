### **Osogbo—November 25, 2026—Governor** Ademola Adeleke of Osun State has dissolved the State Executive Council, relieving all commissioners and political appointees of their duties just two days to his second-term inauguration.

The decision, announced on Wednesday through his spokesperson, Olawale Rasheed, comes ahead of the inauguration ceremony fixed for Thursday, November 27, 2026, in Osogbo.

According to the statement shared on X by @Osun_State_Gov and reported by TheCable, which garnered 1,387 views within hours, the dissolution does not affect everyone.

The Secretary to the State Government, SSG, the Chief of Staff, and the governor’s media aide/spokesperson are expressly exempted and will remain in office to guarantee a seamless transition.

Similarly, the Local Government Service Commission, the recently reconstituted governing board of the Osun State University, and other statutory agency boards are to remain intact and undissolved.

Adeleke, first sworn in November 2022 as the sixth elected governor of Osun State and re-elected in August 2026, described the move as the symbolic end of one chapter and the beginning of a new phase.

He commended members of the outgoing council for their selfless service, commitment, and integrity, saying they played a critical role in implementing policies that delivered visible achievements.

Those achievements, he noted, cut across education, healthcare, road infrastructure, sports development, science and technology, digital economy, and climate change—sectors where his administration claims to have set new records.

*“I sincerely appreciate all members of the outgoing council for their selfless service, commitment, and integrity. You have played important roles in the growth and development of our dear state, and your contributions will remain part of the golden era of Osun’s development,”* Adeleke said.

*“The secretary to the state government, the chief of staff and the spokesperson to the governor are exempted from the dissolution.* 

*The Local Government Service Commission and the recently reconstituted governing board of Osun State University and other statutory agency boards also remain undissolved. To all affected appointees, I thank you once again for serving our people with dedication.* 

*Together, we have served. Together, we have achieved. And together, we shall continue to build a greater Osun.”*

The governor has in recent engagements, vowed to sustain people-oriented governance in his second term, pledging better welfare for citizens, more jobs, expanded infrastructure, and deeper social protection.

Analysts view has,dissolution as both constitutional routine and political strategy—freeing Adeleke to reward loyalty, drop underperformers, and inject fresh energy for the final four years.

As Osogbo lights up for Friday’s inauguration, residents wait pensively: who returns, who leaves, and can the dancing governor outdance his first-term record?
