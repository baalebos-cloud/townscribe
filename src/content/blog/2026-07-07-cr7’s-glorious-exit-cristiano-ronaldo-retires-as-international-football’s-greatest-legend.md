**LISBON, Portugal** — In an emotional farewell befitting one of football’s all-time greats, Cristiano Ronaldo has confirmed his retirement from international duty following Portugal’s campaign at the 2026 FIFA World Cup. 

The 41-year-old superstar, whose career has defined an era, steps away as a living legend with a trophy cabinet and statistical dominance that may never be equaled.

A widely shared tribute highlighted Ronaldo’s extraordinary achievements: most international goals (146), most international appearances (233), most World Cup goals for Portugal (11), the only player to score in six different FIFA World Cups, and most goals at the Euros (14). 

The post captured the global sentiment: “*Cristiano bows out of international football as a living legend.”*

Accompanying the tribute was a powerful image of Ronaldo standing on the pitch at a packed World Cup stadium, hands on hips, gazing upward in quiet reflection amid roaring crowds — a fitting visual for a player who has thrilled billions over two decades.

Ronaldo made his international debut for Portugal in 2003 and quickly became the face of the national team. He captained Portugal to victory in the 2016 UEFA European Championship — their first major trophy — and led them to the 2019 UEFA Nations League title. 

His longevity is legendary: at 41, he remained a key figure in Portugal’s 2026 World Cup squad, scoring crucial goals while mentoring younger talents.

His records speak volumes. Ronaldo’s 146 international goals make him the all-time leading scorer in men’s football history. 

He has appeared in a record six World Cups, scoring in each, and holds the Euros goal record. Beyond numbers, his work ethic, leadership, and clutch performances have inspired generations.

Ronaldo addressed fans and media ahead of Portugal’s knockout stages, expressing gratitude and emphasizing enjoyment in his final tournament. 

While confirming this World Cup as his last on the global stage, he left the exact timing of full international retirement open, saying he would decide when the moment feels right.

The football world reacted with an outpouring of respect. Fans, teammates, and rivals flooded social media with tributes, celebrating not just the goals but Ronaldo’s transformation of the game through professionalism and ambition. 

From his early days at Sporting CP to stardom at Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al-Nassr, and Portugal, CR7 redefined what’s possible for a forward.

Portugal’s campaign in 2026 carried extra weight as fans prepared for life after Ronaldo. 

His potential absence will leave a massive void, but his influence will endure through academies, young players he has mentored, and a generation that grew up wanting to “be like Ronaldo.”

As the curtain falls on his international chapter, Ronaldo leaves with a clear conscience, having given everything to the game he loves. 

His story — from Madeira to global icon — remains one of football’s greatest triumphs of talent, resilience, and relentless drive.

Thank you, Cristiano. The pitch will never be the same.
