**Los Angeles, November 2, 2026**—Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass has seen her chances of securing a second term surge to 58 percent on the prediction market Polymarket, according to data highlighted Monday evening.

The sharp rise comes just one day before voters head to the polls for the November 3 runoff election between Bass and City Councilmember Nithya Raman.



**Race Dynamics**

Bass, the incumbent, advanced from the June primary alongside Raman after neither candidate secured a majority. 

Early polls and market odds had at times favored Raman, with the progressive councilmember briefly holding a clear lead. 

A late October UC Berkeley Institute of Governmental Studies poll co-sponsored by the Los Angeles Times showed the two candidates tied at 40 percent among likely voters, with nearly one in five still undecided.

Traders on Polymarket have since shifted, pricing Bass as the favorite at 58 percent while Raman sits near 42 percent. The market has seen millions of dollars in trading volume as the race tightened in the final stretch.



**Key Issues and Context**

The contest has centered on homelessness, housing affordability, public safety, and the city’s preparation for the 2028 Olympics. 

Bass has defended her record and emphasized experience, while Raman has positioned the race as a referendum on the status quo, drawing support from progressive groups.

Bass’s improved standing in recent weeks has been linked in part to gains among voters who backed third-place primary candidate Spencer Pratt, as well as shifts among Black, Latino and San Fernando Valley voters.

With Election Day tomorrow, the race remains one of the most closely watched municipal contests in the country. 

Prediction markets have fluctuated repeatedly in recent weeks, reflecting the competitiveness of the matchup between the veteran mayor and her younger challenger.
