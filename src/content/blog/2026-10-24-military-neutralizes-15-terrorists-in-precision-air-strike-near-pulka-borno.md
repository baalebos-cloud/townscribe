**Maiduguri, Nigeria—October 24, 2026**—Troops of the Joint Task Force (North East), Operation HADIN KAI, have neutralized 15 terrorists in a precision air strike conducted north-west of Pulka in Gwoza Local Government Area of Borno State.

The operation took place at about 11:50 p.m. on Friday, 23 October 2026, according to a statement issued on Saturday by Captain Mohammed Goni, Acting Military Information Officer of Headquarters Joint Task Force (North East) Operation HADIN KAI.



**Intelligence-Led Success**

The military said the strike followed credible intelligence and confirmed sightings of terrorist movements in the general area of Pulka. 

After the air assault, ground troops moved in to exploit the location and consolidate the gains recorded.

Captain Goni noted that the successful engagement highlights the effectiveness of intelligence-driven targeting and coordinated military action in disrupting terrorist movements and degrading their operational capabilities.

*“It also reflects the unwavering commitment of Operation HADIN KAI to denying terrorist groups freedom of action and preventing them from threatening communities within the theatre,”* the statement added.



**Sustained Offensive Posture**

The operation forms part of the sustained offensive posture of troops, supported by effective intelligence gathering and the coordinated use of available military capabilities. 

Through these efforts, the Theatre Command continues to maintain pressure on terrorist elements, disrupt their activities, and strengthen the security environment across the North East.

Operation HADIN KAI remains resolute in its mission to defeat terrorism and insurgency, protect law-abiding citizens, and create the conditions necessary for the restoration of normalcy and socio-economic activities in affected communities.

The Theatre Command reassured the public that troops will sustain the momentum of ongoing operations with vigilance, determination, and professionalism. 

It also called on residents to continue cooperating with security agencies by providing timely and credible information on suspicious movements and activities.

The continued partnership between the military and the civilian population remains essential to consolidating operational gains and achieving lasting peace and security across the North East.
