**Abuja, Nigeria—November 20, 2026**—President Bola Ahmed Tinubu on Thursday received Honourable Abdulmumin Jibrin Kofa, the member representing Kiru/Bebeji Federal Constituency of Kano State, in audience at the State House.

The lawmaker, who currently chairs the House Committee on Housing and Habitat, presented copies of his newly published 572-page book, ***Economic Diversification and Structural Transformation in Nigeria: The Past, the Present, and the Future*.** 

The President, who wrote the foreword to the publication, commended Jibrin’s deep understanding of Nigeria’s economic transformation and the thorough research behind the work.



**Book Launch and New Initiatives**

The book was officially launched on Tuesday at the State House Conference Centre in Abuja. Speaker of the House of Representatives, Rt. Hon. Tajudeen Abbas, represented President Tinubu at the event. 

The launch also featured the unveiling of two strategic centres under the Abdulmumin Jibrin Kofa Foundation—the Global Centre for Policy Implementation and Sustainable Development and the Global Centre for Solid Minerals and Natural Resources Development—as part of activities marking the lawmaker’s 50th birthday.

President Tinubu described the publication as a valuable contribution to national economic discourse. He noted that Jibrin’s legislative experience, particularly in finance and appropriation matters, had enriched the research, which documents Nigeria’s historical efforts at economic diversification alongside the current administration’s reforms aimed at structural transformation.

*“These noble initiatives are in consonance with our commitment towards the documentation of researched knowledge for the benefit of citizens and humanity who seek to understand the nation’s continued effort at economic diversification.* 

*They are also in tandem with our commitment to the upliftment of the citizenry through the acquisition of necessary knowledge and skill sets for improved life and prosperity,”* the President said.



**Lawmaker’s Commitment**



In a statement following the meeting, Jibrin expressed appreciation for the opportunity to engage the President. 

He said the book and the newly established centres reflect his commitment to contributing to national development through research, evidence-based policy, and the strategic development of Nigeria’s natural resources.

The lawmaker, a former chairman of the House Committees on Finance and Appropriations and a former Executive Director of the Federal Housing Authority, holds dual PhDs in International Relations and Economics.

The meeting underscores ongoing conversations around economic diversification as the administration continues to pursue reforms aimed at building a more resilient and productive Nigerian economy.

*This report is based on official statements from the Presidency and the lawmaker’s account of the engagement*
