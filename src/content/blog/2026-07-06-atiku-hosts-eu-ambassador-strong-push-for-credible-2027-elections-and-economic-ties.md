**ABUJA, Nigeria**— In a significant display of diplomatic engagement, Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) Presidential Candidate for the 2027 general elections, hosted His Excellency Ambassador Gautier Mignot, Head of the European Union Delegation to Nigeria, at his residence in Abuja this afternoon.

The meeting, described by Atiku as an honour, focused on issues of mutual interest between Nigeria and the European Union. 

Discussions placed strong emphasis on the upcoming general elections, with Atiku expressing deep appreciation for the EU’s consistent support for democratic processes in Africa’s most populous nation.

Atiku commended the European Union’s ongoing dedication to promoting free, fair, peaceful, and credible elections. 

The engagement underscores the importance both sides place on strengthening electoral integrity as Nigeria prepares for the 2027 polls. 

This comes at a time when international observers continue to play a vital role in bolstering public confidence in the democratic process.

Beyond politics, the conversation highlighted opportunities to deepen economic cooperation. 

Atiku reiterated his vision for a stronger, more productive partnership that can drive sustainable growth, job creation, and development — aligning with broader efforts to attract quality foreign investment into Nigeria.

Ambassador Mignot’s visit reflects the EU’s active interest in Nigeria’s political and economic trajectory. 

Such high-level engagements are viewed as encouraging signs of international support for transparent governance and economic reforms at a critical juncture.

For Atiku Abubakar, the meeting reinforces his long-standing advocacy for credible elections and collaborative international partnerships. 

As the 2027 election season gathers momentum, this productive dialogue highlights a shared desire for stability, accountability, and progress.

In a political climate often marked by tension, today’s engagement stands out as a beacon of constructive statesmanship — one that prioritizes Nigeria’s democratic future and economic potential on the global stage.
