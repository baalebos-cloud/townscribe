Former Big Brother Naija star Alex Unusual has drawn a firm line under a heated social media argument after telling a persistent critic she would no longer engage with her. 

The exchange, which began over a simple request for an English translation of an Igbo video, quickly turned personal and has since gone viral across Nigerian entertainment platforms.

The drama started when Alex commented *“English translation”* under a video in which speakers shared stories in the Nsukka dialect of Igbo, with subtitles also written in Igbo. 

Her intention, she later clarified, was not that she herself needed the translation but that non-Igbo speakers or those who cannot read the dialect should be able to follow the content.

A woman who identified herself as a former schoolmate from their university days in the Akintola/Akpabio hostel quickly challenged her. 

The critic questioned why an Igbo girl from Nsukka would ask for an English version and suggested Alex was being pretentious. What began as a linguistic disagreement soon escalated into personal attacks. 

The woman dug into Alex’s past, referencing their time in school, mocking her distinctive green hair and hairstyles, and making remarks about her associations with male students while she studied Agriculture.

Alex responded firmly. She stated that simply knowing someone from school is not an achievement and that many people had known her both during and after her university days because she had always been popular. 

She also made it clear that she did not know the woman personally and that watching her from a hostel window or reading about her in magazines did not give anyone the right to jump to conclusions or speak to her disrespectfully.

As the back-and-forth grew more heated, Alex described the woman as a “troll” and told her to stop typing nonsense simply because she claimed familiarity from the past. In her final message on the thread, she announced that she would not respond to the critic again, effectively ending the conversation.

Screenshots of the exchange were widely shared by entertainment platforms, including YabaLeftOnline, sparking mixed reactions online. Some users praised Alex for standing her ground and refusing to be dragged further into unnecessary conflict. 

Others felt the original comment requesting a translation could have been phrased more carefully, while a number of commenters pointed out that asking for English subtitles on content meant for a wider audience is reasonable, especially in a multilingual country like Nigeria.

The incident is the latest reminder of how quickly online interactions can turn toxic, particularly for public figures. Alex Unusual, who rose to fame during Big Brother Naija Season 3 and later returned for All Stars, has faced her share of online criticism over the years. 

She has previously spoken about cyberbullying and the need for people to focus on their own lives rather than monitoring others.

In a separate post around the same period, she also shared a reflective message comparing life to a football match, noting that while spectators are necessary, *“spectatorship should never become a life’s purpose.”* 

The comment appeared to speak to the same theme of people investing too much energy in criticizing others.

For now, Alex has chosen to disengage from this particular argument. Whether the critic continues elsewhere or moves on remains to be seen, but the reality star’s decision to walk away has been welcomed by many who believe not every online confrontation deserves endless energy.

The episode once again highlights the challenges public figures face when even a well-intentioned comment can open the door to personal attacks rooted in old schoolyard familiarity or long-held resentment.
