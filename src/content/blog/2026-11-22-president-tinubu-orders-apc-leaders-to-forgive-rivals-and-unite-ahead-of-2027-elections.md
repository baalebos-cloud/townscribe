**Lagos, Nigeria—November 22, 2026**—President Bola Ahmed Tinubu on Sunday called on All Progressives Congress (APC) leaders to forgive one another, set aside personal differences, and strengthen party cohesion ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Speaking at an Islamic prayer session organized in his honor by his extended family at the Yoruba Tennis Club in Lagos, the President specifically directed his Special Adviser on Political Matters, Alhaji Ibrahim Kabiru Masari, and former Defence Minister Alhaji Abubakar Badaru to pursue reconciliation.

Apparently responding to reports of a quarrel between Katsina State Governor Dikko Umar Radda and Masari, Tinubu instructed the adviser: 

*“Alhaji Kabiru Masari, go back home and reconcile with everybody. That is the President’s order.* 

*When you finish that, you move to Jigawa. You move to other places and reconcile them, beg them because we have to reconcile and every anger must dissipate and evaporate.”* He issued the same directive to Badaru, the former Jigawa governor.

The President appealed to all APC leaders to work collectively to consolidate the gains of his administration. He recalled the severe hardships of the 2023 campaign period, including cash scarcity and fuel shortages, and attributed his victory to divine intervention, quoting the Quranic phrase “Kun faya kun” (Be, and it becomes).

Tinubu reminded supporters of his 2022 *“Emilokan”* (It is my turn) declaration and declared it was now *“Eyinlokan” (It is your turn) to secure his re-election. He urged Lagosians to obtain their Permanent Voter Cards and turn out massively on election days in January and February 2027, stressing the need to “generate enough votes to overwhelm the opponents.”*

The gathering was attended by governors of Lagos, Kaduna, Kwara, Ogun and Imo states, National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu, Chief of Staff Femi Gbajabiamila; and senior clerics from both Muslim and Christian faiths. 

Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu praised the President’s commitment to justice and inclusive governance, while Badaru called on Northern voters to reciprocate Tinubu’s past support for the late President Muhammadu Buhari. 

Ninety-four-year-old elder statesman Alhaji Lateef Femi Okunnu also pledged his support, citing infrastructure projects and economic reforms.

The President reaffirmed his determination to provide purposeful leadership, promote national unity and build a prosperous Nigeria for all citizens.



***This report is based on the official State House press release issued on November 22, 2026.***
