**Ibadan, Nigeria—October 14, 2026**—The Oyo State Police Command has rescued five Congolese nationals believed to be victims of human trafficking in an intelligence-led operation at Bollarz Hotel in the Araromi area of Ibadan.

Acting on credible information, the Commissioner of Police directed operatives of the Violent Crime Response Unit (VCRU) to move in swiftly. The team successfully freed the five individuals from the hotel.

**The Rescued Victims**

Those rescued are:

\- Bonsaly Mpika Donel, male, 24

\- Charles Siloe, male, 23

\- Eliane Ruth Makengo, female, 26

\- Makota Janie Darcel, female, 23

\- Simba Babeki Elie, male, 33



All five are nationals of the Republic of Congo.

During preliminary questioning, the victims told police that a man identified only as Smith, a national of the Republic of Benin, had lured them into Nigeria with promises of greener pastures and better economic opportunities. 

The suspect was not found at the hotel during the raid, and efforts are ongoing to track him down.



**Next Steps and Medical Care**

Following their rescue, the victims received medical attention and underwent examinations. The Commissioner of Police has directed that they be handed over to the Nigeria Immigration Service, Oyo State Command, for further processing in line with established procedures.

The Command reaffirmed its commitment to fighting human trafficking and protecting vulnerable persons. It urged members of the public to provide credible information that could help identify and apprehend those linked to the suspected trafficking operation.

Residents can reach the police through the Oyo State Call Response Centre toll-free line 615 or the Command Control Room on 08081768614 and 07055495413.
