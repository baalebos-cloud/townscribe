**Ado-Ekiti, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore on Sunday released a video that he says clearly shows the faces of individuals who participated in the brutal armed attack on him and members of his team in Ado-Ekiti on October 30, 2026.

In a detailed post on X, Sowore described the footage as a “*major breakthrough*” in the investigation into the assault that took place at Tawakalitu Restaurant in the Dalimore area of the Ekiti State capital shortly after he granted an interview at MIDAS FM.



**Allegations of Cover-Up**

Sowore claimed that CCTV recordings from the restaurant had been completely erased after police officers and operatives of the Department of State Services (DSS) took possession of the equipment, reportedly transported it to the Government House, and later returned it without any images.

He said he contacted Ekiti State Commissioner of Police Michael Falade, the Divisional Police Officer of Ado-Ekiti Central Police Station, Mustapha Mohammed, and the Rapid Response Squad commander for updates. 

According to Sowore, the officers claimed there was “no complainant” because he had not submitted a written statement, even though police had rescued victims, taken them to hospital, arrested suspects, and recovered weapons, phones and other items.

Sowore further alleged that one of the phones stolen during the attack had been electronically traced to the Governor’s Lodge. 

He said restaurant management confirmed that a DPO returned the CCTV equipment with the recordings wiped but refused to name the officer involved. 

He described the developments as evidence of a *“coordinated cover-up”* by the police hierarchy in the state.



**Police Response and Arrests**

The Ekiti State Police Command had earlier announced the arrest of 11 suspects in connection with the attack. 

The suspects, arrested by the Rapid Response Squad at hideouts in the Atikankan area on the night of October 30, were identified as Joseph Tayo, Olatunji Abayomi, Isaac Amba, Kehinde Ajigbana, Tosin David, Solomon Iyanu, Ogunsola Ayomide, Ojo Ayodeji, Kayode Dada, Segun Olatunji and Salami Emmanuel.

Items recovered included two walkie-talkies, 13 mobile phones, eight international passports, substances suspected to be Indian hemp, and other materials. Police said investigations were ongoing.

Senior Advocate of Nigeria Femi Falana has called on the police to arrest and arraign the “well-known” armed thugs involved without further delay.

Sowore has demanded an independent investigation into the identities and roles of those captured in the new video and called for the removal of the Commissioner of Police and other officers he accused of involvement in the alleged cover-up.

The Ekiti State Government has previously denied any involvement in the incident.
