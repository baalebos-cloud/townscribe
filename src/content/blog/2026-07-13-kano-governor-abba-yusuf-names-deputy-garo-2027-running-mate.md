**ABUJA/KANO**— In a decisive political move that signals early preparations for the 2027 governorship election, Kano State Governor Alhaji Abba Kabir Yusuf has officially selected his Deputy Governor, Murtala Sule Galadima Garo, as his running mate for the upcoming polls.

The announcement was made on Monday during a strategic meeting with Federal Political Appointees serving under President Bola Ahmed Tinubu at the Kano Governor’s Lodge in Abuja. 

Governor Yusuf’s spokesman, Sunusi Bature Dawakin Tofa, confirmed the development in a statement shortly after the meeting.

The decision, according to the Governor, stems from Garo’s demonstrated loyalty, dedication, and tangible contributions to the administration’s development agenda since assuming office. 

Yusuf expressed full confidence in his deputy, stating: 

*“I have no regrets for choosing Garo as my Deputy... he has proven to be the right choice so far. I want to confirm to you that I have every confidence in him.”*

The Governor passed a strong vote of confidence on Garo, praising his competence, commitment, and sense of responsibility. 

He urged party leaders, appointees, and supporters to remain united as the administration focuses on consolidating achievements while gearing up for the elections.

This announcement comes amid shifting political dynamics in Kano, one of Nigeria’s most populous and politically influential states. 

Murtala Sule Galadima Garo, who previously served in other capacities and was once an APC governorship running mate in 2023, brings experience and continuity to the ticket. 

His selection reinforces a message of stability and performance within the current administration.

Governor Abba Yusuf, who rose to power under the New Nigeria Peoples Party (NNPP) banner, has been navigating complex alliances, including interactions with the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) at the federal level. 

The choice of Garo as running mate is widely seen as a bid to project unity, reward loyalty, and build momentum early for 2027.

A compelling image circulating alongside the news shows Governor Yusuf and Deputy Garo in traditional attire, deeply engaged in discussion — a visual symbol of their close working relationship.

As Kano’s political landscape heats up, this early declaration is likely to shape conversations around performance, loyalty, and governance deliverables. 

Supporters view it as a strong continuity ticket focused on sustained development, while critics and opposition figures will undoubtedly intensify scrutiny of the administration’s record.

For now, Governor Yusuf’s bold endorsement of his deputy sends a clear message: the partnership that has defined this administration so far will remain the cornerstone of its re-election bid.

The 2027 race in Kano promises to be intense, with implications that could ripple across northern Nigeria’s political ecosystem.
