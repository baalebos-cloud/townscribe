**Abuja, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—Phrank Shaibu, Director of Strategic Communication of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) Presidential Campaign Council, has dismissed the idea that a second term can be secured through spiritual claims, reacting to President Bola Tinubu’s recent Umrah pilgrimage to Saudi Arabia.

Speaking on Channels Television’s **Politics Today** on Monday, Shaibu said visiting the holy land should remain a personal matter and accused supporters of the President of politicizing the trip.

*“Nigerians will remember Tinubu’s prayer in Mecca the same way they remember the high prices of food, goods, and services,”* he stated.



**Criticism of the Pilgrimage**

Shaibu argued that no second term can be given to anybody via a spiritual claim. He questioned the sincerity of the President’s faith, noting that Tinubu’s last Umrah was about three years ago and linking the timing of the current visit to political considerations ahead of the 2027 elections.

He further criticized the inclusion of northern governors in the entourage while suggesting the cabinet remained dominated by officials from the South-West, describing the approach as hypocritical.

The ADC spokesperson maintained that Atiku Abubakar remains strong in the northern states and that governance performance, not religious symbolism, will determine the outcome of the next election.



**Tinubu’s Position**

President Tinubu performed Umrah in Makkah, where he was granted access to pray inside the Holy Kaaba. He said he prayed for wisdom and resilience to continue serving Nigerians and recalled undertaking a similar spiritual exercise before the 2023 election.

The Presidency has described the trip as a period of spiritual reflection and prayers for Nigeria’s peace, unity and progress.

The exchange forms part of the intensifying political rhetoric as the 2027 general elections approach.



***This report is based on statements made by Phrank Shaibu on Channels Television’s Politics Today and related official comments on November 2, 2026.***
