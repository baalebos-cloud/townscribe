**Abuja, Nigeria—October 13, 2026**—The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (NCDC) has called on citizens to prioritize awareness and informed decision-making in the ongoing effort to prevent Ebola Virus Disease.

In a public advisory released on its official X account, the agency emphasized that preparedness begins with staying informed and making safe choices that protect communities.

*“Preparedness starts with awareness. Protecting our communities begins with staying informed and making safe choices.* 

*Ebola prevention is a shared responsibility. Kindly adhere to guidelines issued by local and international authorities. Every informed decision contributes to stronger public health preparedness,”* the NCDC stated.



**Shared Responsibility for Health Security**

The message underscores that preventing the spread of Ebola is not solely the duty of health authorities. Citizens are urged to follow guidance from both Nigerian institutions and international partners to reduce risks.

The advisory comes at a time when public health agencies across the region continue to monitor developments related to Ebola and reinforce surveillance systems. 

The NCDC has consistently highlighted the importance of early reporting, proper hygiene, and avoiding contact with potentially infected persons or materials.



**Building Stronger Preparedness**

Officials stress that every individual action—from practicing good hand hygiene to seeking verified information—strengthens the country’s overall ability to detect and respond to potential threats.

Nigerians are encouraged to rely on official sources for accurate updates and to report any suspected cases promptly through the NCDC’s toll-free line, 6232.

The agency reaffirmed its commitment to safeguarding public health through continuous risk communication, surveillance, and community engagement.



***This report is based on the official advisory issued by the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention on October 13, 2026.***
