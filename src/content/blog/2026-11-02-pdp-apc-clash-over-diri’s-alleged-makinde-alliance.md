**Yenagoa, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—The Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) and the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Bayelsa State have traded sharp words over allegations that Governor Douye Diri is secretly supporting the presidential ambition of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde instead of President Bola Tinubu’s re-election bid.

Makinde is the presidential candidate of the Allied Peoples Movement (APM).



**PDP’s Claims**

PDP State Chairman George Turnah first raised the allegation. Publicity Secretary Ikaebimo Mark later reinforced it, stating that Diri’s government pays monthly stipends to thousands of former PDP officials from ward to state levels who belong to the Kabiru Tanimu Turaki group.

Mark said these individuals are openly allied with the APM and are mobilizing support for Makinde’s presidential campaign. 

He insisted that neither Diri nor the APC leadership in Bayelsa is backing Tinubu’s 2027 bid and accused the governor of engaging in anti-party activities against the interests of the President and the ruling party.

The PDP also demanded accountability from Diri’s government on public spending and the state of the Bayelsa economy. 

It urged voters to carefully assess candidates ahead of the 2027 elections and reject what it described as incompetent APC candidates for National and State Assembly seats.



**APC’s Response**

APC State Chairman Hon. Warman Weri Ogoriba dismissed the allegations as *“laughable and untrue.*” 

He said Turnah, whom he described as chairman of a factional PDP, “needs his head examined” for making what he called false claims.

The exchange highlights deepening political tensions in Bayelsa as parties position themselves for the 2027 general elections, with questions of loyalty and cross-party alliances already dominating the discourse.



***This report is based on statements from PDP and APC leaders in Bayelsa State as reported on November 2, 2026.***
