**Abuja, Nigeria—October 30, 2026**—The Federal Government has commenced a monthly cash relief programme offering ₦50,000 to eligible vulnerable households to help cushion the impact of rising living costs.



Minister of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction, Dr Bernard Doro, announced the National Emergency Relief and Stabilization Intervention (NERSI) in a statement on Friday. 

He described it as a presidential initiative designed to provide immediate financial assistance to millions of vulnerable Nigerians.



**Response to Economic Pressures**

Doro said the programme was introduced in response to global food inflation and the high cost of essential goods, which continue to place pressure on households. It aligns with President Bola Tinubu’s Renewed Hope Agenda.

*“Under this intervention, eligible beneficiaries will receive ₦50,000 monthly, providing direct assistance to help meet essential needs and ease the burden of living costs,”* the minister stated.

He added that the intervention would be reviewed regularly in line with changes in the cost of living, with beneficiaries notified directly before any adjustments.



**How the Payments Work**

The ministry is partnering with licensed digital financial service providers to ensure secure and transparent delivery of the funds. 

Beneficiaries will receive text messages from the providers requesting their consent before payments are credited directly into their bank accounts or mobile wallets.

Importantly, no application, fee, or registration through agents is required. Selection is based on existing government records, including the National Social Register.



**Strong Warning Against Fraud**

Doro cautioned Nigerians against fraudsters. He advised beneficiaries not to disclose their Personal Identification Numbers (PINs) or Bank Verification Numbers (BVNs) to anyone.

*“If you are unsure about any message, or anyone asks you for money, call the official NERSI helpline on 0700-022-2222,”* he said.

The minister reaffirmed the ministry’s commitment to delivering the programme with dignity, transparency, and accountability, noting that it complements existing social protection and economic support initiatives.



The scheme is being rolled out in phases across the country.
