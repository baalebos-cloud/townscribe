**Abuja, Nigeria – July 21, 2026** — In a move signaling continued focus on governance efficiency and sector-specific expertise, President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has approved 26 new appointments across critical federal agencies and commissions. 

The appointments, which take immediate effect, span energy access, fiscal responsibility, population management, atomic energy, agriculture, housing, and education, among others.

Veteran journalist and former Ekiti State Governor Ayodele Fayose emerges as a notable pick, appointed Chairman of the Board of the Rural Electrification Agency (REA). 

This cross-party selection underscores Tinubu’s pragmatic approach to leveraging experienced politicians for national development priorities, particularly expanding electricity access to rural communities.



 **Key Appointments at a Glance**



Rural Electrification Agency (REA): 

\- Ayo Fayose – Chairman, Board  

\- Alhaji Ahmadu Abubakar – Member/Non-Executive Director  

\- Engr. Ilyasu Ibrahim Makinta – Member/Non-Executive Director



National Salary and Wages Commission:  

\- Maj. Gen. Junaid Bindawa – Chairman  

\- Olajumoke Okoya-Thomas – Secretary  

\- Commissioners and members from Benue, Oyo, Kaduna, Borno, Enugu, Edo, and Kogi states.



Revenue Mobilisation Allocation and Fiscal Commission (RMAFC): 

\- Tosin Johnson Adeyanju – Secretary



National Population Commission (NPC):  

\- Dr. Abuh Mohammed – Director-General



Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading (NBET):

\- Dr. Akinola Odeyemi – Managing Director



Nigeria Atomic Energy Commission:  

\- Dr. Anthony Inalegwu Godwin – Chairman/Chief Executive Officer



National Centre for Agricultural Mechanisation (NCAM):  

\- Engr. Julius Oloro – Chief Executive Officer



Fiscal Responsibility Commission (FRC):  

\- Dr. Abdullahi Maikano Saidu – Chairman  

\- Multiple board members including Mohammed Asmau, Mohammed Aliyu Makama, and others.



National Commission for Mass Literacy, Adult and Non-Formal Education:  

\- Shuni Muhammad Dahiru – Executive Secretary



Federal Housing Authority (FHA):  

\- Gisaor Vincent Iorja – Executive Director (Finance)



These appointments reflect a deliberate effort to infuse fresh leadership while drawing on diverse regional and professional expertise. 

Many appointees bring backgrounds in engineering, administration, academia, and public service, aligning with the administration’s goals in infrastructure, fiscal prudence, human capital development, and sustainable energy.

The list, widely circulated on social media by accounts like @MSIngawa (a prominent voice on jobs and Nigerian affairs), comes as the Tinubu administration navigates economic reforms, power sector challenges, and preparations for future electoral cycles. 

Fayose’s inclusion, despite his PDP roots and vocal past criticisms, highlights themes of collaboration and competence over strict partisanship—echoing his recent public endorsements of aspects of Tinubu’s leadership and predictions of strong performance in 2027.

Observers note that strengthening agencies like REA is vital for addressing Nigeria’s persistent electricity deficits, while roles in salaries, revenue, and population data support broader policy implementation. 

The Fiscal Responsibility Commission appointments, in particular, signal renewed emphasis on accountability in public spending.

Critics and supporters alike will watch closely for impact: Will these leaders deliver tangible results in rural electrification, fair wage policies, and efficient resource allocation? 

As one social media reaction put it, such moves test the balance between *“reform and appointments.”*

President Tinubu’s directive for immediate effect positions these individuals to hit the ground running, contributing to the Renewed Hope Agenda’s focus on inclusive growth and institutional strengthening.
