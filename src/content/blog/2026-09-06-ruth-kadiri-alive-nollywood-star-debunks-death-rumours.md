**Lagos, Nigeria—September 6, 2026—Nollywood** actress, screenwriter, and filmmaker Ruth Kadiri has firmly dismissed circulating reports of her death, assuring fans she is alive, healthy, and safe.

In a video shared on her Instagram page on Sunday, the mother of two addressed the false claims head-on after waking up to find multiple accounts spreading the rumour.

*“Please note that I am alive. I did not have any accident, and I am safe,”* Kadiri said. She revealed that about seven to eight accounts had been created specifically to push the false narrative.

Visibly frustrated, the actress pleaded with her followers and the general public to report the pages rather than engage with or try to debunk the posts.

*“I AM ALIVE!!! Please help me report. Please, I’m begging you, help me report it*,” she said. “*I’m really sad at the extent some people will go on this social media space to hurt and wish death on another person. I stay on my lane. Leave me alone. I beg you all. It’s really becoming tiring and sad.”*



**A Growing Pattern of Celebrity Death Hoaxes**

The incident highlights a recurring problem in Nigerian social media, where unverified death reports about public figures frequently go viral, causing unnecessary distress to families and fans. 

Similar false claims have targeted other entertainers in recent months.

Kadiri, known for her consistent digital content and independent productions, continues to focus on her work despite the distraction. 

She recently released Episode 5 of her popular series \*Once Upon a Village\* on her YouTube channel, RuthKadiri247, featuring actors including Henrietta Ibekwe and Deza The Great.



**Background on Ruth Kadiri**

Born on March 24, 1988, in Benin City, Edo State, Ruth Kadiri has built a solid career as an actress, writer and producer since entering Nollywood in the mid-2000s. 

She is recognised for creating original stories and connecting directly with audiences through her digital platforms. 

Married with two children, she has largely kept her private life away from constant public scrutiny while remaining active in the industry.

Fans and colleagues have responded positively to her clarification, with many expressing relief and condemning the spread of harmful false information.

As of Sunday evening, the actress continues to urge her audience to help stop the rumour by simply reporting the offending pages.
