**Abuja, August 19, 2026** — The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) on Wednesday publicly declared Oluwatosin Osasona Adekunle wanted in connection with an alleged case of conspiracy, obtaining money by false pretence, and fraudulent diversion of funds.

According to the official wanted notice released by the Commission and signed by its Head of Media and Publicity, Dele Oyewale, the 32-year-old man from Ekiti State is associated with Oasis Vivacity Global Limited. 

A clear photograph of Adekunle was published alongside the notice to help members of the public identify him.

The EFCC stated that Adekunle’s last known address is House No. 3, Millennium Estate, Alhaji Gbenga Ashafa Street, Gbagada, Lagos State. 

Anyone with useful information about his current whereabouts is strongly urged to contact the Commission without delay.

The public can reach the EFCC through any of its offices in Ibadan, Uyo, Sokoto, Maiduguri, Benin, Makurdi, Kaduna, Ilorin, Enugu, Kano, Lagos, Gombe, Port Harcourt or Abuja. 

Alternative channels include the dedicated telephone line 08093322644, the email address info@efcc.gov.ng, or the nearest police station and other security agencies.

While the notice did not disclose the specific amount involved or the identity of the petitioners, the nature of the alleged offences underscores the Commission’s continued determination to track down individuals accused of economic and financial crimes.

The declaration forms part of the EFCC’s wider efforts to combat fraud and recover proceeds of crime. Citizens are reminded that timely and accurate information can play a critical role in bringing wanted suspects to justice.

As of the time of filing this report, there was no indication that Adekunle had been arrested or had presented himself to the authorities. Further developments are expected as the search continues.
