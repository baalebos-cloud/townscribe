**Madrid, Spain – July 24, 2026**—Real Madrid’s aggressive summer spending ambitions have hit a roadblock in their chase for RB Leipzig’s teenage star **Yan Diomande,** after the German club swiftly rejected a substantial €100 million proposal.

According to reports from BILD and The Athletic, Real Madrid tabled an offer of €90 million plus €10 million in add-ons, which Leipzig turned down. 

The Bundesliga side is holding out for around €130 million, with some indications they may seek closer to the €150 million mark that Real Madrid president Florentino Pérez had publicly referenced in campaign promises.

The 19-year-old Ivorian winger has emerged as one of the most coveted young talents in world football following a breakout debut season at Leipzig. 

Diomande, who joined the club from Leganés in 2025, impressed with double-digit goals and assists, helping secure a strong Bundesliga finish and Champions League qualification. 

His pace, skill, and versatility on either wing have drawn comparisons to elite attackers.

This latest bid from Real Madrid is not identical to a previous rejected offer from Liverpool (€80m + €20m add-ons). Multiple top clubs — including Arsenal, Manchester City, Liverpool, and Paris Saint-Germain — are circling, turning Diomande’s future into one of the summer’s hottest transfer dramas.

Diomande has been linked with a preference for certain destinations, with earlier reports suggesting interest in PSG or Real Madrid, though negotiations remain fluid. 

Leipzig appears keen to retain the player for at least another season unless their substantial valuation is met.

Leipzig has cleverly referenced Pérez’s pre-election pledge for a major €150 million signing, using it as leverage in talks. Real Madrid, under manager José Mourinho, continues to seek reinforcements on the right flank to bolster their attacking options.

At just 19 years old, Diomande — born in Abidjan, Ivory Coast, and developed partly through academies in the United States — represents a long-term investment with immediate superstar potential. He has already earned senior caps for the Ivory Coast national team.

Talks are ongoing, but Leipzig’s firm stance suggests any deal would shatter records for a player of his age and position. Real Madrid fans are eagerly watching whether the club will escalate their offer or pivot to other targets as the transfer window progresses.

For now, the young sensation remains a Leipzig player, but the intense interest from Europe’s elite clubs guarantees this story is far from over.
