**Abuja, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has concluded his visit to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, where he and members of his delegation had the rare honor of entering the Holy Kaaba to pray and perform Umrah.

In a statement shared on his official X handle on Monday, the President expressed deep gratitude to the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, King Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud, and Crown Prince and Prime Minister Mohammed bin Salman for extending the privilege and for their gracious hospitality and brotherly care.

*“It reflects the enduring bonds of faith and friendship between Nigeria and Saudi Arabia,”* Tinubu said.



**Prayers for the Nation**

The President noted that in those sacred moments inside the Kaaba, he prayed for peace, unity and prosperity for Nigeria and for all Nigerians.

The visit underscores the longstanding diplomatic and religious ties between the two countries. Saudi Arabia remains a key partner for Nigeria, particularly in the areas of faith, pilgrimage facilitation, and bilateral cooperation.

Photos accompanying the statement showed the President and his delegation during the spiritual exercises in the holy sites.

Tinubu signed the message as President of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, tagging relevant Saudi government accounts.



***This report is based on the official statement released by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu on November 2, 2026.***
