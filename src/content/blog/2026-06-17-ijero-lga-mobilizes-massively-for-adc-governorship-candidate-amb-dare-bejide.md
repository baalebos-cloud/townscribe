**Ijero-Ekiti—In** a vibrant display of commitment and grassroots strength, the Ijero Local Government Area (LGA) of Ekiti State has taken the lead in sensitizing and mobilizing voters across its 12 wards and 145 polling units ahead of Saturday’s off-cycle Governorship election.

The mobilization exercise, which kicked off at the Local Government Secretariat in Ijero-Ekiti, drew a large turnout of enthusiastic party members and supporters. 

In high spirits, the team moved through the communities with a clear message: the African Democratic Congress (ADC) is here to stay, and a vote for its governorship candidate, Ambassador Dare Bejide, represents a vote for a better, more prosperous Ekiti State.

Party leaders described Ambassador Bejide as the credible alternative capable of delivering good governance, meaningful development, and responsive leadership to the people of Ekiti. 

The mobilization drive aimed not only to encourage voter turnout but also to reinforce confidence in the ADC’s vision for the state.

Present at the flag-off were top party executives, including State, Local Government, and Ward officials of the ADC. 

Their unified presence underscored the party’s organizational readiness and determination to deliver victory at the polls.

Residents of Ijero LGA received the mobilization team warmly, with many expressing optimism about the forthcoming election. 

Several community members gave strong assurances of support, promising to deliver their wards and polling units for Ambassador Bejide and the ADC.

Speaking during the exercise, party leaders expressed confidence in the positive feedback received from the people, describing it as a strong indication of impending victory on Saturday.

The Ijero LGA mobilization is part of broader efforts by the ADC across Ekiti State to ensure that voters are fully sensitized, energized, and ready to elect a government that truly prioritizes the welfare and progress of the people.

As the election draws near, the ADC remains focused on issue-based campaigns, promising accountable leadership and sustainable development under the stewardship of Ambassador Dare Bejide.
