**Lagos, Nigeria—November 2, 2026**—Justice Dipeolu of the Federal High Court sitting in Ikoyi, Lagos, on October 30, 2026, convicted and sentenced 18-year-old Hassan Usman Olamilekan for money laundering linked to a gift card fraud scheme.

The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) Lagos Zonal Directorate 1 arraigned Olamilekan on a one-count charge of money laundering, contrary to Section 18(2)(a) and punishable under Section 18(3) of the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act, 2022.

The charge stated that he indirectly disguised the origin of $800 in gift cards, which formed part of the proceeds of his unlawful activity.



**Guilty Plea and Sentence**

Olamilekan pleaded guilty to the charge. After an EFCC operative, Ebuka Abanga, reviewed the facts of the case, the court found him guilty and convicted him.

Justice Dipeolu sentenced him to a fine of N200,000 and ordered him to remit N1,000,000 to the Federal Government of Nigeria. 

He is to remain in the Correctional Centre pending payment of the fine and restitution. The court also ordered the forfeiture of an iPhone 11 Pro and a Poco C71 mobile phone recovered in connection with the offence.



**How the Scam Worked**

Investigations revealed that Olamilekan, who claimed to hail from the Republic of Benin but was born in Nigeria, was involved in lottery scams and operated accounts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Zangi and iMessage.

He met his victim, Gary Brewer, a United States citizen, on the Zangi application and impersonated a white American woman named Julie Leach. 

Olamilekan told the victim he had won a $250,000,000 lottery prize and needed money to activate the winnings. He persuaded Brewer to send gift cards through the app.

The defendant admitted selling the gift cards via the Cardsonic application, converting them to naira and transferring the proceeds to his grandmother’s OPay account. He confessed to benefiting from a total of $800 from the scheme.

Olamilekan was among 21 suspected internet fraudsters arrested by EFCC operatives during a sting operation at Applewood Estate, Orchid Road, Lekki, Lagos State, on October 26, 2026.
