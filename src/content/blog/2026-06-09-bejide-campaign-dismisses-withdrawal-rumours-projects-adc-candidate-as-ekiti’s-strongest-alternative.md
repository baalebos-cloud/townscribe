*The campaign organization of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) governorship candidate in Ekiti State, Oluwadare Bejide, has dismissed reports suggesting that the candidate has stepped down from the race, describing such claims as false and politically motivated.

In a statement released by the campaign's media team, supporters of Ambassador Bejide insisted that the ADC flagbearer remains fully committed to contesting the forthcoming governorship election and continues to enjoy increasing support across the state.

The campaign argued that Bejide has emerged as a credible alternative for voters seeking a departure from the political uncertainties surrounding the state's major parties.

According to the statement, the ADC candidate has remained focused on engaging with communities and presenting his development agenda rather than becoming entangled in political controversies.

Campaign officials highlighted what they described as Bejide's longstanding commitment to public service, youth empowerment, educational advancement, healthcare improvement, and economic development. They maintained that his message continues to resonate with residents who are eager for practical solutions to the state's challenges.

The organization further claimed that recent rumours alleging that Bejide had endorsed or stepped down for another candidate were part of efforts by political opponents to undermine the growing momentum of his campaign. It stressed that the ADC candidate remains firmly in the race and is actively mobilising supporters ahead of the election.

The statement also drew attention to ongoing disputes and legal battles involving some rival political camps, arguing that such developments have created uncertainty within the state's political landscape. In contrast, the campaign portrayed Bejide as a candidate offering stability and a clear vision for governance.
"As others focus on internal political struggles, Ambassador Bejide remains committed to discussing issues that directly affect the people of Ekiti," the statement said.

The campaign concluded with an appeal to voters to rally behind the ADC candidate, expressing confidence that Bejide represents the best opportunity for what it described as meaningful transformation and sustainable development in the state.

The forthcoming governorship election is expected to attract keen attention as political parties intensify campaigns and seek to convince voters of their plans for the future of Ekiti State.

Source: Ambassador Dare Bejide Campaign Organization Media Team.*
