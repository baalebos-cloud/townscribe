**ANKARA, Turkey** — In a powerful display of unity, NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte announced that leaders from 32 Allied nations, alongside key partners from Ukraine, the European Union, the Indo-Pacific region, and the Gulf, are gathering in Ankara over the next two days for a critical summit. 

The meeting aims to ensure NATO continues delivering strong security outcomes in an increasingly complex global landscape.

Rutte highlighted that Allies have already demonstrated significant commitment and stepped up contributions. “*We are stronger together, in NATO, with our partners, to ensure the freedom and security we all hold dear,*” he emphasized in a video message.

**A Beacon of Solidarity Amid Global Challenges**

This summit in Ankara comes at a pivotal moment. With ongoing support for Ukraine, rising security concerns, and the need for enhanced cooperation with like-minded partners, the gathering underscores NATO’s role as a cornerstone of transatlantic and global stability. 

The inclusion of diverse partners signals a broader, more inclusive approach to addressing common threats while promoting peace and prosperity. 

The event is expected to produce concrete outcomes on defense spending, capability development, and strategic partnerships — reinforcing the Alliance’s readiness and resilience.

**Positive Momentum for International Cooperation**

For citizens across member nations and partner countries, the summit offers reassurance that multilateral institutions remain committed to collective defense and democratic values. 

As Rutte noted, the progress already made shows what is possible when nations stand united.

This Ankara gathering not only strengthens military and political ties but also sends a clear message of solidarity to the world: NATO and its partners are more determined than ever to protect shared freedoms and adapt to future challenges together.

In an era often marked by division, this high-level convergence in Turkey represents welcome good news — a testament to enduring alliances working proactively for a safer, more secure future.
