**Abuja, Nigeria—October 6, 2026**—Nigeria's Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) is today holding a high-profile World Press Conference and National Stakeholders’ Meeting to mark the official start of the 100-day countdown to the 2027 general elections.

The event, scheduled for 10 a.m. at the Bola Ahmed Tinubu International Conference Centre in Abuja, will be led by INEC Chairman Professor Joash Amupitan, SAN. 

It is expected to provide a comprehensive update on the Commission’s state of readiness as the country moves into the critical final phase of election preparations.



**What the Briefing Will Cover**

According to a statement by Adedayo Oketola, Chief Press Secretary to the INEC Chairman, the Commission will brief stakeholders on key areas, including:



\- The status of the national voters’ register  

\- Candidate nominations and political party compliance  

\- Election logistics and material distribution  

\- Critical measures designed to protect the integrity of the electoral process  



INEC described the meeting as a demonstration of its “c*ommitment to absolute transparency, proactive public communication, and collaborative engagement with key actors in the electoral ecosystem.”*



**Who Will Be in the Room**

The gathering is expected to bring together a wide cross-section of the electoral community. Invited participants include political party leaders, security agencies under the Inter-Agency Consultative Committee on Election Security (ICCES), members of the diplomatic corps, development partners, civil society organizations, election observer groups, disability rights advocates, religious and traditional leaders, youth organizations, and media executives.

Attendance is strictly by invitation, and the proceedings will be broadcast live on major national television networks and streamed on INEC’s official platforms.



**The Road to 2027**

Under INEC’s revised timetable, the presidential and National Assembly elections are scheduled for **16 January 2027**, while the governorship and State Houses of Assembly elections will follow on **6 February 2027.**

The Commission has already completed several major milestones, including the publication of the final list of presidential candidates and the opening of campaigns. 

A nationwide collection of Permanent Voter Cards for newly registered voters is also set to begin later this week.



**Why This Moment Matters**

With just over three months remaining before Nigerians head to the polls, today’s briefing carries significant weight. 

It offers the public, political parties, and international partners a clearer picture of how prepared INEC is to deliver credible elections, while also providing a platform for stakeholders to raise concerns and seek clarifications.

As the 100-day clock begins ticking, attention will now turn to whether the Commission can translate its stated commitments into seamless logistics, secure voting environments, and public confidence on election day.
