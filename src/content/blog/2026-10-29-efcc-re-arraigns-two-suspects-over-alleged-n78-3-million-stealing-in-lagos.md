**Lagos, Nigeria—October 29, 2026**—The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) on Wednesday re-arraigned two suspects, Blessing Onyinaya Chimaobi and Uzofor Benson Chinedu, before a Lagos Special Offences Court over an alleged N78.3 million stealing case.

The Lagos Zonal Directorate 1 of the anti-graft agency brought the defendants before Justice Olubunmi Abike-Fadipe of the Special Offences Court sitting in Ikeja.



**Amended Charges and Not-Guilty Plea**

The duo were first arraigned on February 7, 2024, on a three-count charge of conspiracy and stealing. The prosecution later amended the charges to a more detailed 20-count information.

The fresh charges border on conspiracy contrary to Section 409 of the Criminal Law of Lagos State, 2011; stealing contrary to Section 285 of the same law; and retention of proceeds of criminal conduct under Sections 17(a) and (b) of the EFCC Establishment Act, 2004.

One of the counts alleges that the defendants, along with one Olisa Charles (still at large), sometimes in 2023 in Lagos, conspired to defraud Christopher Onuigbo of the sum of N78,347,100. 

Another count accuses them of dishonestly converting the same amount belonging to Onuigbo for their own use between 2018 and 2023.

Both defendants pleaded not guilty to the amended charges when they were read to them in court.



**Court Proceedings and Adjournment**

Prosecution counsel T.J. Banjo urged the court to revoke the defendants’ bail and remand them in a correctional facility. He also requested dates for the continuation of the trial.

Defence counsel Peter Taiwo did not oppose the amendment of the charges but asked for adequate time to study the expanded 20-count information. 

He noted that the number of counts had risen from three to 20 and indicated that the defence might seek to recall earlier prosecution witnesses if necessary.

Justice Fadipe took the defendants’ pleas and adjourned the matter to February 10, 11, 16 and 17, 2027, for the continuation of the trial.

The case remains one of several ongoing prosecutions by the EFCC aimed at recovering funds allegedly obtained through fraud and ensuring accountability for financial crimes in Lagos.
