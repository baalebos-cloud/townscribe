**London, UK** -In one of the most explosive transfers of the 2026 summer window, Chelsea have secured a verbal agreement with Aston Villa for the signing of dynamic attacking midfielder Morgan Rogers in a staggering £117 million deal. 

David Ornstein of The Athletic broke the exclusive story, confirming the bid has been accepted and the move is rapidly progressing toward completion.

Personal terms are locked in on a six-year contract until 2032, with an option for a further year. Rogers, currently representing England at the 2026 FIFA World Cup in North America, is slated for a medical on Monday. 

The fee eclipses Chelsea’s previous club record for Enzo Fernández and narrowly surpasses Manchester City’s recent £116m British record for Elliot Anderson.

At 23, the 6ft 2in (1.87m) Halesowen native has scripted a fairytale rise. Starting at West Bromwich Albion, he moved to Manchester City’s academy in 2019 without a senior appearance there. 

Loan spells at Lincoln City (impressive goal tally), Bournemouth, Blackpool, and Middlesbrough showcased his potential before Villa pounced in February 2024 for around £8m plus add-ons.

Under Unai Emery, Rogers transformed into a Premier League force. The 2025-26 campaign delivered 14 goals and 12 assists in 55 appearances across competitions, helping Villa clinch Champions League qualification and Europa League glory. 

Overall at Villa, he boasts roughly 31 goals and 29 assists in 125 games—a versatile threat capable of operating centrally, on either flank, or as a creative hub with elite ball-carrying ability.

His England breakthrough came in November 2024. At the World Cup, he has started key matches, including against Panama, and delivered a crucial assist in the semi-final versus Argentina. 

Teammates and coaches praise his maturity, work rate, and leadership despite his youth.

Rogers chose Chelsea over suitors including Arsenal, drawn by the club’s exciting rebuild under new manager Xabi Alonso. 

Long-term admirers at Stamford Bridge — bolstered by recruitment links from his City academy days and friendships like Cole Palmer — saw him as the ideal versatile attacker to energize their attack.

For Alonso, Rogers fits seamlessly into a youthful, high-pressing system. The deal signals Chelsea’s ambition to reclaim their place among Europe’s elite after recent challenges. 

It reunites the player with familiar faces and offers a platform for sustained success.

Aston Villa reluctantly part ways with a fan favourite but gain vital funds. Middlesbrough retain a 20% sell-on. 

The club is already reinvesting, having finalised the arrival of promising midfielder Johan Manzambi from Freiburg and targeting wing reinforcements like Ibrahim Mbaye or Crysencio Summerville. 

Emery’s project remains ambitious despite the loss.

Rogers had signed improved terms until 2030 in late 2025, but the scale of Chelsea’s offer and sporting project proved compelling.

This move intensifies scrutiny on Premier League finances and valuations for homegrown talents. Arsenal, who had tracked Rogers closely as a priority target, must now accelerate alternatives to strengthen their attack.

For the Three Lions, Rogers’ club future adds intrigue as England navigate the World Cup knockout stages. 

His departure highlights the Premier League’s talent drain toward ambitious projects while rewarding clubs that develop stars like Villa did.

Morgan Rogers’ story — from overlooked prospect to record-breaking signing — captures football’s enduring magic. 

Chelsea supporters have every right to dream big; Villa fans will cherish memories of a player who helped redefine their club. The football world will be watching closely as this transfer saga concludes.
