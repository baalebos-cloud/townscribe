The Ogun State Police Command has arrested a 35-year-old businessman, Akinola Damilare, popularly known as “***General Don,***” for allegedly impersonating a military officer in Abeokuta, the state capital.

The suspect was paraded on Friday by the Command’s Public Relations Officer, DSP Oluseyi Babaseyi, who confirmed that the arrest followed credible intelligence from a community leader and discreet surveillance by operatives of the Special Weapons and Tactics (SWAT) Unit.

According to Babaseyi, Damilare was arrested at about 2:00 a.m. on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, at Kajola in the Elega area of Abeokuta North Local Government Area. 

Police operatives acted after a community leader raised the alarm over the suspect’s activities in the neighborhood.

Items recovered from him included a military face cap, a pair of three-quarter military trousers, and four photographs showing him dressed in full military uniform. 

The police said these items form part of the ongoing investigation into the extent of his alleged impersonation.

*“The suspect was arrested at Kajola, Elega, Abeokuta North Local Government of the state following credible intelligence from a community leader and discreet surveillance by operatives of the Command’s Special Weapons and Tactics,”* Babaseyi stated. 

He added that investigation is continuing to establish the full circumstances of the case and to determine whether other persons were involved.

The Commissioner of Police in Ogun State, Bode Ojajuni, reaffirmed the Command’s determination to tackle the growing menace of impersonation and other criminal activities across the state. 

He urged residents to remain vigilant and to promptly report any suspicious conduct to the police for timely intervention.

Impersonation of military and security personnel has become a recurring concern in parts of the country, with some suspects using fake uniforms and ranks to intimidate residents, settle personal scores, or commit other offences. 

Police authorities say such acts undermine public trust in genuine security agencies and create fear in communities.

The arrest of “General Don” is part of the Ogun State Police Command’s broader efforts to clamp down on criminal elements who exploit the respect and authority associated with military uniforms. 

Officials noted that the operation was intelligence-driven and executed with minimal disruption to the community.

Damilare, described by police as a businessman, is currently in custody. Detectives are working to establish whether he used the military identity for any specific criminal purpose beyond the act of impersonation itself. 

The Command has assured the public that the case will be thoroughly investigated and that the suspect will be charged to court once investigations are concluded.

Residents of Abeokuta and other parts of Ogun State have been advised to be wary of individuals who parade themselves as security personnel without proper identification. 

The police emphasized that genuine officers always carry official means of identification and operate within the law.

The successful operation has been hailed by community leaders who provided the initial tip-off, with many expressing relief that the suspect was taken off the streets before further incidents could occur. 

The Ogun State Police Command continues to call for greater public cooperation in the fight against crime.
