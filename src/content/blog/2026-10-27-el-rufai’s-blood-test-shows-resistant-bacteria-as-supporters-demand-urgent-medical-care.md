**Abuja, Nigeria—October 27, 2026**—Fresh medical documents circulating on social media claim that former Kaduna State Governor Mallam Nasir El-Rufai’s blood sample has tested positive for \*Enterococcus faecium\*, a bacterium capable of causing serious infections and resistant to vancomycin.

The laboratory report, reportedly dated 16 October 2026, raises concerns about a possible bloodstream infection. 

A subsequent medical recommendation dated 22 October 2026 is said to have advised urgent hospital admission, treatment, and specialist consultation, warning that the antibiotics proposed may not adequately address the infection.

**Supporters Raise Alarm Over Medical Access**

Associates and supporters of the former governor, including accounts linked to the African Democratic Congress (ADC), have expressed strong concern that the recommendations are not being prioritized. 

They argue that detention should not override the fundamental right to appropriate medical care.

One post stated, "Yet, *those holding him appear more concerned about keeping him behind bars than ensuring he receives appropriate medical care. When urgent medical recommendations are not prioritized, questions about deliberate neglect become unavoidable.”*

Another commentary emphasized that even convicted individuals retain the right to treatment and called for independent medical assessment, verification of the reports, and public clarity on how El-Rufai’s medical rights are being protected.

**Context of Detention**

El-Rufai has been facing multiple legal proceedings involving the Independent Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences Commission (ICPC) and the Department of State Services (DSS). 

These relate to allegations arising from his tenure as Kaduna governor and claims of unlawful interception of communications. Courts have previously ordered access to his personal physicians and treatment as required.

Family members and aides have in the past complained about restrictions on medical access, while authorities have disputed some of those claims. The latest documents have intensified calls for transparency and prompt specialist intervention.



**Human Rights Perspective**

Supporters insist that prosecution and the right to medical care are not mutually exclusive. They maintain that El-Rufai should face the allegations against him through due process while receiving any necessary treatment recommended by qualified doctors.

As of the time of filing this report, there has been no official public confirmation or detailed response from the relevant agencies regarding the specific laboratory findings or the 22 October medical recommendation.



***This report is based on documents and statements shared by supporters of Mallam Nasir El-Rufai on 27 October 2026, alongside publicly available information on his ongoing legal matters.***
