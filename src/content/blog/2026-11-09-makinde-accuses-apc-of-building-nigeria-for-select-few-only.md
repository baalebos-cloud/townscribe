**Jos, Plateau State—November 9, 2026**—Oyo State Governor and Allied Peoples Movement (APM) presidential candidate, Engineer Seyi Makinde, on Monday used his campaign rally in Jos to sharply criticize the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), accusing it of governing Nigeria for a select few over the past nearly four years.

In remarks delivered at the rally, Makinde pointed out that the APC is now promising *“a Nigeria that works for everyone.”* 

He said this sudden shift in language only confirms that the Nigeria the party has been building so far was designed for a privileged minority rather than the generality of citizens.

*“In my remarks at our campaign rally in Jos this afternoon, I emphasized that our goal is to build a Nigeria that works for every Nigerian. After nearly four years in power, APC is also now promising ‘a Nigeria that works for everyone,' which confirms that the Nigeria they have been building so far is for a select few*,” Makinde posted on his official X account after the event.



**Call for a National Reset**

Makinde told the crowd that Nigeria urgently needs a complete reset. He presented his Reset Nigeria Agenda, which rests on four main pillars: security, the economy, education, and the socio-political environment. 

He argued that lasting security is the foundation for economic recovery, particularly in Plateau State, which he said has the potential to reclaim its place as a major tourism destination once peace is restored.

*“If Nigeria is not working for you, then let us reset. When we reset Nigeria, we will have a Nigeria that will work for everybody,”* he declared. 

He urged supporters to take the message back to their polling units, stressing that elections are won at the grassroots.

The governor also called on Nigerians to “send the oppressors packing” and bring in a team committed to genuine national renewal. 

His running mate, Lawal Daura, joined him in appealing for Plateau’s support, describing the state as strategically important in the North-Central zone.



**Engagements in Plateau**

Before the rally, Makinde spent two days in Plateau State. He visited the palace of the Gbong Gwom Jos, HRH Da Jacob Gyang Buba, Chairman of the Plateau State Council of Chiefs and Emirs, where he presented the Reset Agenda. 

He also met with Rev. Dr. Job Ayuba Bagat, President of the Evangelical Church Winning All (ECWA), to share his plans for the country.

On Sunday, he attended service at the Church of Christ in Nations (COCIN) Headquarters in Jos, where he encouraged Christians to participate actively in politics and hold leaders accountable. 

He presented the Reset Nigeria Manifesto to church leaders and invited citizens to measure him against the written commitments if elected.

Makinde thanked the people of Plateau for their warm reception and hospitality during the visit.



**Broader Campaign Message**

The Jos rally forms part of the APM’s ongoing nationwide campaign. Makinde has consistently framed his presidential bid as a people-centered alternative focused on transparency, accountability, and inclusive development. 

He has repeatedly told audiences that his ambition is backed by God and ordinary Nigerians rather than political godfathers and that he wants citizens to hold him accountable through the documented promises in his manifesto.

As the 2027 election season intensifies, Makinde’s direct criticism of the APC’s record and his call for a national reset are expected to remain central to the APM’s messaging across the country.



***This report is based on statements by Governor Seyi Makinde, official campaign updates, and multiple independent news accounts of the Jos rally on November 9, 2026.***
